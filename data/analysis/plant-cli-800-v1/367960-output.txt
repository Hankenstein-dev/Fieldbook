**Turkish hazel (*Corylus colurna*)** is a large deciduous tree in the birch family, native to southeastern Europe and southwestern Asia. It can reach about 25–33 m tall and develops a broad, irregular crown with age.

Its edible hazelnuts ripen from September to October and taste similar to common hazelnuts, but their small size and thick shells limit commercial value. The species is widely planted as an ornamental and street tree because it tolerates urban conditions, drought, cold, heat, and alkaline soils. Its non-suckering growth also makes it a valuable rootstock for commercial hazelnut cultivars.
