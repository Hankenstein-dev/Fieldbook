The Gymea lily (*Doryanthes excelsa*) is a striking flowering plant endemic to coastal New South Wales, Australia. It forms a rosette of long, sword-shaped leaves and produces a flower spike up to 6 m tall, crowned with large, bright-red flowers in spring. Itakah.

Aboriginal people traditionally roasted its young stems for food, made cakes from the roasted roots, and used fibres from its leaves to produce brushes and matting. It grows naturally in acidic, low-nutrient soils within open dry forests.
