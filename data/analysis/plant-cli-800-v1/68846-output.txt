Muscari neglectum is a perennial Eurasian bulb in the asparagus family. It produces 5–20 cm stems bearing dense spikes of dark-blue, urn-shaped flowers with white-tipped lobes. Often cultivated as a spring ornamental, it spreads rapidly and can become invasive. Its native range extends from the Mediterranean and Central Europe through Western and Central Asia. It is commonly called common grape hyacinth or starch grape hyacinth.
