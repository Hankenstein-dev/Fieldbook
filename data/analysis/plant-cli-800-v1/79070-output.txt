Setaria viridis (green bristlegrass or green foxtail) is a hardy annual grass native to Eurasia and introduced across most continents. It grows in urban, cultivated, and disturbed habitats, reaching about 1–2 meters tall and producing dense, bristled, spike-like flower clusters. It is the wild ancestor of cultivated foxtail millet and is also used as a model organism for C4 photosynthesis research. (Sources: s1:p1–p4)
