The Madeira foxglove (*Digitalis sceptrum*) is a tender evergreen shrub native to Madeira. It grows up to 1.8 m tall, with toothed oblong or ovate leaves and summer racemes of yellow, orange, or tawny-russet flowers, often patterned with chocolate-brown. It favors wooded habitats, cloud forests, and steep slopes near water, and may take five years to reach full flowering maturity. [s1:p1–p2]
