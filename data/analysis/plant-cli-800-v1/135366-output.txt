Water gum (*Tristaniopsis laurina*), also called kanooka, is a slow-growing Australian tree found mainly along the eastern coast and stream banks. Usually 4.5–9 m tall but occasionally reaching 39 m in the wild, it has bright yellow, bee-attracting flowers and is widely planted as an ornamental shade and street tree. The supplied source does not mention edible uses. [s1:p2–p5]
