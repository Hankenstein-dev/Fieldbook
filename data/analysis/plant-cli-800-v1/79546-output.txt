Horned pondweed (*Zannichellia palustris*) is an aquatic plant found in fresh to brackish waters across the Americas, Europe, Asia, and Australasia. It has long, thread-like leaves, tendril-like roots, and distinctively horned seeds. Its name *palustris* means “of the marsh,” and it is diploid with 2n = 24 chromosomes. [s1:p1]
