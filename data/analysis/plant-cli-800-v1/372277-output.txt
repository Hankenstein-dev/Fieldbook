Limonium multiflorum is a sea-lavender endemic to the rocky cliffs and sandy coastal areas of central-west mainland Portugal. Its purplish-red flowers measure 6.3–7.2 mm across. Assessed as Vulnerable in Portugal, it is threatened by habitat fragmentation, invasive plants, coastal erosion, and poor recruitment of young plants. Recommended conservation measures include restricting access to sensitive sites, controlling invasive species, and regularly monitoring populations. [s1:p1, s1:p2, s2:p1]
