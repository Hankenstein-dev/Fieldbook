Jasmine tobacco (*Nicotiana alata*) is an ornamental tobacco species that grows about 30–60 cm tall. Its fragrant flowers open in the late afternoon or evening from summer through fall and occur in white, yellow, pink, crimson, lime green, or maroon. It prefers moist soil and full sun to partial shade and is not drought-tolerant. Numerous cultivars and hybrids exist; in Iran, it is also sometimes used to produce narghila tobacco.
