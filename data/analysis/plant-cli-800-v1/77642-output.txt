Marsh pea (*Lathyrus palustris*) is a perennial wild pea native to Europe, Asia, and North America. It grows in sunny, wet habitats and has oval to oblong leaflets, branched coiling tendrils, clusters of two to eight pinkish-purple flowers, and dehiscent seed pods. [Source: s1:p1]

Note: passages s1:p2–p3 inconsistently refer to *Lathyrus linifolius*, so they were not relied upon.
