Milk parsley (*Peucedanum palustre*) is an almost hairless biennial in the carrot family, native across most of Europe into Central Asia. It grows in seasonally flooded wetlands, river and estuary margins, and is the main food plant of the Old World swallowtail. Its roots have historically been used as a ginger substitute in southeastern Europe and Russia, and as an antiepileptic remedy in western Latvia.
