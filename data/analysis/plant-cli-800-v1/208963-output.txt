Black swallow-wort (*Vincetoxicum nigrum*) is a perennial herbaceous vine in the dogbane family (Apocynaceae), native to Europe. It has paired, pointed ovate leaves, dark purple-to-black star-shaped flowers, and tapered seed pods containing wind-dispersed seeds. It also spreads through underground rhizomes. [s1:p1, s1:p2, s1:p6]

It is invasive in parts of the United States and Canada, where it can form dense growth that displaces native plants and degrades wildlife habitat. Early detection and complete removal of its rhizomes and seed pods are recommended; mowing limits seed production but does not eliminate established plants. [s1:p8–s1:p13, s1:p15–s1:p16]
