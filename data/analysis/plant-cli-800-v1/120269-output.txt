Arctotis fastuosa (Monarch-of-the-veldt) is an annual, hairy herb in the daisy family, native to Namibia and South Africa and naturalized in parts of southern California. Cultivated as an ornamental, it usually grows to about 35 cm but may reach 90 cm. Its solitary flower heads can span 10 cm, with cultivars producing orange or white rays accented by purple, yellow, or orange and dark central florets. No edible or medicinal uses are documented in the provided source. [s1:p1–p2]
