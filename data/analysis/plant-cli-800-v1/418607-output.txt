Canary laurel (*Laurus novocanariensis*) is an aromatic evergreen shrub or tree in the Lauraceae family, typically growing 6–20 m tall. Native to Madeira and the western and central Canary Islands, it inhabits humid laurisilva cloud forests. It has glossy, lance-shaped leaves, small fragrant yellowish-white flowers on separate male and female plants, and black olive-like berries. Formerly classified as *Laurus azorica*, it is now recognized as genetically, morphologically, and physiologically distinct. (Sources: s1:p2–p5, s1:p8–p9)
