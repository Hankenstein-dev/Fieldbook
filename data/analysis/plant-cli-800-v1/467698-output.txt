Stachys circinata is a perennial-looking herbaceous species in the mint family (Lamiaceae), described by L’Héritier in 1786. It has ascending, silky-haired stems; thick, heart-shaped, toothed, woolly leaves; dense upper flower clusters; and pink corollas. It grows on rocky slopes and at the bases of cliffs from 330 to 1,450 metres in Mediterranean regions of southern Spain, Morocco, Algeria, and Tunisia. [s1:p1–p4]

Reported synonyms include Stachys latifolia, S. numidica, S. pallida, and S. velutina. [s1:p7]
