Crested cockscomb (*Celosia cristata*) is an annual herb in the amaranth family (Amaranthaceae). It grows about 30–80 cm tall and has upright, succulent stems, lance-shaped leaves, and thick, velvety, crest-like flower heads in red, pink, white, or creamy yellow. Cultivated as a cut flower and ornamental, it prefers full sun, warmth, fertile well-drained soil, and regular watering. It is easily propagated from its abundant seeds. [Source: s1:p2–p7]
