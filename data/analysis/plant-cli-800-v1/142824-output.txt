The Japanese painted fern (*Athyrium niponicum*) is a deciduous fern native to eastern Asia. It forms tufted colonies from creeping rhizomes, with subdivided fronds typically 30–75 cm long. Commonly cultivated in shady gardens—especially var. *pictum*—it is prized for gray-green foliage and reddish midribs. The cultivar ‘Silver Falls’ and var. *pictum* have received the Royal Horticultural Society’s Award of Garden Merit. [s1:p1, s1:p3, s1:p5, s1:p6]
