Carlina salicifolia is a low, shrubby perennial thistle growing up to 1 m tall. Native to Madeira and the Canary Islands, it typically occurs on cliffs and rocky slopes at elevations of 200–1,600 m. It has narrow, leathery leaves with densely white-woolly undersides and creamy-yellow flowers, blooming from May to August. Four infraspecific subdivisions are accepted. (Sources: s1:p2–s1:p5)
