Heather garlic (*Allium ericetorum*) is a perennial bulb-forming herb with a round flowering stalk up to 50 cm tall. It bears hemispherical umbels of 15–45 bell-shaped white flowers, with protruding stamens, brown anthers, and a green ovary. The species occurs across southern and central Europe, from Portugal to Ukraine. In mainland Portugal, it inhabits mountain heathland clearings and rocky areas in the north and bogs and peatlands in the south; a 2020 regional assessment classified it as Vulnerable due to habitat decline and other threats.
