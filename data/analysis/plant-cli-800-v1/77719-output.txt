Leucanthemum maximum, or max chrysanthemum, is a rhizomatous perennial in the aster family. Native to France and Spain, it grows 30–70 cm tall and bears large, solitary flower heads with white ray florets surrounding densely packed yellow disc florets. It also occurs elsewhere as an introduced species or garden escapee and was used in developing the hybrid Shasta daisy, Leucanthemum × superbum. [Sources: s1:p1–s1:p4]
