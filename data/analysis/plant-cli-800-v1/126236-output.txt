Cherimoya (*Annona cherimola*) is a slow-growing, deciduous fruit tree in the custard-apple family (Annonaceae), typically reaching 7–8 m. Native to Andean regions of northern Peru and southern Ecuador, it is now cultivated throughout tropical and subtropical regions worldwide. Its fruit has delicate green skin and creamy, moderately juicy white flesh containing numerous dark seeds. The fruit is eaten fresh and is especially valued for juices and cocktails.
