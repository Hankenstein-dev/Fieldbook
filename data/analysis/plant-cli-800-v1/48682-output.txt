Steeplebush (*Spiraea tomentosa*) is a flowering shrub native to eastern Canada and the United States. Growing up to four feet tall, it favors sunny, moist-to-wet sites and produces narrow clusters of tiny pink flowers in summer. Its stems and leaf undersides have dense, woolly hairs. The plant attracts numerous bee species and has traditionally been used medicinally for its astringent properties. [s1:p2–p5]
