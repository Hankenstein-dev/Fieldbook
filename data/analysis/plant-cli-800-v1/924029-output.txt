Berberis oiwakensis (Mountain Grape) is a barberry-family shrub or tree reaching 7 m. Native to parts of East and Southeast Asia at elevations of 600–3,800 m, it bears long compound leaves, clustered racemes, and small dark-blue to nearly black berries. It has also naturalized in South Africa and is used in several ornamental hybrids. The sources do not establish whether its berries are edible. [s1:p1–p4]
