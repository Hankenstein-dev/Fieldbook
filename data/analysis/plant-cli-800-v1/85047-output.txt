Small Scabious (*Scabiosa columbaria*) is a widespread flowering plant native to Europe, Africa, and western Asia. It is a short-lived deciduous perennial that favors calcareous grasslands in Europe. Growing up to 1 m tall and 0.5 m wide, it has branched grey-green leaves and pale lavender or blue flowers from summer through autumn. Four subspecies are currently accepted. [s1:p1–p4]
