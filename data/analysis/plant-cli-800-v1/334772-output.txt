The lemon-scented gum (*Corymbia citriodora*) is a tall tree endemic to northeastern Australia, typically reaching 25–40 m. It has smooth pale bark, narrow glossy leaves that often smell lemony when crushed, white flowers, and woody urn- or barrel-shaped fruit. Its citronellal-rich leaf oil is used in perfumery and mosquito repellents. [s1:p1, s1:p2, s1:p9]
