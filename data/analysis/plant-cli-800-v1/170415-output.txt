The white rain-lily (*Zephyranthes candida*) is a bulbous ornamental native to Argentina, Uruguay, Paraguay, and Brazil and naturalized in many warmer regions worldwide. It has narrow, glossy dark-green leaves and erect white flowers that may be pinkish underneath. It grows best in full sun to partial shade in moist soil and can be propagated by seed or by dividing bulbs and offsets. The plant contains several alkaloids, including lycorine, nerinine, and haemanthamine. [Sources: s1:p1, s1:p3, s1:p7]
