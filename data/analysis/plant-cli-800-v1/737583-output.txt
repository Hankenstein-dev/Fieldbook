Echium boissieri is a large, single-stemmed monocarpic herb in the borage family (Boraginaceae), reaching up to 2.5 m tall. It is native to the western Mediterranean, including the Iberian Peninsula and Northwest Africa. In mainland Portugal, it occurs in dryland crops, abandoned olive groves, roadside slopes, and path verges. The 2020 Portuguese Red List assessed it as Vulnerable because of its small, fragmented population, continuing habitat decline, agricultural intensification, herbicide use, and roadworks.
