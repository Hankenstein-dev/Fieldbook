Elatine hexandra (six-stamened waterwort) is a small, creeping aquatic herb found in shallow, clear, nutrient-poor lakes and pools across Europe. It has yellow-green leaves underwater, tiny pinkish-white flowers typically bearing six stamens, and can self-pollinate while submerged. Globally classified as Least Concern, it is declining in some regions because of drainage and water pollution. It is also suitable for aquariums due to its compact, fast-growing habit.
