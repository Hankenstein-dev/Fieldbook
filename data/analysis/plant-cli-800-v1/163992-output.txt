Hypnum cupressiforme (Cypress-leaved Plait-moss) is a common, widespread moss found on every continent except Antarctica. It forms smooth, dense mats on tree trunks, logs, walls, rocks, and other surfaces, preferring acidic conditions while tolerating pollution. Its creeping, branched stems bear overlapping, curved leaves resembling cypress foliage. Formerly, it was used to fill pillows and mattresses. [s1:p1–p2]
