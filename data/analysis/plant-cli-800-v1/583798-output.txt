*Digitaria debilis* (Desf.) Willd. is a flowering grass in the family Poaceae. It is native to mainland Portugal and is not protected under Portuguese or European Community legislation. The name was published in 1809 in *Enumeratio Plantarum Horti Regii Berolinensis Altera* (p. 91). [Sources: s1:p1–p2, s1:p4–p7]
