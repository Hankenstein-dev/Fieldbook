Floriferous greenweed (*Genista florida*) is a highly branched shrub in the legume family Fabaceae, growing up to 3 m tall. It has lance-shaped leaves, clusters of as many as 30 yellow flowers from May to July, and pods containing up to six seeds. Native to much of the Iberian Peninsula, it inhabits degraded forests, clearings, and heathlands, favoring dry, acidic, nitrogen-poor soils and tolerating cold well. [s1:p1–p3]
