Juniper haircap moss (*Polytrichum juniperinum*) is an evergreen perennial moss found on every continent, including Antarctica. It typically forms thin, interwoven mats and has reddish stems with grey-green leaves tipped red-brown. It favors dry, acidic, exposed or disturbed habitats, including burned or logged areas, mineral soil, rocks, banks, trailsides, and open woods. Male and female plants are separate, and water is required for fertilization. [s1:p1–p3, s1:p6]
