Using only the supplied plant information, return one JSON response.
Do not browse, use tools, or add facts from memory.

SUMMARY
Write one paragraph of no more than 75 words and three sentences.

Start with what the plant is: a tree, shrub, herb, fern, etc.
Include its native origin only when the source explicitly establishes it.

Then describe one or two of its most interesting supported features:
distinctive appearance, ecology, reproduction, adaptations or human uses.
Choose details that help someone understand or remember the plant.

Use plain, everyday English. Omit unexplained botanical terminology,
gene identifiers, naming history, legal references and lists of measurements.
Do not pad the paragraph with generic praise or gardening statements.

There is no minimum length. A short source should produce a short paragraph.
If the source provides only a name, family or naming history, return null.

ACCURACY
Preserve uncertainty and relevant historical or regional limits.
Do not turn occurrence in a place into native origin.
Do not apply a cultivar's or subspecies' traits to the whole species.
Do not invent explanations connecting facts.
If passages disagree, omit the disputed claim rather than silently choosing one.

HUMAN EDIBILITY
Return one status:
- "yes": the source explicitly documents human food use.
- "no": the source explicitly establishes that it is inedible or poisonous
  to humans when eaten, without documenting a qualified edible use.
- "unknown": the source is silent, unclear or contradictory.

"Yes" describes the documented food use, not every part of the plant.
In edibilityNote, specify the edible part and any essential preparation,
restrictions or historical context. Maximum 30 words.

Do not infer food use from animal feeding, medicinal use, a common name,
or membership of a plant family. Leave unspecified parts unspecified.
Different plant parts or preparation conditions are not automatically
contradictory evidence.

If mentioning food use in the summary, retain its essential qualifications
there too. Otherwise leave food information in the edibility fields.

OUTPUT
Return only these JSON fields:
{
  "summary": "...",
  "humanEdibility": "yes",
  "edibilityNote": "...",
  "evidence": {
    "summary": ["source passage IDs"],
    "humanEdibility": ["source passage IDs"]
  }
}

Use null for an unavailable summary or unnecessary edibilityNote.
Use empty evidence lists when no supporting claim is made.
Every evidence reference must support the attached claim.
