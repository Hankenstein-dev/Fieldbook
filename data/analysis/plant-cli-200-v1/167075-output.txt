Plumeria obtusa, or Singapore graveyard flower, is a small Neotropical tree in the dogbane family (Apocynaceae). It has glossy, blunt-tipped leaves and clusters of fragrant, five-petaled white flowers with yellow centers. Native from southern Mexico and Guatemala through the West Indies, it is widely cultivated in warm climates as an ornamental. Its flowers are used ceremonially in Cambodia, where bark decoctions also have traditional medicinal uses. [s1:p1–p4, s1:p6]
