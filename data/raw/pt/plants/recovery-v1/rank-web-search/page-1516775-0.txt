L42: ## Other common names
L43: 
L44: grain oak
L45: 
L46: Shrubs Trees
L47: 
L48: cite6†Buy this from Plant Nurseries5 Suppliers L49: 
L50: A very slow-growing, dense, bushy, evergreen shrub to 5m tall and wide or rarely a small tree with smooth grey bark that cracks with age. Glossy, dark green holly-like leaves with spiny margins are 3-5cm long. Insignificant yellow-green spring flowers may be followed by solitary, spherical to ovoid acorns carried in spiny cups
