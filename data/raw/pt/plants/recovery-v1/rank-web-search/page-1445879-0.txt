L136: cite59†All. L137: Galium lucidum is a species of plants in the cite56†Rubiaceae . It is native to the cite60†Mediterranean region , from cite61†Portugal and cite62†Morocco to cite63†Greece , the range extending northwards into cite64†Germany .[ 1 ][ 2 ]
L138: 
L139: Galium lucidum is an erect, perennial, cite65†glabrous plant up to 70 cm tall. Leaves are narrow and linear, up to 3 cm long, cite66†whorled with as many as 10 per cite67†node . cite68†Inflorescence is a large terminal panicle of many small, white to cream-colored flowers.[ 3 ]
