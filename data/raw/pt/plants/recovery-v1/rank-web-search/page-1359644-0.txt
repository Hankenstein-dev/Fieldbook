L123: Dichanthelium dichotomum
L124: (cite51†L. ) cite52†Gould L125: Dichanthelium dichotomum is a species of cite53†perennial cite54†graminoid in the family cite47†Poaceae that is found throughout the cite55†Americas .
L126: ## Description
L127: 
L128: [cite56†edit ]
L129: Dichanthelium dichotomum has distinct basal cite57†rosettes and occasional branching from nodes above the base. cite58†Culms range from 20 to 110 cm tall and vary from weak to stout, with nodes that are either densely bearded with backward-facing hairs or smooth, and glabrous internodes. Leaves occur both at the base and along the stem, with blades up to 12 cm long and 3–12 mm wide that may be softly hairy, shaggy, or smooth on both surfaces; margins are typically rough and sometimes fringed with hairs.
L130: Sheaths range from glabrous to pilose or villous, and cite59†ligules are ciliate, sometimes reduced or absent. The cite60†panicle is 3–12 cm long and 2–8 cm wide, with spreading to ascending branches that are smooth or slightly roughened. cite61†Spikelets are 2-flowered and broadly ellipsoid, with the first cite62†glume glabrous and 0.5–0.8 mm long. The second glume and sterile lemma are 1.4–2.4 mm long, glabrous or softly hairy, and obtuse.
L131: Fertile lemmas and paleas are hardened, 1.4–2.4 mm long, and lack hyaline margins. The grain is broadly ellipsoid to nearly round, yellowish to purplish, and 1–1.2 mm long.^{cite63†[1] }
L132: ## Distribution and habitat
L133: 
L134: [cite64†edit ]
L135: The variety D. dichotomum var. dichotomum is found from southern cite65†Canada and cite66†Michigan south to cite67†Florida and cite68†Texas . It grows in wet-mesic to dry woods, thickets, and woodland openings.^{cite69†[2] } Another variety, D. dichotomum var. nitidum (cite70†Lam. ) is found from cite71†Pennsylvania and cite72†New Jersey south to Florida and west to cite73†Missouri and Texas. It is also found in cite74†The Bahamas , cite75†West Indies , and from cite76†Mexico south to cite77†Venezuela .
L136: This variety grows in moist sandy or cite78†peaty soil of wet pine cite79†savannas and cite80†pocosin ecotones, as well as cite81†marshes , cite82†swamps , and wet meadows near the coast.^{cite83†[3] } It is fire tolerant.^{cite84†[4] }
