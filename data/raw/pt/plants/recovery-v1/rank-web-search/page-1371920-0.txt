L161: Crocus vernus
L162: (cite100†L. ) cite101†Hill L163: Crocus vernus (spring crocus, giant crocus) is a species of flowering plant in the family cite97†Iridaceae , native to the cite102†Alps , the cite103†Pyrenees , and the cite104†Balkans . Its cite105†cultivars and those of cite106†Crocus flavus (Dutch crocus) are used as cite107†ornamental plants . The Dutch crocuses are larger than the other cultivated crocus species (e.g., Crocus chrysanthus).
L164: Depending on the year, Crocus vernus starts flowering about the same time or up to 2 weeks after cite108†Crocus chrysanthus (snow crocus) starts flowering. Height: 4–6 inches (10–15 cm).
