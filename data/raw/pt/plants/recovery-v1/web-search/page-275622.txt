L46: 
L47: First published in Brit. Fern Gaz. 9: 219 (1965)
L48: 
L49: This species is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this species is Mexico to Tropical America. It is a rhizomatous geophyte and grows primarily in the wet tropical biome. It is used to treat unspecified medicinal disorders and as a medicine.
