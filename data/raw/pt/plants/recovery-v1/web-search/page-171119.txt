L50: 
L51: First published in G.Hegi, Ill. Fl. Mitt.-Eur., ed. 2, 3(2): 1136 (1979)
L52: 
L53: This hybrid is accepted
L54: 
L55: The native range of this hybrid is Europe. It is a perennial and grows primarily in the temperate biome. The hybrid formula is S. dioica × S. latifolia subsp. alba.
