# Glandularia chiricahensis Umber

First published in Syst. Bot. 4: 92 (1979)

This species is accepted

The native range of this species is Arizona to New Mexico and Mexico (Sonora, Coahuila). It is a subshrub and grows primarily in the desert or dry shrubland biome.
