L46: 
L47: First published in Novon 10: 34 (2000)
L48: 
L49: This species is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this species is S. Mexico to Tropical America. It is a subshrub or climbing shrub and grows primarily in the seasonally dry tropical biome. It is used as a medicine.
