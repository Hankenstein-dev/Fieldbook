    Perennial herb, up to 80 cm (as measured on the only sheet from Iraq)

    Stems erect, usually unbranched, quadrangular, narrowly winged, glabrous
Morphology Leaves
    Leaves petiolate; petiole 5–30 mm; lamina ovate to elliptic, 25–35 × 8–15 mm, base cuneate, apex obtuse, margins crenate to crenulate, sometimes irregularly dentate, glabrous

    Inflorescence thyrsoid, forming almost half the length of stem, glabrous

    Flowers 5–7 in lax axillary cymes; pedicel 6–7 mm, glabrous, winged, ± glandular
