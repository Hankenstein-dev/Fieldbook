# Orobanche picridis-hieracioidis Holandre

First published in Fl. Moselle: 322 (1829)

This species is accepted

The native range of this species is Europe to Central Asia and N. Iran, NW. Africa. It is a holoparasite and grows primarily in the temperate biome.
