# Phyllanthus salviifolius Kunth

First published in F.W.H.von Humboldt, A.J.A.Bonpland & C.S.Kunth, Nov. Gen. Sp. 2: 116 (1817)

This species is accepted

The native range of this species is Costa Rica to NW. Venezuela and Peru. It is a tree and grows primarily in the wet tropical biome. It is used as a poison and for food.
