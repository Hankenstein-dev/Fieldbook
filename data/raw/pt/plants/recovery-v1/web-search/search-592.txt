# Limodorum trabutianum Batt.

First published in Bull. Soc. Bot. France 33: 297 (1886)

This species is accepted

The native range of this species is SW. Europe to W. Italy, NW. Africa. It is a holomycotrophic rhizomatous geophyte and grows primarily in the subtropical biome.
