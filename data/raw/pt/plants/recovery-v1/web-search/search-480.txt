# Helminthotheca spinosa (DC.) Talavera & Tremetsb.

First published in AOB Pl. 8: plv142, 14 (2016)

This species is accepted

The native range of this species is Portugal to SW. Spain. It is a perennial or rhizomatous geophyte and grows primarily in the subtropical biome.
