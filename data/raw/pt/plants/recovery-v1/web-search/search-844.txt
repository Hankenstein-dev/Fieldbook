## Descriptions

### According to Flora of Tropical East Africa

Scrophulariaceae, S.A. Ghazanfar, F.N. Hepper & D. Philcox. Flora of Tropical East Africa. 2008

Type
    Types: Egypt, Sinai, Schimper 357 (K!, syn.) & between Suez and Gaza, Bové 77 (P, syn.)
Morphology General Habit
    Biennial herb, 1–2 m or more high.
Morphology Stem
    Stems simple, densely stellate-pubescent, with a whitish or pale rusty colour
Morphology Leaves
    Juvenile plants with a rosette of leaves oblanceolate to oblong, up to 30 cm long, 7–11 cm wide, broadly petiolate, coarsely serrate, acute to acuminate; lower stem leaves similar, smaller, upper ones becoming ovate, ± sessile; all densely stellate-pubescent on both surfaces
Morphology Reproductive morphology Inflorescences

Distribution
    From Sinai, Israel, Jordania and Syria to Afghanistan and south-central Asia, introduced as weed into Yemen.
Morphology General Habit
    Plant biennial, 60–200 cm tall; stem erect, richly branched, densely greyish-tomentose without glandular hairs
Morphology Leaves
    Leaves entire, densely greyish-tomentose; basal leaves with oblong or ovate-oblong blade, 60–300 x 40–140 mm, dentate, with 20–90 mm long petiole; stem leaves with shorter petiole or sessile, rounded-truncate at the base
Morphology Reproductive morphology Inflorescences
    Inflorescence with numerous long branches; flowers in 2–7-flowered clusters (partial inflorescences); lower bracts subcordate-triangular, shortly cuspidate, scarcely longer than partial inflorescences, upper bracts shorter than partial inflorescences; pedicel of primary flower of a cluster 4–10 mm long
Morphology Reproductive morphology Flowers Calyx
    Calyx-lobes densely tomentose, 4–7 mm long, oblong, entire, acute
Morphology Reproductive morphology Flowers Corolla
Elsewhere in the Arabian Peninsula found in Saudi Arabia, UAE, Yemen.
Altitude
    250–700 m.
Note
    Originally described from Lebanon, a widespread species, common in Oman and UAE.
[FO]
