L46: 
L47: First published in Enum. Hort. Berol. Alt. 2: 74 (1822)
L48: 
L49: This hybrid is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this hybrid is W. Medit. It is a shrub and grows primarily in the subtropical biome. The hybrid formula is C. ladanifer × C. monspeliensis.
