# Anacamptis × simorrensis (E.G.Camus) H.Kretzschmar, Eccarius & H.Dietr.

First published in Orchid Gen. Anacamptis Orchis Neotinea, ed. 2: 428 (2007)

This hybrid is accepted

The native range of this hybrid is Europe to E. Medit. It is a tuberous geophyte and grows primarily in the temperate biome. The hybrid formula is A. coriophora × A. pyramidalis.
