# Ranunculus × lutarius (Revel) Bouvet

First published in Bull. Soc. Industr. Agric. Angers, sér. 3, 3: 96 (1874)

This hybrid is accepted

The native range of this hybrid is W. & SW. Europe. It is a hydrosubshrub and grows primarily in the temperate biome. The hybrid formula is R. omiophyllus × R. tripartitus.
