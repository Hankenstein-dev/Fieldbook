L46: 
L47: First published in Brit. Fl. Gard. 5: t. 174 (1833)
L48: 
L49: This hybrid is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this hybrid is SE. Canada to N. Central U.S.A. It is a perennial and grows primarily in the temperate biome. The hybrid formula is L. cardinalis × L. siphilitica.
