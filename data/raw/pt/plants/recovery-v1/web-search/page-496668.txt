L46: 
L47: First published in Mexic. Begon.: 22 (1852)
L48: 
L49: This species is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this species is Central & S. Tropical America, Tobago. It is an annual and grows primarily in the wet tropical biome. It is used as a medicine.
