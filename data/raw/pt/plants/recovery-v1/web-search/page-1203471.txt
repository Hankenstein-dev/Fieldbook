L50: 
L51: First published in N.Wallich, Pl. Asiat. Rar. 2: 16 (1830)
L52: 
L53: This species is accepted
L54: 
L55: The native range of this species is S. India to S. Indo-China. It is a shrub and grows primarily in the wet tropical biome. It is has social uses.
