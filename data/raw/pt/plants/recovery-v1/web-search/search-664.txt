# Dracaena hyacinthoides (L.) Mabb.

First published in Mabberley's Pl.-Book, ed. 4: 1101 (2017)

This species is accepted

The native range of this species is Kenya to S. Africa. It is a rhizomatous geophyte and grows primarily in the seasonally dry tropical biome. It is used as animal food and a medicine, has environmental uses and social uses and for food.
