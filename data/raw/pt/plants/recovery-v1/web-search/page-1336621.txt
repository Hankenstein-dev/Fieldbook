L49: This species is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this species is Cameroon, Eritrea to E. Central & E. Africa, Arabian Peninsula, Indian Subcontinent to Tibet. It is a succulent subshrub and grows primarily in the seasonally dry tropical biome.
