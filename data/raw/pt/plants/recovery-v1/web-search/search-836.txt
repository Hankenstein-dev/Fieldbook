# Eucalyptus pulverulenta Sims

First published in Bot. Mag. 46: t. 2087 (1818)

This species is accepted

The native range of this species is SE. New South Wales. It is a tree and grows primarily in the subtropical biome. It is has environmental uses, as a medicine and for food.
