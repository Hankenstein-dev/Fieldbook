L44: cite22†Orchis L45: # Orchis × penzigiana A.Camus
L46: 
L47: First published in E.G.Camus & A.A.Camus, Iconogr. Orchid. Europe, Texte: 270 (1928)
L48: 
L49: This hybrid is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this hybrid is Europe to Caucasus. It is a tuberous geophyte and grows primarily in the temperate biome. The hybrid formula is O. mascula × O. provincialis.
