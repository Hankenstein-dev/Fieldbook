L46: 
L47: First published in Acta Bot. Barcinon. 52: 65 (2009)
L48: 
L49: This hybrid is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this hybrid is S. Europe. It is a tuberous geophyte and grows primarily in the temperate biome. The hybrid formula is O. apifera × O. tenthredinifera.
