L50: 
L51: First published in Phytotaxa 279: 53 (2016)
L52: 
L53: This species is accepted
L54: 
L55: The native range of this species is E. Colombia to Trinidad-Tobago. It is a perennial or epiphyte and grows primarily in the wet tropical biome. It is used as a medicine and has environmental uses.
