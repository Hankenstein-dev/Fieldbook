# Orobanche litorea Guss.

First published in Fl. Sicul. Prodr. 2: 184 (1828)

This species is accepted

The native range of this species is S. Portugal, SE. France to Sicilia and Türkiye, NW. Africa. It is a holoparasite and grows primarily in the subtropical biome.
