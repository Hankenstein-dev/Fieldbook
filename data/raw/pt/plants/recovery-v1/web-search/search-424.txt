## Descriptions

### According to GrassBase - The Online World Grass Flora

Morphology General Habit
    Perennial; caespitose. Rhizomes elongated; leptomorph. Culms erect; 300-400 cm long; 12-18 mm diam.; woody. Culm-internodes semiterete; thin-walled; yellow; smooth. Culm-nodes with distinct supra-nodal ridge; glabrous. Lateral branches dendroid. Culm-sheaths deciduous; 15-25 cm long; 5-6 times as long as wide; chartaceous; truncate at apex; setose on shoulders. Culm-sheath ligule dentate. Culm-sheath blade linear; reflexed. Leaves cauline. Leaf-sheaths loose; keeled; striately veined; glabrous on surface; outer margin hairy. --------------------------------------------------------------------------------
