# Leontodon × friasi M.Moura & Silva

First published in Phytotaxa 210: 38 (2015)

This hybrid is accepted

The native range of this hybrid is Azores (Flores). It is a subshrub and grows primarily in the subtropical biome. The hybrid formula is L. hochstetteri × L. saxatilis.

# Leontodon L.

First published in Sp. Pl.: 798 (1753)
