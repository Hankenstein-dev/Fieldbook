Includes data from the synonyms:

### According to Flora of Iraq under the synonym Carthamus lanatus

Ghazanfar, S. A., Edmondson, J. R. & Hind, D. J. N. (Eds). (2019). Flora of Iraq, Volume 6: Compositae.Kew Publishing

Morphology General Habit
    Annual herb 20–90 cm tall
Morphology Stem
    Stem stramineous to brownish, simple or usually divaricately branched above middle, sparsely to densely furnished with shaggy arachnoid hairs
Morphology Leaves
    Basal and lower cauline leaves 2.5–14 × 1–4 cm, oblong- or ovate-lanceolate, sessile and ± amplexicaul, veins prominent, green to greyish-arachnoid and minutely glandular, spinous-pinnatifid or sometimes pinnatisect with 3–8 pairs of lobes; upper lobes narrowly oblong with 1 or more pairs of secondary lobes, proximal lobes linear; upper cauline leaves less deeply lobed Green leaves
Bachman, S.P., Brown, M.J.M., Leão, T.C.C., Lughadha, E.N., Walker, B.E. https://nph.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/nph.19592

Conservation
    Predicted extinction risk: not threatened. Confidence: confident
