Melastomataceae, G. E. Wickens. Flora of Tropical East Africa. 1975

Morphology General Habit
    It is easily recognized by the leaves, which are mostly on abbreviated lateral shoots, having a number of ascending pinnate nerves (not all meeting at or near the base); the delicate pink or white flowers are borne in cymose panicles.
