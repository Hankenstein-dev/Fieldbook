# Eudianthe laeta (Aiton) Rchb. ex Willk.

First published in Icon. Descr. Pl. Nov. 1: 29 (1853)

This species is accepted

The native range of this species is W. Medit. to Central Italy. It is an annual and grows primarily in the subtropical biome.
