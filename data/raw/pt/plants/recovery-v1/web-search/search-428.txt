# Glandularia tweedieana (Niven ex Hook.) P.Peralta

First published in Ann. Missouri Bot. Gard. 98: 400 (2011)

This species is accepted

The native range of this species is Bolivia to S. Brazil and N. Argentina. It is a perennial and grows primarily in the subtropical biome.
