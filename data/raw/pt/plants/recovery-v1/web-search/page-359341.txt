L46: 
L47: First published in Ann. Mag. Nat. Hist., ser. 3, 17: 287 (1866)
L48: 
L49: This hybrid is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this hybrid is Madeira. It is a perennial and grows primarily in the subtropical biome. The hybrid formula is P. falcinellum × P. setiferum.
