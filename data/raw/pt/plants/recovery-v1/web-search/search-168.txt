Leguminosae, J. B. Gillett, R. M. Polhill & B. Verdcourt. Flora of Tropical East Africa. 1971

Morphology General Habit
    An annual ± pilose herb up to 40 cm. tall.
Morphology Leaves
    Leaves imparipinnate; stipules rather papery, ovate-lanceolate, entire, up to 4 mm. long and 2·5 mm. wide; rhachis up to 12 cm. long; leaflets usually opposite in 5–9 pairs, elliptic-oblong, up to 10 mm. long and 5 mm. wide, cuneate at the base, notched at the apex, pilose beneath, glabrous above.
