L46: 
L47: First published in Sp. Pl.: 74 (1753)
L48: 
L49: This species is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this species is Europe to Taiwan and Sri Lanka, Macaronesia to Arabian Peninsula and N. Tanzania. It is an annual and grows primarily in the temperate biome. It is has environmental uses and as animal food and a medicine.
