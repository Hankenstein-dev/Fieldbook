# Canna × hybrida Rodigas

First published in Ill. Hort. 42: 75 (1895)

This is an artificial hybrid

The hybrid formula of this artificial cross is C. glauca × C. indica × C. iridiflora.


## Descriptions

Includes data from the synonyms:

  * Canna × generalis L.H.Bailey

### According to Flora of Somalia under the synonym Canna × generalis

M. Thulin et al. Flora of Somalia, Vol. 1-4 [updated 2008] https://plants.jstor.org/collection/FLOS

Morphology General Habit
    Plant to 2 m tall
Morphology Leaves
    Leaves elliptic, acute; blade to 45 cm long
Morphology Reproductive morphology Flowers Calyx
    Calyx c. 2 cm long
Morphology Reproductive morphology Flowers Corolla
    Corolla tube up to 1.5 cm long; lobes 4–5 cm long
Morphology Reproductive morphology Flowers Androecium Staminodes
    Outer staminodes 3, to 10 x 5.5 cm, white to yellow to red or pink, sometimes with markings in other colours; labellum to 8 x 3 cm, streaked or blotched
Morphology Reproductive morphology Flowers Androecium Stamens
    Fertile stamen c. 1.5 mm wide
Morphology Reproductive morphology Flowers Gynoecium Style
    Style c. 4 mm wide
Morphology Reproductive morphology Fruits
    Capsule 2–3 cm long.
Note
    The cultivated Cannas in Somalia probably belong to this horticultural hybrid group of unclear origin. Often called C. indica L., which is one of the putative parents of C. x generalis. C. indica has smaller flowers with outer staminodes less than 1.5 cm wide.
[FSOM]
