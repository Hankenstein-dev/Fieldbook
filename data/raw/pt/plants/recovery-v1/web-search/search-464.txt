# Lantana canescens Kunth

First published in F.W.H.von Humboldt, A.J.A.Bonpland & C.S.Kunth, Nov. Gen. Sp. 2: 259 (1818)

This species is accepted

The native range of this species is Texas to Tropical America. It is a shrub and grows primarily in the seasonally dry tropical biome. It is has social uses and as a medicine.

Distribution
    Biogeografic region: Andean. Elevation range: 570–2680 m a.s.l. Native to Colombia. Colombian departments: Antioquia, Bogotá DC, Cauca, Cundinamarca, Magdalena, Nariño, Santander, Valle del Cauca.
Habit
    Herb, Shrub.
Conservation
    National Red List of Colombia (2021): Potential LC.
Ecology
    Habitat according IUCN Habitats Classification: forest and woodland, savanna, shrubland, artificial - terrestrial.
Vernacular
