L46: 
L47: First published in Bull. Soc. Industr. Agric. Angers, sér. 3, 3: 96 (1874)
L48: 
L49: This hybrid is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this hybrid is W. & SW. Europe. It is a hydrosubshrub and grows primarily in the temperate biome. The hybrid formula is R. omiophyllus × R. tripartitus.
