L46: 
L47: First published in Diss. 3: 133 (1787)
L48: 
L49: This species is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this species is Florida to Caribbean. It is a shrub or tree and grows primarily in the seasonally dry tropical biome. It is has environmental uses and as a medicine.
