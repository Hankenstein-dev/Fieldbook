# Sedum mendozae (Glass & Cházaro) V.V.Byalt

First published in Novosti Sist. Vyssh. Rast. 43: 81 (2012)

This species is accepted

The native range of this species is Mexico (Veracruz: near Tepetzintla). It is a succulent subshrub and grows primarily in the seasonally dry tropical biome.
