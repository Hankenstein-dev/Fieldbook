L50: 
L51: First published in F.W.H.von Humboldt, A.J.A.Bonpland & C.S.Kunth, Nov. Gen. Sp. 2: 261 (1818)
L52: 
L53: This species is accepted
L54: 
L55: The native range of this species is W. South America to Brazil. It is a shrub and grows primarily in the seasonally dry tropical biome. It is used for food.
