L46: 
L47: First published in Sp. Pl.: 389 (1753)
L48: 
L49: This species is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this species is Peru to N. Chile. It is a tree and grows primarily in the subtropical biome. It is used to treat unspecified medicinal disorders, as a medicine and for food.
