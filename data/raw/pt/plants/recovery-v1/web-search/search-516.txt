# Nasturtium × sterile (Airy Shaw) Oefelein

First published in Ber. Schweiz. Bot. Ges. 68: 250 (1958)

This hybrid is accepted

The native range of this hybrid is Europe. It is a perennial or helophyte and grows primarily in the temperate biome. The hybrid formula is N. microphyllum × N. officinale.
