## Descriptions

### According to Flora of the Cayman Islands

George R. Proctor (2012). Flora of the Cayman Isands (Second Edition). Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew

Morphology General Habit
    Acaulescent rosette plant rarely developing a short caudex, usually proliferating freely by stoloniferous offshoots
Morphology Leaves
    Leaves narrowly linear to lanceolate, 40–75 cm long, 3.5–8 cm broad below the middle; apical spine 2.5–4 cm long; upper surface smooth but concave; marginal prickles numerous, black, 2–5 mm long, upcurved, sinuous or recurved from a black deltoid base
