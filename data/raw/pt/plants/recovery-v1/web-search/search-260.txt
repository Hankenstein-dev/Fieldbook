# Neotinea conica (Willd.) R.M.Bateman

First published in Bot. J. Linn. Soc. 142: 12 (2003)

This species is accepted

The native range of this species is SW. France to NW. Africa. It is a tuberous geophyte and grows primarily in the subtropical biome.
