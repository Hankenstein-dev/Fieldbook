L44: cite22†Mucuna L45: # Mucuna sloanei Fawc. & Rendle
L46: 
L47: First published in J. Bot. 55: 36 (1917)
L48: 
L49: This species is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this species is S. Mexico and S. Florida to Tropical America, W. Tropical Africa to Angola. It is a liana and grows primarily in the wet tropical biome. It is used as a medicine, has environmental uses and for food.
