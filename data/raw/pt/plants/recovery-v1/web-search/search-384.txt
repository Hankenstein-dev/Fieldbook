# Lutheria splendens (Brongn. ex Neumann) Barfuss & W.Till

First published in Phytotaxa 279: 53 (2016)

This species is accepted

The native range of this species is E. Colombia to Trinidad-Tobago. It is a perennial or epiphyte and grows primarily in the wet tropical biome. It is used as a medicine and has environmental uses.
