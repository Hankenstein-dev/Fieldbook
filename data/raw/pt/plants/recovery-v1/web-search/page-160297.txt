L46: 
L47: First published in G.-F.Cuvier, Dict. Sci. Nat., ed. 2. 7: 384 (1817)
L48: 
L49: This species is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this species is S. Tropical America. It is an annual and grows primarily in the seasonally dry tropical biome. It is has environmental uses and as a medicine.
