L46: 
L47: First published in Cl. Umbell. Emend.: 76 (1767)
L48: 
L49: This species is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this species is E. Central & S. Europe to Central Asia and Iran, NW. Africa. It is an annual and grows primarily in the temperate biome.
