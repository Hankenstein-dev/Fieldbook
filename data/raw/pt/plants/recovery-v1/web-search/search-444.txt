# Blechnum cordatum (Desv.) Hieron.

First published in Hedwigia 47: 239 (1908)

This species is accepted

The native range of this species is S. Tropical America. It is a perennial or lithophyte and grows primarily in the temperate biome. It is used to treat unspecified medicinal disorders, as a medicine and for food.
