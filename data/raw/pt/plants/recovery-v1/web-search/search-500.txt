# Holcus × azoricus M.Seq. & Castrov.

First published in Bot. J. Linn. Soc. 154: 261 (2007)

This hybrid is accepted

The native range of this hybrid is Azores. It is a perennial and grows primarily in the temperate biome. The hybrid formula is H. lanatus × H. rigidus.
