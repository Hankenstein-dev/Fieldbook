# Epipactis helleborine subsp. tremolsii (Pau) E.Klein

First published in Orchidee (Hamburg) 30: 49 (1979)

This subspecies is accepted

The native range of this subspecies is Medit. to Caucasus. It is a perennial or rhizomatous geophyte and grows primarily in the temperate biome.
