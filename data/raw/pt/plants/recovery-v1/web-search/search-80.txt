### According to GrassBase - The Online World Grass Flora under the synonym Vulpia ciliata

Morphology General Habit
    Annual; caespitose. Culms erect, or decumbent; 5-30 cm long; 1-2 -noded. Leaf-sheaths without keel; smooth; glabrous on surface. Ligule an eciliate membrane. Ovary glabrous.
Morphology Reproductive morphology Fruits
    Caryopsis with adherent pericarp; linear; glabrous. Hilum linear; 1 length of caryopsis.
Distribution
    Europe: northern, central, southwestern, southeastern, and eastern. Africa: north and Macaronesia. Asia-temperate: Soviet Middle Asia, Caucasus, and western Asia. Asia-tropical: India. Australasia: Australia and New Zealand.
