# Pityrogramma ebenea (L.) Proctor

First published in Brit. Fern Gaz. 9: 219 (1965)

This species is accepted

The native range of this species is Mexico to Tropical America. It is a rhizomatous geophyte and grows primarily in the wet tropical biome. It is used to treat unspecified medicinal disorders and as a medicine.
