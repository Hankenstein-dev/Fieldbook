L46: 
L47: First published in Chlor. Andina 1: 151 (1856)
L48: 
L49: This species is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this species is Tropical & Subtropical America to Falkland Islands. It is an annual or perennial and grows primarily in the seasonally dry tropical biome. It is used as a medicine.
