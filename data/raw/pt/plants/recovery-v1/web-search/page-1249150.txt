L46: 
L47: First published in Skvortsovia 4(3): 77 (2018)
L48: 
L49: This hybrid is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this hybrid is as a cultigen derived from artificial hybrids. It is a perennial and grows primarily in the subtropical biome. The hybrid formula is C. elegans × C. setaceus.
