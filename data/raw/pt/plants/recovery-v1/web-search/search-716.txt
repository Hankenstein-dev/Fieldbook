# Ophrys × olbiensis E.G.Camus

First published in E.G.Camus, P.Bergon & A.A.Camus, Monogr. Orchid.: 306 (1908)

This hybrid is accepted

The native range of this hybrid is S. Europe. It is a tuberous geophyte and grows primarily in the subtropical biome. The hybrid formula is O. bombyliflora × O. scolopax.
