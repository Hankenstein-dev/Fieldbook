# Ophrys × fernandii Rolfe

First published in Orchid Rev. 26: 102 (1918)

This hybrid is accepted

The native range of this hybrid is W. Medit. It is a tuberous geophyte and grows primarily in the subtropical biome. The hybrid formula is O. bombyliflora × O. speculum.
