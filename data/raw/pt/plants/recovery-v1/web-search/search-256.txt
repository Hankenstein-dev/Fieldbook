# Kniphofia linearifolia Baker

First published in Beibl. Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 35: 5 (1892)

This species is accepted

The native range of this species is Malawi to S. Africa. It is a perennial and grows primarily in the subtropical biome.


## Distribution

### Native to:
