# Coelogyne tomentosa Lindl.

First published in Fol. Orchid. 5: 3 (1854)

This species is accepted

The native range of this species is Peninsula Thailand to W. Malesia. It is a pseudobulbous epiphyte and grows primarily in the wet tropical biome.
