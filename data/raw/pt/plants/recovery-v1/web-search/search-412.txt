### According to Flora Zambesiaca under the synonym Verbena × hybrida

Flora Zambesiaca. Vol 8, Pt 7. Avicenniaceae, R. Fernandes. Nesogenaceae, M.A. Diniz. Verbenaceae, R. Fernandes. Lamiaceae, R. Fernandes. 2005.

Morphology General
    This is the common garden verbena with white, pink, red, blue, purple or variegated flowers and is easily distinguished by the leaves lobed or toothed and corolla limb up to c. 2 cm wide.
Morphology Stem
    Stems decumbent or ascending, much branched; branches ascending, slender
Morphology Leaves
    Leaves variable shape and size, ± deeply incised-pinnatifid, often ± 3-partite the lowest divisions usually again pinnatifid; body of blade and lowest lobes relatively broad; lobes sharply acute
Morphology Reproductive morphology Inflorescences
    Spikes flattened and almost capitate, later elongating to c. 15 cm, densely many-flowered
Morphology Reproductive morphology Inflorescences Bracts
    Bractlets c. one third as long as the calyx
Morphology Reproductive morphology Flowers Calyx
    Calyx 7–13 mm long
Morphology Reproductive morphology Flowers Corolla
    Corolla showy, blue, purple, pink or white; tube 15–20 mm long; limb 5–11 mm wide.
Distribution
    District code: MOZ M. District code: ZIM C. Zambia Mozambique
[FZ]
