L46: 
L47: First published in Tent. Fl. Germ. 1: 34 (1788)
L48: 
L49: This species is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this species is Canary Islands, Europe to Medit. It is a perennial or rhizomatous geophyte and grows primarily in the temperate biome.
