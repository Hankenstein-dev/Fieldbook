L50: 
L51: First published in Folia Dendrol. 10: 292 (1983)
L52: 
L53: This hybrid is accepted
L54: 
L55: The native range of this hybrid is N. China. It is a shrub or tree and grows primarily in the temperate biome. The hybrid formula is J. chinensis × J. sabina.
