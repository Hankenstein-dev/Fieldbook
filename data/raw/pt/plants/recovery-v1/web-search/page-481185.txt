L46: 
L47: First published in Bot. Mag. (Tokyo) 39: 118 (1925)
L48: 
L49: This species is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this species is Himalaya to Central Japan and Taiwan. It is a perennial or lithophyte and grows primarily in the temperate biome.
