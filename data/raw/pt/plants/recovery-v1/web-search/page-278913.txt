L46: 
L47: First published in Labiat. Gen. Spec.: 11 (1832)
L48: 
L49: This species is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this species is Mexico, N. Venezuela, S. Bolivia to Brazil and N. Argentina. It is an annual or subshrub and grows primarily in the seasonally dry tropical biome.
