# Malus × dasyphylla Borkh.

First published in Theor. Prakt. Handb. Forstbot. 2: 1269 (1803), nom. rej.

This hybrid is accepted

The native range of this hybrid is as naturally occuring between the native and naturalised parents. It is a tree and grows primarily in the temperate biome. The hybrid formula is M. domestica × M. sylvestris.
