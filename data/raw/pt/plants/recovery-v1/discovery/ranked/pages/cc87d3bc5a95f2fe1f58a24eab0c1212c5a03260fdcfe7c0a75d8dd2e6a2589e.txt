L174: Phalacrodiscus Less.
L175: Leucanthemum is a cite99†genus of flowering plants in the aster cite100†family , cite92†Asteraceae .^{cite101†[2] } Species range naturally from Europe through the Caucasus, Turkey, Iran, Central Asia, and Siberia to the Russian Far East.^{cite98†[1] } Some species are known on other continents as cite102†introduced species , and some are cultivated as cite103†ornamental plants . The name Leucanthemum derives from the Greek words λευκός – leukos ("white") and ἄνθεμον – anthemon ("flower").
L176: Common names for Leucanthemum species usually include the name daisy (e.g. cite84†ox-eye daisy , cite104†Shasta daisy ), but "cite105†daisy " can also refer to numerous other genera in the Asteraceae family.
L177: ## Description
L178: 
L179: [cite106†edit ]
L180: Leucanthemum species are cite107†perennial plants growing from red-tipped cite108†rhizomes . The plant produces one erect stem usually reaching 40 to 130 centimeters tall, but known to exceed 2 meters at times. It is branching or unbranched and hairy to hairless. Some species have mainly basal leaves, and some have leaves along the stem, as well. Some leaves are borne on cite109†petioles , and others are sessile, attached to the stem at their bases. They vary in shape, and some are lobed or toothed.
L181: The cite110†flower head is solitary, paired, or in a group of three on the stem. The base of the head is layered with up to 60 or more rough-edged cite111†phyllaries . The Leucanthemum head has about 13 to 34 ray florets of various widths, occasionally more, and rarely none. The ray florets are always white but fade pink with age. The head has over 100 yellow disc florets at the center. The fruit is a ribbed, hairless cypsela.^{cite112†[3] }
L182: ## Ecology
L183: 
L184: [cite113†edit ]
L185: 
L186: Leucanthemum species are used as food plants by the cite114†larvae of some cite115†Lepidoptera species, including the cite116†bucculatricid leaf-miners cite117†Bucculatrix argentisignella , cite118†B. leucanthemella , and cite119†B. nigricomella , which feed exclusively on cite84†Leucanthemum vulgare .
