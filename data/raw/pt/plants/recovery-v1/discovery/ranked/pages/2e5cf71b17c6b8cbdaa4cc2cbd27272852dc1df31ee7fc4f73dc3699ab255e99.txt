L155: ### Publication
L156: 
L157: [cite62†edit ]
L158: 
L159: It was first published as Hygrochilus Pfitzer by cite63†Ernst Hugo Heinrich Pfitzer in 1897[ 4 ] with Hygrochilus parishii (Rchb. f.) Pfitzer. as the type species.[ 5 ] It was later integrated into the genus Phalaenopsis Blume on the subgeneric level as Phalaenopsis subg. Hygrochilus (Pfitzer) Kocyan & Schuit. published by cite64†Alexander Kocyan and cite65†André Schuiteman in 2014.[ 6 ][ 1 ]
