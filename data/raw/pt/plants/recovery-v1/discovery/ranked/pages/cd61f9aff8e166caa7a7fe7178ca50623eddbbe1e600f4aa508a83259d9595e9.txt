L11@P0: ABSTRACT. Four new species of Begonia (sect. Coelocentrum), namely B. debaoensis, B.
L12@P0: pseudodaxinensis, B. pseudoleprosa and B. semiparietalis, from the limestone areas in Guangxi Zhuangzu
L13@P0: Autonomous Region, southern China are described and illustrated. A somatic chromosome number of 2n = 30
L14@P0: is determined for three of them. Begonia debaoensis resembles B. bonii Gagnep., differing in having smaller
L15@P0: leaves; a slender rhizome with long internodes; bicolored outer tepals that are pink toward the margins and
L16@P0: whitish in the middle; and bearing abnormal, feminized stamens on staminate flowers. It is perhaps of hybrid
L17@P0: origin. Begonia pseudodaxinensis is similar to B. daxinensis T.C. Ku, differing in the leaves being entirely
L18@P0: green and without white maculation; the stipule apex obtuse or emarginate; and the rhizome stout and nearly
L19@P0: glabrous. Begonia pseudoleprosa bears a superficial resemblance to B. leprosa Hance (sect. Leprosae),
L20@P0: differing in the fruits being winged, trigonous obovoid-ellipsoid and with parietal placentation. It also
L21@P0: resembles B. bonii Gagnep., differing in the leaves being subcoriaceous, nearly flat and very sparsely
L22@P0: setulose or subglabrous on the adaxial surface; and petioles and the main veins on the lower leaf surface
L23@P0: being appressed hairy. Begonia semiparietalis is also somewhat similar to B. bonii Gagnep., differing in
L24@P0: the leaves having white maculation along main veins and the upper surface sparsely setose-pilose; ovaries
L25@P0: and fruits long glandular-pilose; placentation parietal on upper half of the ovary and axile on the lower
L26@P0: half.
