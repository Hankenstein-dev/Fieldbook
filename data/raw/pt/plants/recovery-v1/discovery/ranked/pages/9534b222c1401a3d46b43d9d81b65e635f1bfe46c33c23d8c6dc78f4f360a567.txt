L30@P2: are distinguished by the latex which flows from all broken portions; the alternate
L31@P2: usually leathery leaves; the prominent stipule (↑) which encloses the terminal bud
L32@P2: and the “fig” (↑) or syconia. This fleshy receptacle bears the flowers on the inside;
L33@P2: as the seeds mature the receptacle enlarges and often softens (Think of the edible
L34@P2: fig!). Some of the common species are:

L36@P2: Banyan, with large, broadly ovate leaves up to 30 x 20 cm, softly pubescent
