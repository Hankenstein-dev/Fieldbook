L15: # 12. Ornithogalum L. Sp. Pi. 306. 1753
L16: Scapose cite5†herbs , with coated bulbs, narrow basal fleshy leaves, and cite6†large cite7†white or cite8†yellow flowers cite9†in a terminal bracted corymb or raceme. Perianth-segments equal or nearly so, separate, white, or sometimes green without, persistent, faintly several-nerved. Stamens hypogy-nous; filaments flattened, often broad; anthers versatile, introrse. Ovary 3-celled, sessile; ovules several or numerous in each cavity; style short or columnar, 3-sided; stigma capitate, 3-lobed or 3-ridged.
L17: Capsule subglobose, 3-sided or 3-lobed, loculicidal. Seeds cite10†black . [Greek, signifying bird's milk, said to be in allusion to the egg-white color of the flowers in some cite11†species .]
L18: About 75 species, natives of Europe, Asia and Africa. Type species: O. arabicum L.
