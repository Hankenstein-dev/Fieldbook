L44: cite22†Lantana L45: # Lantana canescens Kunth
L46: 
L47: First published in F.W.H.von Humboldt, A.J.A.Bonpland & C.S.Kunth, Nov. Gen. Sp. 2: 259 (1818)
L48: 
L49: This species is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this species is Texas to Tropical America. It is a shrub and grows primarily in the seasonally dry tropical biome. It is used as a medicine and has social uses.
