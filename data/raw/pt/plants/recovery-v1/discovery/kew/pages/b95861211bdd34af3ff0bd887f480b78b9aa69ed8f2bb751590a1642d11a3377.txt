L46: 
L47: First published in Hist. & Mém. Acad. Roy. Sci. Toulouse 3: 312 (1788)
L48: 
L49: This hybrid is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this hybrid is W. Medit. It is a shrub and grows primarily in the subtropical biome. The hybrid formula is C. populifolius × C. salviifolius.
