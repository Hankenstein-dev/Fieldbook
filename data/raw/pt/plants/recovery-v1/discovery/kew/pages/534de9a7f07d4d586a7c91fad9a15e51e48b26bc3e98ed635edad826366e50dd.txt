L50: 
L51: First published in Bull. Soc. Bot. France 55: 475 (1908)
L52: 
L53: This hybrid is accepted
L54: 
L55: The native range of this hybrid is W. Medit. It is a shrub and grows primarily in the subtropical biome. The hybrid formula is C. ladanifer subsp. mauritianus × C. salviifolius.
