L44: cite22†Anacamptis L45: # Anacamptis × simorrensis (E.G.Camus) H.Kretzschmar, Eccarius & H.Dietr.
L46: 
L47: First published in Orchid Gen. Anacamptis Orchis Neotinea, ed. 2: 428 (2007)
L48: 
L49: This hybrid is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this hybrid is Europe to E. Medit. It is a tuberous geophyte and grows primarily in the temperate biome. The hybrid formula is A. coriophora × A. pyramidalis.
