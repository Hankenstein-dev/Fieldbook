L50: 
L51: First published in Willdenowia 47: 214 (2017)
L52: 
L53: This species is accepted
L54: 
L55: The native range of this species is Macaronesia, N. Africa to Mauritania, Sicilia (Linosa) to NW. India and NE. Tropical Africa. It is an annual and grows primarily in the desert or dry shrubland biome.
