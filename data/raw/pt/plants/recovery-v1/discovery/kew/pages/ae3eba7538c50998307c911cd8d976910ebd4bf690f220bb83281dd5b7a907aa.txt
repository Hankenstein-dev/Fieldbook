L46: 
L47: First published in F.W.H.von Humboldt, A.J.A.Bonpland & C.S.Kunth, Nov. Gen. Sp. 5: 110 (1821)
L48: 
L49: This species is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this species is S. Tropical America. It is a liana and grows primarily in the seasonally dry tropical biome. It is used as a poison and a medicine.
