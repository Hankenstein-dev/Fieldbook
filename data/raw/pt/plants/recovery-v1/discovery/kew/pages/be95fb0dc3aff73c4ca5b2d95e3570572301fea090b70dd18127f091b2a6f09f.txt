L44: cite22†Delphinium L45: # Delphinium ajacis L.
L46: 
L47: First published in Sp. Pl.: 531 (1753)
L48: 
L49: This species is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this species is S. Europe to W. Himalaya and Central Asia. It is an annual and grows primarily in the temperate biome. It is used to treat unspecified medicinal disorders, has environmental uses, as a medicine and for food.
