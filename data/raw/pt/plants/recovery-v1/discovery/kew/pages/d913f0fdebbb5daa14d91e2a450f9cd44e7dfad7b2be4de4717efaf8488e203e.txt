L50: 
L51: First published in Fl. Peruv. 2: 23 (1799)
L52: 
L53: This species is accepted
L54: 
L55: The native range of this species is W. South America to Venezuela. It is a shrub or tree and grows primarily in the wet tropical biome. It is used as a medicine.
