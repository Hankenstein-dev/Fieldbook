L46: 
L47: First published in Mém. Soc. Émul. Doubs, sér. 3, 3: 6 (1859)
L48: 
L49: This species is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this species is W. Medit. to NW. Italy (W. Liguria). It is a tuberous geophyte and grows primarily in the subtropical biome.
