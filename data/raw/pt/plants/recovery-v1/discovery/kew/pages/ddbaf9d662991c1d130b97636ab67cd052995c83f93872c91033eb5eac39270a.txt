L46: 
L47: First published in Amer. Fern J. 77: 101 (1987)
L48: 
L49: This species is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this species is Tropical & Subtropical America. It is a rhizomatous geophyte and grows primarily in the wet tropical biome. It is used as a medicine.
