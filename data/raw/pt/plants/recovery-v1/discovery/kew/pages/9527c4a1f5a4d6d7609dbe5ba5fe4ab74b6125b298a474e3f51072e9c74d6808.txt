L46: 
L47: First published in N.Wallich, Pl. Asiat. Rar. 3: 86 (1832)

L49: This species is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this species is Pakistan to China (Yunnan, Guangxi) and Peninsula Malaysia (Langkawi). It is a subshrub or shrub and grows primarily in the wet tropical biome.
