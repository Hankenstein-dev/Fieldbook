L46: 
L47: First published in Fl. Peruv. 1: 7 (1798)
L48: 
L49: This species is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this species is SE. Colombia to NW. Bolivia and N. Brazil. It is a shrub and grows primarily in the wet tropical biome. It is has environmental uses and as a medicine.
