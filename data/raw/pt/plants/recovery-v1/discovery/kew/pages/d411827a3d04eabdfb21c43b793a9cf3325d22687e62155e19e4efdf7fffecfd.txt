L46: 
L47: First published in Index Seminum (WU, Vindobonensis) 1849-1850(Adv. Bot.): [10] (1851)
L48: 
L49: This species is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this species is Tropical & Subtropical America. It is an annual and grows primarily in the seasonally dry tropical biome. It is used as a medicine and for food.
