L51: First published in Phytotaxa 441: 225 (2020)
L52: 
L53: This hybrid is accepted
L54: 
L55: The native range of this hybrid is Northern Prov. It is a succulent subshrub and grows primarily in the subtropical biome. The hybrid formula is K. luciae × K. sexangularis.
