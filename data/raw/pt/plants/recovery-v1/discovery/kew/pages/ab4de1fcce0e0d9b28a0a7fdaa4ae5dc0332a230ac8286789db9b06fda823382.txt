L48: cite20†Equisetum L49: # Equisetum × litorale Kühlew. ex Rupr.
L50: 
L51: First published in Beitr. Pflanzenk. Russ. Reiches 4: 91 (1845)
L52: 
L53: This hybrid is accepted
L54: 
L55: The native range of this hybrid is Subarctic & Temp. Northern Hemisphere. It is a rhizomatous geophyte and grows primarily in the temperate biome. The hybrid formula is E. arvense × A. fluviatile.
