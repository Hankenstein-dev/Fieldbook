L50: 
L51: First published in Man. Fl. Alrededores Buenos Aires: 398 (1953)
L52: 
L53: This species is accepted
L54: 
L55: The native range of this species is Brazil (São Paulo) to NE. & N. Central Argentina. It is a subshrub and grows primarily in the subtropical biome.
