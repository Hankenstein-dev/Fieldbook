L46: 
L47: First published in Mém. Soc. Phys. Genève 1: 423 (1822)
L48: 
L49: This species is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this species is Nepal to Nansei-shotō (Ishigaki-jima, Iriomote-jima) and Peninsula Malaysia. It is a tree and grows primarily in the subtropical biome.
