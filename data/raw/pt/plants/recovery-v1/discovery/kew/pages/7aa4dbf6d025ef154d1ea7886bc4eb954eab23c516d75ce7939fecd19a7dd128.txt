L46: 
L47: First published in PhytoKeys 129: 54 (2019)
L48: 
L49: This species is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this species is Egypt to S. Africa, SW. Arabian Peninsula, Maldives, Sri Lanka. It is a sometimes tuberous subshrub and grows primarily in the seasonally dry tropical biome.
