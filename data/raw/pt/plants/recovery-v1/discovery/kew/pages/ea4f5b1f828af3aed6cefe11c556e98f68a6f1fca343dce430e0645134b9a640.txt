L46: 
L47: First published in Revis. Gen. Pl. 2: 538 (1891)
L48: 
L49: This species is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this species is Mexico (Veracruz) to Chile. It is a subshrub and grows primarily in the seasonally dry tropical biome. It is used to treat infection and unspecified medicinal disorders and as a medicine.
