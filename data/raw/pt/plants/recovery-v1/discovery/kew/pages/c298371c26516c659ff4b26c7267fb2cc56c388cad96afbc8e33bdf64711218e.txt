L47: First published in Chênes, Atlas 2: 55 (1935)
L48: 
L49: This hybrid is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this hybrid is Iberian Peninsula. It is a shrub or tree and grows primarily in the temperate biome. The hybrid formula is Q. faginea × Q. rotundifolia.
