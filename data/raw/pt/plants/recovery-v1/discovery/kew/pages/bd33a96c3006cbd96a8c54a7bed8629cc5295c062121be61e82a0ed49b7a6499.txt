L48: cite20†Aloe L49: # Aloe × schimperi Tod.
L50: 
L51: First published in Hort. Bot. Panorm. 1: 70 (1878)
L52: 
L53: This hybrid is accepted
L54: 
L55: The native range of this hybrid is Cape Prov. It is a succulent perennial and grows primarily in the subtropical biome. The hybrid formula is A. maculata × A. striata.
