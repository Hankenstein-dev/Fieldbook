L44: cite22†Eragrostis L45: # Eragrostis tenuifolia (A.Rich.) Hochst. ex Steud.
L46: 
L47: First published in Syn. Pl. Glumac. 1: 268 (1854)
L48: 
L49: This species is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this species is Africa to Indo-China. It is a perennial or annual and grows primarily in the seasonally dry tropical biome. It is used as a medicine.
