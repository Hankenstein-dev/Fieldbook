L46: 
L47: First published in Hedwigia 47: 239 (1908)
L48: 
L49: This species is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this species is S. Tropical America. It is a perennial or lithophyte and grows primarily in the temperate biome. It is used to treat unspecified medicinal disorders, as a medicine and for food.
