L46: 
L47: First published in Taxon 34: 669 (1985)
L48: 
L49: This species is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this species is Mexico to NW. Colombia. It is a shrub or tree and grows primarily in the wet tropical biome. It is used as a medicine.
