L46: 
L47: First published in Calyx 2: 58 (1992)
L48: 
L49: This hybrid is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this hybrid is Madeira. It is a succulent subshrub and grows primarily in the subtropical biome. The hybrid formula is A. glandulosum × A. glutinosum.
