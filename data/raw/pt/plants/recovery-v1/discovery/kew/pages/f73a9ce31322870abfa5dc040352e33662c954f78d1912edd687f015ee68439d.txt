L44: cite22†Ludwigia L45: # Ludwigia erecta (L.) H.Hara
L46: 
L47: First published in J. Jap. Bot. 28: 292 (1953)
L48: 
L49: This species is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this species is Tropical & Subtropical America. It is an annual or subshrub and grows primarily in the seasonally dry tropical biome. It is used as animal food and a medicine, has social uses and for food.
