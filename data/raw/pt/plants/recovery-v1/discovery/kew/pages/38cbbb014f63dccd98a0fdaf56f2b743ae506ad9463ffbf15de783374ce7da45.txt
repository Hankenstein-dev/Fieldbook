L46: 
L47: First published in Fl. Morbihan: 731 (1852)
L48: 
L49: This species is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this species is Canary Islands, W. Europe, Medit. to Central Asia and W. Himalaya. It is an annual and grows primarily in the temperate biome.
