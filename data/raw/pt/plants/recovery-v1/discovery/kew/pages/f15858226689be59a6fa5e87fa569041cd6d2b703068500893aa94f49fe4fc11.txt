L46: 
L47: First published in Fl. Aegypt.-Arab.: CXXIV, 186 (1775)
L48: 
L49: This species is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this species is Aegean Islands to Iran and Arabian Peninsula, Africa. It is a rhizomatous geophyte and grows primarily in the seasonally dry tropical biome.
