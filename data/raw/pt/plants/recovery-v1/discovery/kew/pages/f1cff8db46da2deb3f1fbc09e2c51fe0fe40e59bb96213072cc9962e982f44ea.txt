L46: 
L47: First published in Schriften Ges. Beförd. Gesammten Naturwiss. Marburg 2: 235 (1831)
L48: 
L49: This hybrid is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this hybrid is as naturally occuring between the native and naturalised parents. It is a tree and grows primarily in the temperate biome. The hybrid formula is S. alba × S. babylonica × S. euxina.
