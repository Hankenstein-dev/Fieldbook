L46: 
L47: First published in Pl. Rar.: 387 (1826)
L48: 
L49: This hybrid is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this hybrid is Europe. It is a perennial and grows primarily in the temperate biome. The hybrid formula is M. aquatica × M. suaveolens.
