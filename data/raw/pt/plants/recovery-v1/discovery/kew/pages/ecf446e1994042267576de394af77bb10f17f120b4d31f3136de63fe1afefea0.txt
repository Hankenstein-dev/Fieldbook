L48: cite20†Phyllanthus L49: # Phyllanthus salviifolius Kunth
L50: 
L51: First published in F.W.H.von Humboldt, A.J.A.Bonpland & C.S.Kunth, Nov. Gen. Sp. 2: 116 (1817)
L52: 
L53: This species is accepted
L54: 
L55: The native range of this species is Costa Rica to NW. Venezuela and Peru. It is a tree and grows primarily in the wet tropical biome. It is used as a poison and for food.
