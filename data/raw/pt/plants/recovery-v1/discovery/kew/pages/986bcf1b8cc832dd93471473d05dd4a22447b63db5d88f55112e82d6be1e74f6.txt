L46: 
L47: First published in Fl. Rust. 3: 87 (1793)
L48: 
L49: This hybrid is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this hybrid is Caucasus to Central Asia and Iran. It is a perennial and grows primarily in the temperate biome. The hybrid formula is M. falcata × M. sativa.
