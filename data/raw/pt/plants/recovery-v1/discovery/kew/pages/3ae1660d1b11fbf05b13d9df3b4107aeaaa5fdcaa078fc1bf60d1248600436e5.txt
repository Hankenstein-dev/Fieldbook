L46: 
L47: First published in Syn. Pl. Aequin. 1: 218 (1822)
L48: 
L49: This species is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this species is Macaronesia, Hungary to Medit. and Arabian Peninsula. It is an annual or perennial and grows primarily in the subtropical biome.
