L46: 
L47: First published in Linnaea 5: 604 (1830)
L48: 
L49: This species is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this species is Mexico to Colombia. It is a perennial and grows primarily in the wet tropical biome. It is has environmental uses, as a medicine and for food.
