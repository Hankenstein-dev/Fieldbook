L50: 
L51: First published in J. Bot. (Morot) 6: 26 (1892)
L52: 
L53: This hybrid is accepted
L54: 
L55: The native range of this hybrid is Medit. It is a tuberous geophyte and grows primarily in the subtropical biome. The hybrid formula is S. cordigera × S. lingua.
