L46: 
L47: First published in Linnaea 1: 200 (1826)
L48: 
L49: This species is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this species is Mexico to S. Tropical America. It is a perennial and grows primarily in the wet tropical biome. It is used as a medicine.
