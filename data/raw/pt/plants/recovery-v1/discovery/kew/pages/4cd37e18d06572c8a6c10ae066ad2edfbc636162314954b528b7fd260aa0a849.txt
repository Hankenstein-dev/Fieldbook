L50: 
L51: First published in Sp. Pl.: 737 (1753)
L52: 
L53: This species is accepted
L54: 
L55: The native range of this species is Medit. to Central Asia and Afghanistan. It is a scrambling annual and grows primarily in the subtropical biome.
