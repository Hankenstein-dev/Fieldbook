L48: cite20†Lantana L49: # Lantana × strigocamara R.W.Sanders
L50: 
L51: First published in Sida 22: 392 (2006)
L52: 
L53: This hybrid is accepted
L54: 
L55: The native range of this hybrid is as naturally occuring between the naturalised species. It is a shrub and grows primarily in the subtropical biome. The hybrid formula is L. camara × L. nivea × L. scabrida × L. splendens. It is used as a medicine.
