L46: 
L47: First published in Lidia 6: 25 (2003)
L48: 
L49: This species is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this species is Iberian Peninsula Sahel to S. Africa, Mascarenes, N. India to Indo-China, SE. U.S.A. to Mexico, S. South America. It is an annual and grows primarily in the seasonally dry tropical biome.
