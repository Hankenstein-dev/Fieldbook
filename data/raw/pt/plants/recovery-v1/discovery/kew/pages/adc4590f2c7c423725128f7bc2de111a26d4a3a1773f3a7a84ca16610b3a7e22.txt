L46: 
L47: First published in Nov. Stirp. Pug. 1: 38 (1828)
L48: 
L49: This hybrid is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this hybrid is Old World. It is a hydrosubshrub and grows primarily in the temperate biome. The hybrid formula is U. tenuicaulis × U. vulgaris.
