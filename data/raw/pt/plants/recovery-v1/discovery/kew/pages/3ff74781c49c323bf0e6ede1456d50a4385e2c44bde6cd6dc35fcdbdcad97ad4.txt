L46: 
L47: First published in Ann. Bot. (Rome) 1: 360 (1904)
L48: 
L49: This hybrid is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this hybrid is Medit. It is a tuberous geophyte and grows primarily in the subtropical biome. The hybrid formula is O. bombyliflora × O. tenthredinifera.
