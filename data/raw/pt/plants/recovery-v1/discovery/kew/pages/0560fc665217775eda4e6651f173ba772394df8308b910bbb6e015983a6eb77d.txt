L46: 
L47: First published in Fontqueria 2: 37 (1982)
L48: 
L49: This hybrid is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this hybrid is Spain. It is a bulbous geophyte and grows primarily in the temperate biome. The hybrid formula is N. bulbocodium var. minor × N. triandrus subsp. pallidulus.
