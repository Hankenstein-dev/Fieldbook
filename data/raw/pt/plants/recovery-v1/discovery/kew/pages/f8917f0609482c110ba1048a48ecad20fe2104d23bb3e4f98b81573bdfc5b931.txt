L46: 
L47: First published in Handbuch 1: 91 (1829)
L48: 
L49: This species is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this species is Macaronesia to SW. Italy and India, NE. & E. Tropical Africa. It is a perennial and grows primarily in the desert or dry shrubland biome.
