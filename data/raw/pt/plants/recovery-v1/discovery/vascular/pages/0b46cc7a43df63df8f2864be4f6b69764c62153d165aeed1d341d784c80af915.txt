L134: The cite70†Azores are a group of islands in the cite71†North Atlantic Ocean , west of the Iberian Peninsula. They are home to dozens of cite72†endemic species and subspecies of cite73†vascular plants .^{cite74†[1] } Although the Azores are politically part of cite75†Portugal , the cite76†World Geographical Scheme for Recording Plant Distributions treats the Azores as a distinct botanical country.
L135: The archipelago are part of cite77†Macaronesia , a biogeographical region which also includes the cite78†Canary Islands , cite79†Cape Verde Islands , cite80†Madeira , and the cite81†Selvagens .^{cite82†[2] }
L136: Plants are listed alphabetically by plant family. Extinct and presumed extinct species are indicated with †.
