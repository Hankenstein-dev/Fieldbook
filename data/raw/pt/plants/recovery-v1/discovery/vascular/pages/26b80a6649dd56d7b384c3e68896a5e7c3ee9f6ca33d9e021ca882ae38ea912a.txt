L20: # Morphology:
L21: Unicellular lunate desmids up to 660 µm long (Neindl and Url 1985) with two large chloroplasts, one per semicell. Lack an isthmus (found in many desmid genera) yet the overlap of the two hemicell walls is often evident, often by a change in color (older wall stained by iron oxide, etc.). Near each cell terminus is a transparent spherical or elongate vacuole containing from one to many barium sulfate granules (Brook et al. 1980) visible with light microscopy, that may act as gravity sensors.
L22: Some species "walk" by tumbling end over end across substrates.
