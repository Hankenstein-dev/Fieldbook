L57: ## Descriptions
L58: ### According to Flora of Somalia
L59: 
L60: M. Thulin et al. Flora of Somalia, Vol. 1-4 [updated 2008] https://plants.jstor.org/collection/FLOS
L61: Distribution
L62:     Some 70 species in temperate and subtropical Eurasia, extending into Arabia and northern and north-eastern Africa.
L63: Morphology General Habit
L64:     Perennial herbs to small shrubs
L65: Morphology Leaves
L66:     Leaves alternate, mostly simple and entire, more rarely 3(–5)-sect, petiolate or sessile, gland-dotted
L67: Morphology Reproductive morphology Flowers
L68:     Flowers in ± corymbose cymes, bisexual, 5-merous
L69: Morphology Reproductive morphology Flowers Calyx
L70:     Sepals free or ± united at the base
L71: Morphology Reproductive morphology Flowers Corolla
L72:     Petals white to yellow or red, entire
L73: Morphology Reproductive morphology Flowers Androecium Stamens
L74:     Stamens 10, shorter than petals, usually with ± bearded filaments
L75: Morphology Reproductive morphology Flowers Gynoecium Ovary
L76:     Ovary deeply 3–5-lobed, 3–5-celled, each cell with (1–)2–4(–9) ovules; style 1, almost gynobasic, with a ± capitate, very obscurely lobed, papillose stigma
L77: Morphology Reproductive morphology Fruits
L78:     Capsules subglobose to distinctly wider than long, deeply 3–5-lobed
L79: Morphology Reproductive morphology Seeds
L80:     Seeds reniform, strongly transversally or longitudinally rugose.
