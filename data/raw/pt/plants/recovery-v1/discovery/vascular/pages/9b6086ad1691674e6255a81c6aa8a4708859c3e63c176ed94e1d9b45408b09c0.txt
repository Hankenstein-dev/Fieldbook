L18: Description
L19: cite5†Rhizome short creeping, clothed with chaffy lanceolate scales, embeded in a nest of fronds and roots. Roots cite6†proliferous . Fronds cite7†dimorphic with sterile base fronds laminating against the host and sterile or fertilze foliage fronds emergent. Base fronds rounded-cite8†reniform , 15.5–36 cm long, at least 15–26 cm wide; upper margin cite9†entire to slightly wavy, firmly cite10†appressed to substrate, soon becoming brown.
L20: Foliage fronds ascending, 23–90 cm or more long, to 60 cm wide; base cite11†cuneate , spreading out to a broad apex; cite12†distal one-third forked 1–5 times; ultimate segments oblong, 1–6 cm wide, dark green, cite13†glabrous or almost so. Sporangia covering most of the undersurface of ultimate segments of foliage fronds, frequently reaching apex and sometimes extending around sinus of the ultimate fork. Spores 40–52.5 µm long, 20–35 µm wide.

L29: Natural history
L30: 
L31: The cite16†nest fronds of this species are thick and corky and contribute to a thick sponge-like nest. The upper margin of the nest-fronds are not lobed and seal the upper surface of the nest.
L32: 
L33: Cultivation
L34: Readily cultivated on a tree, rock, slab or in a pot of coarse material. Grows well in a garden or fernhouse in the tropics and subtropics. A usefull host for growing pendulous epiphytes.
