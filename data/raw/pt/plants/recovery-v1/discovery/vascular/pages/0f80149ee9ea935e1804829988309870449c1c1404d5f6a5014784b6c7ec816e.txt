L138: Porella is a large, common, and widespread cite59†genus of cite60†liverworts in cite61†order cite54†Porellales . It is a member of the cite62†family cite55†Porellaceae within that order.
L139: 
L140: There were 84 species recognized in 2016, most of them from East Asia.^{cite63†[2] } cite64†World Flora Online accepts 125 species,^{cite65†[3] } and cite66†GBIF accepts 150 species (as of 4 July 2023).^{cite67†[4] }
L141: The genus has a wide cite68†cosmopolitan distribution in temperate areas,^{cite67†[4] } where it is commonly found growing attached to the bark of trees. Members of the genus have lobed leaves with a large upper lobe and small lower lobe.^{cite69†[5] }
L142: 
L143: Species Porella arboris-vitae, Porella bolanderi,^{cite69†[5] } Porella cordeana, Porella navicularis, Porella pinnata,^{cite69†[5] } Porella platyphylla and Porella roellii, were all found in Montana, USA.^{cite70†[6] }
