L4@P0: Abstract
L5@P0: Nidorella ivifolia is a perennial shrub widely used as herbal medicine in southern Africa. This study is aimed at providing a
L6@P0: critical review of the botany, medicinal uses, phytochemistry and biological activities of N. ivifolia. Documented information
L7@P0: on the botany, medicinal uses, phytochemistry and biological activities of N. ivifolia was collected from several online
L8@P0: sources which included BMC, Scopus, SciFinder, Google Scholar, Science Direct, Elsevier, Pubmed and Web of Science.
L9@P0: Additional information on the botany, medicinal uses, phytochemistry and biological activities of N. ivifolia was gathered
L10@P0: from pre-electronic sources such as book chapters, books, journal articles and scientific publications sourced from the
L11@P0: University library. This study showed that the leaves and roots of N. ivifolia are used as herbal medicines for backache,
L12@P0: hyperventilation, sprains, swollen feet and hands, blood pressure, depression, pain, rheumatism, headache, pleuritic pain,
L13@P0: postnatal cleansing, diabetes, stomach problems, convulsions, inflammation, influenza, heart problems, fever and respiratory
L14@P0: problems. Several phytochemical compounds including diterpene acids, 5α-hydroxy,10β-hydroxyprintzianic acid,
L15@P0: conyscabraic acid, hautriwaic acid, nidoresedaic acid, printziaic acid, cardiac glycosides, saponins and tannins have been
L16@P0: identified from the leaves of N. ivifolia. Pharmacological research revealed that N. ivifolia crude extracts and essential oils
L17@P0: isolated from the species have antibacterial, antimycobacterial, antifungal, antiplasmodial, antiprotozoal and cytotoxicity
L18@P0: activities. Nidorella ivifolia should be subjected to further phytochemical, pharmacological and toxicological evaluations
L19@P0: aimed at correlating its medicinal uses with its phytochemistry and pharmacological activities.
L20@P0: Keywords: Asteraceae, Compositae, ethnopharmacology, herbal medicine, indigenous pharmacopeia, Nidorella ivifolia
L21@P0: INTRODUCTION
L22@P0: Nidorella ivifolia (L.) J.C. Manning & Goldblatt is a
L23@P0: perennial shrub belonging to the Asteraceae or
L24@P0: Compositae family. The family Asteraceae is an important
L25@P0: source of pharmaceutical drugs such as artemisinin,
L26@P0: developed from the leaves of Artemisia annua L.1-8
L27@P0: Asteraceae is one of the largest families of flowering
L28@P0: plants in the world, with about 1600 genera and 23,000
L29@P0: species found almost everywhere in the world except in
L30@P0: Antarctica.
L31@P0: 9 Several members of the family Asteraceae are
L32@P0: characterized by phytochemical compounds such as
L33@P0: acetophenones, caffeoylquinic acids, phloroglucinol,
L34@P0: polyphenols, pyrrolizidine alkaloids, polyacetylenes,
L35@P0: chalcone, flavonoids and diterpenoids.
L36@P0: 10,11 Several species
L37@P0: of the family Asteraceae are characterized by analgesic,
L38@P0: anti-allergic, antibacterial, antidiabetic, antifungal,
L39@P0: antiviral, anti-inflammatory, antimigraine, antioxidant,
L40@P0: antiproliferative, antipyretic, antitumor, antiulcer,
L41@P0: cardiotonic, and neuroprotective and neurotoxicity
L42@P0: activities.
L43@P0: 9-13 Nidorella ivifolia and other related species
L44@P0: such as N. anomala Steetz, N. auriculata DC., N.
L45@P0: microcephalla Steetz, N. pinnata (L.f) J.C. Manning &
L46@P0: Goldblatt and N. resedifolia DC. are used as herbal
L47@P0: medicines in tropical Africa.
L48@P0: 14-16 The leaves of N. ivifolia
L49@P0: are cooked as leafy vegetables in Namibia.
L50@P0: 17,18 Leaves and
L51@P0: stems of N. ivifolia are sold as herbal medicines in the
L52@P0: informal herbal medicines markets in the Eastern Cape
L53@P0: province in South Africa.
L54@P0: 19 Nidorella ivifolia is one of the
L55@P0: important medicinal plants in South Africa and the species
L56@P0: is included in the book “medicinal plants of South Africa”,
L57@P0: a photographic guide to the most commonly used plant
L58@P0: medicines in the country, including their botany, main
L59@P0: traditional uses and active ingredients.20 Research by Van
L60@P0: Wyk21 showed that the leaves of N. ivifolia have
L61@P0: commercial potential as herbal medicines for
L62@P0: inflammation, colds and fever in South Africa. It is within
L63@P0: this context that this review was undertaken aimed at
L64@P0: reviewing the botany, medicinal uses, phytochemical and
L65@P0: biological activities of N. ivifolia so as to provide the
L66@P0: baseline data required in evaluating the therapeutic
L67@P0: potential of the species.
L68@P0: Botanical profile of Nidorella ivifolia
L69@P0: The genus Nidorella Cass. comprises about 15 species that
L70@P0: are restricted in distribution to eastern and southern
L71@P0: Africa.
L72@P0: 22-30 Both morphological and molecular data
L73@P0: showed that species of Nidorella Cass. and those of
L74@P0: Conyza Less. are closely related.
L75@P0: 23,27,31,32 Synonyms
L76@P0: associated with N. ivifolia include Baccharis halimifolia
L77@P0: Moench, B. ivaefolia L., Conyza ivifolia (L.) Less., C.
L78@P0: scabrida DC., Erigeron dentatus Burm.f., E. ivifolius Sch.
L79@P0: Bip., Fimbrillaria baccharoides Cass., Marsea ivifolia
L80@P0: Kuntze and Pluchea scabrida DC.
L81@P0: 23-26,29,30 Nidorella
L82@P0: ivifolia is an erect, multi-stemmed, slender shrub with
L83@P0: willowy branches up to 2 metres in height,
L84@P0: 30 with minutely
L85@P0: hairy stems bearing aromatic leaves.
L86@P0: 18,33 Leaves are
L87@P0: petiolate, elliptic to lanceolate in shape, three-nerved from
L88@P0: the base and leaf margins that are sharply serrate. The
L89@P0: flower heads are inconspicuous, disciform, borne in flattopped clusters, in dense terminal corymbs which are
L90@P0: cream-coloured to pale yellow in colour. The fruits of N.
L91@P0: ivifolia are small nutlets that are dispersed by means of
L92@P0: bristly seed hairs. The species has been recorded in
L93@P0: Botswana, Lesotho, Mozambique, Namibia, South Africa,
L94@P0: Swaziland and Zimbabwe.
L95@P0: 23,25,26,28-30 Nidorella ivifolia has
L96@P0: been recorded near streams and in sandstone slopes,
L97@P0: streambeds or forest margins at an altitude ranging from 5
L98@P0: m to 1920 m above sea level.

L332@P3: CONCLUSION
L333@P3: The present review summarizes the botany, medicinal
L334@P3: uses, phytochemistry and pharmacological properties of N.
L335@P3: ivifolia. Based on presented information, there is not yet
L336@P3: enough data correlating the ethnomedicinal uses of the
L337@P3: species with its phytochemical and pharmacological
L338@P3: properties. Detailed studies on the pharmacokinetics, in
L339@P3: vivo and clinical research involving both extracts and
L340@P3: compounds isolated from the species are required.
L341@P3: Therefore, future research should focus on the molecular
L342@P3: modes or mechanisms of action, pharmacokinetics and
L343@P3: physiological pathways for specific extracts of the species
L344@P3: including identification of the bioactive compounds of the
L345@P3: species and their associated pharmacological activities.
