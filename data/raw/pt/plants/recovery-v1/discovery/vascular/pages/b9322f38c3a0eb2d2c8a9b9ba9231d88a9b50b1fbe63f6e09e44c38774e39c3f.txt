L84: # Delosperma testaceum (Orange Sheepfig)
L85: 
L86: Advertisements
L87: 
L88: Delosperma testaceum is a beautiful small plant with vibrant, daisy-like flowers and gray-green succulent leaves. This unique cite39†species is not commonly found in cultivation, making it a prized addition for collectors looking to enhance their collection with rare and captivating succulents.

L108: #### Etymology
L109: 
L110: The specific epithet "cite42†testaceum " (pronounced "test-uh-SEE-um") means "made of bricks or tiles; resembling brick (especially color); covered with a shell" and refers to the color of the flowers of this species.
L111: #### Origin
L112: 
L113: Delosperma testaceum is native to cite43†South Africa . It grows in flats and hills of karoo sediments in the Upper Karoo, Dry Highveld Grassland, Albany Thickets, Lower Karoo, Rainshadow Valley Karoo, and eastern Fynbos-Renosterveld biome.
L114: #### Description
L115: 
L116: Delosperma testaceum is a dwarf succulent cite44†shrublet with erect branches and relatively narrow leaves with recurved tips. It can grow up to 24 inches (60 cm) tall, and all plant parts have a velvety texture. The leaves are gray-green, thick, fleshy, rounded on the lower surface, and flat on the upper surface.
L117: From spring to late summer, Delosperma testaceum produces orange flowers that gradually turn brick red as they age. The flowers appear in rich cite45†cymes and can reach a diameter of about 0.8 inches (2 cm). The fruits are pale brown capsules that have five cite46†locules .

L122: #### How to Grow and Care for Delosperma testaceum
L123: 
L124: Light: For optimal growth, this plant requires bright, direct sunlight. Ensure it receives at least six hours of direct sunlight each day.
L125: 
L126: Soil: Good drainage is essential to the survival of Delosperma testaceum. Sandy and gravelly cite49†soils are ideal for this plant.
L127: Temperature: Although it can tolerate a wide range of temperatures, it prefers warm, dry climates. It grows best in cite50†USDA Plant Hardiness Zones 5a to 9b, with average minimum winter temperatures ranging from -20 to 30 °F (-28.9 to -1.1 °C).
L128: 
L129: Watering: Once established, cite51†water your Delosperma testaceum sparingly during the growing season. It can tolerate several months of drought. Keep it dry in the winter, and only water it if the leaves start to shrivel.
L130: Fertilizing: This plant can thrive without any feeding. However, if grown in a container, it may require cite52†fertilizing , as nutrients in potting soil deplete more quickly than in garden soil.
L131: 
L132: Repotting: Repot every three years or when the plant outgrows its current pot. Do this in the spring using a larger pot with drainage holes.
L133: Propagation: Delosperma testaceum can be propagated by division, cuttings, or seeds. If cite53†propagating by division, do so in the spring. Cuttings can be taken any time during the growing season, while spring is ideal for sowing seeds.

L136: #### Toxicity of Delosperma testaceum
L137: 
L138: Delosperma testaceum is non-toxic, making it safe around children and pets.
