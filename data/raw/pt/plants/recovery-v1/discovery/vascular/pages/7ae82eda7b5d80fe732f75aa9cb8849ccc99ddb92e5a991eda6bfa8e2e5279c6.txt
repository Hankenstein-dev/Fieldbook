L68: ### Description and Ethnobotany
L69: 
L70: Growth Form  | A slender woody shrub, it sometimes has a scandent growth habit and can grow up to 1m in height.
L71: Foliage  | The simple, elongate-triangular leaves have a serrate leaf margin and a shallowly cordate leaf base. The leaves are minutely pubescent.
L72: Flowers  | The flowers are pendulous and hang downwards. The flower calyx is red and stellate-tomentose in texture while the flower petals are yellow and are velutinose-pillose in texture.
