L32@P2: W.-L. Chew, P.G. Kodela
L33@P2: Common Name: Native Ramie.
L34@P2: Biostatus: Native.
L35@P2: Habitat: Grows along creeks in rainforest, often in open rocky places.
L36@P2: Representative Herbarium Specimens: Qld: Mount Roberts, S.T. Blake 21512 (BRI, NSW); Curtis Fall, Jan. 1916,
L37@P2: C.T. White s.n. (BRI); Mount Clunie, R. Melville 3628 (NSW).
L38@P2: N.S.W.: Mount Gipps, Wiangarie State Forest, R.G. Coveny 10570 (NSW); Mount Warning, SW of Murwillumbah, S.
L39@P2: Clarke 1583 et al. (NSW).
L40@P2: Monoecious shrub to 2 m high [dioecious, fide Harden 1990: 350], strigose throughout, stinging hairs absent. Leaves
L41@P2: opposite, one of each pair smaller; lamina ovate to lanceolate, serrate, acuminate to attenuate apex, broadly
L42@P2: cuneate to rounded at base, (4–) 6–22 cm long, (2–) 5–8 cm wide, thin, upper surface ± scabrous to ± glabrous,
L43@P2: lower surface sparsely hairy, prominently 3-veined from base, lateral veins 4 or 5 pairs, the basal pair often reaching
L44@P2: nearly to apex, the others short; petiole 1–8 cm long; stipules 6–10 mm long, in groups enclosing the terminal buds.
L45@P2: Inflorescence a unisexual or bisexual, interrupted spike 5–25 cm long, pendulous, single or paired; flowers spirally
L46@P2: glomerulate, bracteate. Male flowers mainly at base of spike, subsessile, c. 1 mm long. Female flowers sessile, 1–2
L47@P2: mm long; orifice 2-dentate, attenuate; stigma c. 2 mm long. Achenes ovoid, slightly compressed, c. 2 mm long, 1-
L48@P2: seeded, enclosed in perianth.
L49@P2: Distribution: Occurs in the mountains of southeastern
L50@P2: Queensland and northeastern New South Wales (north
L51@P2: from Dorrigo area), particularly on the ranges of the
L52@P2: Tweed caldera; also in Africa through Asia to Pacific
L53@P2: islands.

L55@P3: Taxonomic Notes: Boehmeria macrophylla is found widely from Africa through Asia to the Pacific. It is highly
L56@P3: variable; numerous varieties have been established for the species (Weddell 1869: 210).
