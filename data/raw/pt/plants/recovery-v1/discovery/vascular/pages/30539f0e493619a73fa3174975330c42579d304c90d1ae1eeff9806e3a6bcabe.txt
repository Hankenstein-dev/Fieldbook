Centaurus, -i m. = centaur, a word designating a race of mythological beings, half man and half horse, that inhabited the mountains of Thessaly and among whom Chiron stood out, wise and prudent, a renowned physician and tutor of many mythological heroes, such as Heracles (Hercules)-.
