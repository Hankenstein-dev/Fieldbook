L127: ## ID Characteristics
L128: 
L129:   * cite4†Field Identification Characteristics L130: 
L131:     * The ligule is small with long hairs behind and at the margins
L132:     * Roots at the nodes and spreads
L133:     * Panicle of 6-20 spicate branches, partly in vericels
L134: 
L135:   * cite4†Leaf and Stem Characteristics L136:     * Sheath: Keeled, without hair
L137:     * Blade: Folded, keeled especially toward the base, without hair to rough (scabrous)
L138:     * Ligule: Ciliate membrane, truncate, with long hairs behind and at the margins
L139:     * Culm: Decumbent and geniculate below, erect above, flattened, solid, often rooting at the lower nodes
L140: 
L141:   * cite4†Floral Characteristics L142:     * Inflorescence: Panicle
L143:     * Spikelet: Florets 2; fertile lemma keeled
L144:     * Awns: Fertile lemma awned from below the tip, rudiment lemma awned
L145:     * Glumes: Unequal, first shorter than the second glume
