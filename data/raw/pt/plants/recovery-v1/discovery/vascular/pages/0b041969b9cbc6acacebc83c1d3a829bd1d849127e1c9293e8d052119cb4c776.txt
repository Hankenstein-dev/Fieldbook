L172: Alyssum montanum is a species of cite62†flowering plant belonging to the family cite66†Brassicaceae .^{cite72†[2] } It is an evergreen, prostrate perennial with small, hairy, greyish cite73†leaves .^{cite74†[3] } It typically grows to 10-15 centimeters tall and 30-45 centimeters across.^{cite75†[4] } It produces tiny, yellow, fragrant cite76†flowers from May to July.^{cite77†[5] } These flowers are typically 4-6 centimeters across and borne in dense cite78†racemes .^{cite79†[6] }
L173: It is native to cite80†France , cite81†Switzerland , and cite82†Germany .^{cite72†[2] } It grows best in full sun with good drainage, and is cite83†drought tolerant once established.^{cite75†[4] } It is suitable for cite84†USDA hardiness zones 3–9, but may be susceptible to cite85†aphids and cite86†root rot .^{cite87†[7] }
