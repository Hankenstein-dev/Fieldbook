L122: Oeosporangium pteridioides (with many cite43†synonyms , including Hemionitis pteridioides)[ 1 ] is a cite44†species of cite38†fern in the family cite40†Pteridaceae .[ 2 ] Its native distribution is cite45†Macaronesia , around the cite46†Mediterranean in Europe, North Africa and Western Asia, and into the cite47†Sahara .[ 1 ]
