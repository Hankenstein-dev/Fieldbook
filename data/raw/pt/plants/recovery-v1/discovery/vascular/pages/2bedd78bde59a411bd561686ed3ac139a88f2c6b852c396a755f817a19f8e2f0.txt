L169: Ononis is a cite92†genus of 80–90 species of cite93†perennial cite94†herbs and subshrubs in the cite95†legume family cite85†Fabaceae . The members of this genus are called restharrows;^{cite96†[3] } an old spelling "wrestharrows" is also recorded from 1965.^{cite97†[4] } as some species grow as weeds on arable lands where their tough roots would catch and stop the cite98†harrow . The genus is native to Europe, northern and eastern Africa, and western and central Asia.^{cite99†[5] }
L170: In cite100†herbalism restharrow is used to treat bladder and kidney problems and water retention.
L171: 
L172: The cite101†active ingredients in restharrow are cite102†essential oils , cite103†flavonoid -cite104†glycosides , and cite105†tannins .
L173: 
L174: Restharrows are used as food plants by the cite106†larvae of some cite107†Lepidoptera species including the cite108†grey pug and cite109†Coleophora ononidella (which feeds exclusively on O. arvensis).
