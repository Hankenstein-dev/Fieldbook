L66: cite32†Add To My Plants cite33†List for Sale or Trade cite34†Write an Article L67: ORIGIN: Found in Jalisco and Michoacan ststes of Mexico on Pacific facing slopes at elevations of 1600 to 2500 meters.
L68: DESCRIPTION: Medium sized, cool to cold growing epiphyte on oaks with subglobose, compressed, obtuse pseudobulbs enveloped basally by leaf-bearing sheaths and carrying 2 to 3 apical, linear-oblong or lanceolate, coriaceous, acute leaves and blooms in the fall and winter on a stout, usually erect, loosely branched, to 3' [90 cm] long, multi-flowered panicle with long-lasting flowers.
L69: FLOWER SIZE: 3 inches [7.5 cm] -- information provided by Jay Pfahl, author of the Internet Orchid Species Encyclopedia (IOSPE).
