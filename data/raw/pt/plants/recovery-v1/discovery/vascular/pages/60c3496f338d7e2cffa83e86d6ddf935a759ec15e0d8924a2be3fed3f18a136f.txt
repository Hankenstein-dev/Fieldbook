L145: ## Appearance
L146: 
L147: [cite63†edit ]
L148: 
L149: Herb 1–2 m, stem a little grooved, nodes jointed. Leaves opp, 14 x 7 cm, edge smooth. Flowers minute, greenish-white, panicle 40 cm long, male more open, female compact, 1 mm. Seed 0.5 mm.[ 5 ]
