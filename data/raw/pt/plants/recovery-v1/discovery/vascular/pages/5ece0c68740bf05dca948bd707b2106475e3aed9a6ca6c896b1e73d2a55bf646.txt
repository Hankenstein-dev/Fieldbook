L6@P0: (up to about 2 mm wide and 2.5 mm long) that contrast with very small, flat lobules
L7@P0: and underleaves. Growth form and habitat are strong pointers to identification:
L8@P0: P. pinnata floats like a fan on the downstream side of rocks and tree roots in rivers.

L10@P0: further east in Britain than the core range of P. pinnata. Both have lobules with upcurved margins and bases that run down onto the stem. Chiloscyphus polyanthos
L11@P0: (p. 187) shares the habitat of P. pinnata and has similarly sized leaves, although it
L12@P0: tends to be slightly lighter green. However, C. polyanthos looks very different
