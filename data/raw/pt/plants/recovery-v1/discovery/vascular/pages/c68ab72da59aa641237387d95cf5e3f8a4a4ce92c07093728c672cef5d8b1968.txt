L196: ##### Botanical details
L197: 
L198: Family
L199:     Iridaceae
L200: Native to GB / Ireland
L201:     No
L202: Genus
L203: 
L204: Watsonia
L205: 
L206: Genus description
L207: 
L208: Watsonia are cormous perennials with erect, linear to narrowly lance-shaped leaves and curved, tubular flowers with spreading lobes, borne in simple or branched spikes
