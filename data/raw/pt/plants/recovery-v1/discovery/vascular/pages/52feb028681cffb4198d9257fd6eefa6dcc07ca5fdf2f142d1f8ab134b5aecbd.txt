L163: ## How to Grow
L164: 
L165: ### Cultivation
L166: 
L167: Grow in fertile, humus-rich, moist but well-drained soil, in full sun. Pinch out growing tips to encourage bushy plants and stake taller dahlias, see cite10†staking perennials . Water in dry periods if needed. cite11†lift and store tubers in autumn to replant, or use as a source of cuttings, in spring. See cite12†dahlia cultivation , and our video cite13†how to plant dahlia tubers and care tips†youtu.be L168: ### Propagation
L169: 
L170: Propagate by cite14†softwood cuttings taken in spring from shoots from stored tubers, or cite15†Divide the tubers ensuring each cite15†division has a viable bud
