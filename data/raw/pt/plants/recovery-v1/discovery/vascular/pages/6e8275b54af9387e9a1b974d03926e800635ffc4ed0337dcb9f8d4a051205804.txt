G. H. Loos subsp. paniculata (syn. Festuca paniculata (L.) Schinz & Thell. subsp. paniculata)
L13: 
L14: cite6†Trajche Dimitrovski cite7†May 24, 2023December 16, 2025 cite8†Plant families , cite9†Poaceae L15: 
L16: Taxonomy: Fam. Poaceae// Genus: Patzkea// Species: Patzkea paniculata (L.) G. H. Loos// Subspecies: Patzkea paniculata (L.) G. H. Loos subsp. paniculata.
L17: Determined according to the botanical key provided by Markgraf-Dannenberg in Tutin et al. (1980) as Festuca paniculata (L.) Schinz & Thell. subsp. paniculata. The accepted name in The Euro+Med PlantBase (Valdés & Scholz; with contributions from Raab-Straube & Parolly) is Patzkea paniculata (L.) G. H. Loos subsp. paniculata.
L18: 
L19: Lifeform: Caespitose perennial; hemicryptophyte according to the Raunkiær system.
L20: Distribution: Native from South Europe to South Central and Southeast Europe according to the distribution information provided by the Plants of the World Online portal (Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew).
L21: 
L22: Habitat: Rocky terrains.
L23: 
L24: Flowering period: Late spring – early summer: MAY, JUN.
