L77: cite18†View picture†cdn.auckland.ac.nz L78: Plagiochasma rupestre has a distinctive bluish-green, branching thallus with purplish margins. The dorsal surface contains minute epidermal pores which are bounded by a ring of 4-5 cells. The ventral surface has two types of rhizoids as well as unusually well developed, deep crimson scales which exhibit a considerable variety in form and structure.  Female and male gametangia are borne in receptacles on the  dorsal thallus surface.
L79: Widespread in New Zealand in on different soils, rock and stonework in exposed to shaded locations.
