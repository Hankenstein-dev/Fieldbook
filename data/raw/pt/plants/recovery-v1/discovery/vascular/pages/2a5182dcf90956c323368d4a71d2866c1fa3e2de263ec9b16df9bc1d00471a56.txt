L54:   * Evergreen shrub, 3 ft × 5 ft, upright then a sprawling habit, stems and new foliage are plum colored.  Leaves opposite, simple, dark green when mature, wavy margin.  Flowers are silvery pink, crepe-paperlike petals, 6.5 cm wide, blooms May into September.
