L66: cite27†[AERP] L67: ### According to Flora Zambesiaca
L68: 
L69: Bignoniaceae, M. A. Diniz. Flora Zambesiaca 8:3. 1988
L70: Morphology General Habit
L71:     Scandent shrubs up to 10 m. high or subshrubs.
L72: Morphology Branches
L73:     Branches 4-angled, grooved minutely lepidote, pubescent only at the nodes.
L74: Morphology Leaves
L75:     Leaves imparipinnate, 13–27 cm. long, 4–7-jugate; petiole 2–6 cm. long; leaflet lamina 3.5–9 x 1.3–2.6 cm., narrowly ovate to ovate-lanceolate, longly acuminate at the apex, rounded to somewhat asymmetric at the base, grass green, inferior surface slightly lighter and glandular, margins entire or sometimes serrate-crenate uni- or bilaterally along the distal half; petiolules up to 1.5 cm. long in proximal pairs, decreasing progressively upwards.
L76: Morphology Reproductive morphology Inflorescences
L77:     Inflorescence a few- to many-flowered terminal panicle, up to 40 mcm. long, glabrous, branches opposite and decussate; pedicels 1–2 cm. long, articulated c. 3.5 mm. from the top, upper articulate, falling off with the flower, the lower persistent and 2-bracteate towards the base.
L78: Morphology Reproductive morphology Flowers Calyx
L79:     Calyx whitish, campanulate, 5-ribbed, inflated, 5-lobed, lobes triangular-oblong, 0.6–1.2 x 0.4–0.7 cm., apiculate at the apex and revolute, tube 0.7–1.5 cm. long, glabrous outside and minutely glandular inside.
L80: Morphology Reproductive morphology Flowers Corolla
L81:     Corolla campanulate, mauvish pink, throat brighter and carmine streaked inside; corolla tube 3.5–5 cm. long, cylindrical at the base c. 1 cm. long suddenly campanulate near the top, glabrous outside and villous inside, limb 5-lobed, bilabiate, lobes 1.3–2.8 cm. diam., almost circular, margin ciliate and pilose at the sinus.
L82: Morphology Reproductive morphology Flowers Androecium Stamens
L83:     Stamens didynamous, included, with the staminode between the shorter pair; filaments adnate up to 1 cm. from the base of the corolla tube, densely pilose at the insertion points; anther-thecae divergent when mature, the connective apiculated and dorsaly crested.
L84: Morphology Reproductive morphology Flowers Disc
L85:     Disk c. 1 mm. long, annular-cupular.
L86: Morphology Reproductive morphology Flowers Gynoecium Ovary
L87:     Ovary c. 6 mm. long.
L88: Morphology Reproductive morphology Fruits
L89:     Capsule 35–48 x 1.5 cm., apex apiculate.
L90: Morphology Reproductive morphology Seeds
L91:     Seeds 0.6–1 x 3 cm. including the wings.
