L14: 
L15: Stinking Viburnum is a white flowering evergreen to semi-evergreen shrub native to Tibet and Indo-China.  It reaches a size of around 10 feet tall and wide.  The common name comes from the unpleasant smell emitted by the leaves when they are crushed. In late spring to early summer clusters of white flowers appear and are followed by red berries that can be eaten raw. The leaves can be variable on the same plant.
L16: Plant in moist well-drained soil in full sun to partial shade. Use as a foundation plant, part of a hedge or shrub border or a specimen.
