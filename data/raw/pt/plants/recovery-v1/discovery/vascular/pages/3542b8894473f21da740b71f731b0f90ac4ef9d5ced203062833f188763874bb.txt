L135:   * Bielzia Schur
L136: Rhaponticoides is a genus of flowering plants in the family cite54†Asteraceae , found in northern Africa, southern and eastern Europe, and western Asia as far east as Mongolia. They were resurrected from cite61†Centaurea .^{cite62†[3] }
L137: In the 20th century the genus cite61†Centaurea was cite63†paraphyletic , because it was based on a cite64†type species , cite65†C. centaurium , which was less related to the vast majority of other Centaurea than to species which were classified as belonging to other genera.
L138: In 2001 cite66†Werner Greuter solved this by moving the C. centaurium and the related species in the former cite67†subgenus Centaurea to an old, resurrected genus: Rhaponticoides, he cite68†conserved the name Centaurea for the majority of the other species, and electing cite69†C. paniculata to serve as the new type species.^{cite70†[4] }^{cite71†[5] }^{cite72†[6] }
