L13: Tufted perennial, flowering stems to c. 40 cm high, generally exceeding leaves. Leaves flat, mostly 2–6 mm wide, flaccid, ciliate along margins, obtuse and thickened at apex. Inflorescence umbellate, with 1 or 2 sessile clusters and up to c. 6 stalked clusters, each 4–10 mm diam., rarely reduced to a single cluster; tepals acuminate, 2–3 mm long, light to dark brown with pale membranous margins; capsules subequal to or slightly longer than tepals; seeds c.
L14: 1 mm long excluding caruncle which is from one-third to one-half as long as seed.
L15: LoM, MuM, Wim, GleP, Brid, VVP, VRiv, MuF, GipP, OtP, WaP, Gold, CVU, GGr, DunT, NIS, EGL, EGU, WPro, HSF, HNF, OtR, Strz, MonT, HFE, VAlp.
L16: 
L17: A variable species with 3 varieties, sometimes treated as species. Specimens with intermediate attributes are not uncommon.
