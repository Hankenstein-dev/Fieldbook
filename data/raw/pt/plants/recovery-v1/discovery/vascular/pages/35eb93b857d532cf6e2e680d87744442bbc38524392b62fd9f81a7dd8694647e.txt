L20: Taxonomy: Fam. Polygonaceae// Genus: Rumex// Species: Rumex tuberosus L.
L21: 
L22: Determined according to the botanical keys provided by Rechinger in Tutin et al. (1964) and Micevski (1995).
L23: 
L24: Distribution: Native from the Mediterranean to South European Russia according to the distribution information provided by the Plants of the World Online portal (Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew).
L25: Habitat: Open habitats- grasslands/ pastures, edges and clearings of oak forest; calcicole.
L26: 
L27: Lifeform: Perennial; hemicryptophyte according to the Raunkiær system.
L28: 
L29: Flowering period: Spring- early summer: APR, MAY, JUN.
