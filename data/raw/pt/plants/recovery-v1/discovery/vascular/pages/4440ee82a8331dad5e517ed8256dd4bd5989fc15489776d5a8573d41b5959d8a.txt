L3: ## Bornetia secundiflora (J Agardh) Thuret
L4: 
L5: * * *
L6: 
L7: Description: Thallus monosiphonous with erect cylindrical fronds to 19 cms long. Branching irregularly alternate and at very narrow angles, each branch with a rounded apex. Cells large visible to the naked eye
L8: 
L9: Habitat: Very rare. Epilithic in the sublittoral to 3 m depth.
L10: Distribution: A southern species recorded from England in Devon and Cornwall also Scilly Isles and the Channel Isles. Europe: the Mediterranean, Portugal, Atlantic coasts of Spain and France. Further afield: Canary Islands.
L11: 
L12: Similar Species: Griffithsia and Halurus.
