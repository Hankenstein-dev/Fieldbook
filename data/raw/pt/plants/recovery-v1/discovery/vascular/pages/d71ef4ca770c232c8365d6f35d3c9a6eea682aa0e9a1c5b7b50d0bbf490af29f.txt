L126: Beccariella Pierre (1872)
L127: Pleioluma is a genus of cite43†flowering plants in the family cite47†Sapotaceae . It includes 40 species of evergreen hermaphroditic or gynodioecious trees, reaching up to 25 meters tall.^{cite50†[1] }
L128: The genus ranges from the cite52†Nicobar Islands through cite53†Thailand , cite54†Malaysia , cite55†Indonesia , and the cite56†Philippines to cite57†New Guinea , the cite58†Bismarck Archipelago , cite59†Solomon Islands , cite60†New Caledonia , and cite61†Australia (cite62†Queensland , cite63†Northern Territory , and cite64†New South Wales ). At least 15 species are native to New Guinea.^{cite50†[1] }
L129: The genus was described in 1961 by cite65†Charles Baehni . In 2013 Swenson et al. enlarged the genus to include the genus Beccariella and some species formerly classed in cite66†Planchonella and cite67†Pouteria , based on a phylogenetic analysis.^{cite68†[2] }
