L79: ## 形態特徵
L80:   * Large shrubs or small trees. Branches light to dark brown, branchlets puberulous or glabrous.
L81:   * Leaf blades oblong-lanceolate, 6-13 cm long, 1.4-2.5 (3.3) cm wide, apex acuminate, base obtuse, often oblique, entire or sometimes sparsely serrulate, lateral veins 11-15 (17) pairs, veinlets branch-like; immature leaf blades densely fulvo-pubescent; mature leaf blades glabrous or nearly so above, densely fulvo-pubescent on lower surface; stipules minute, ovate-rhomboid, ca. 0.5 mm long.
L82:   * Aments coetaneous, subsessile, peduncle with deciduous leafy bract at base; male aments slender, 3.5-5 cm long, pilose; stamens 2, filaments 2.5-4.5 mm long, pilose at base, anthers elliptic, 0.8-1 mm long, 0.6-0.9 mm wide, pollen sacs 2, unequal at base; nectary 1, square; bracts oblong, 1-2 mm long, 0.7-1.2 mm wide, pilose or only margin pilose; pistillate ament 2-5 cm long, to 8 cm in fruit, loosely flowered; pistils glabrous, long ovate, lanceolate, 1.8-2.8 mm long, 5 mm long in fruit, stipe as long as ovary, styles shorter than stigma lobes, stigma double bifid, stipe 1-2 mm long, pilose, as long as bract; nectary and bract as in staminate flowers; ovules 6-10 per ovary.
L83:   * Capsules linear, 5-6 mm long, spliting along suture from tip.Seeds green, 1-2 mm long, comal.
L84: ## 分布
L85: 
L86:   * 台灣 : In open areas in mountains, 1,600-3,200 m throughout the island, common.
