L137: Oloptum miliaceum^{cite63†[1] } is a species of cite55†grass known by the common name smilograss.^{cite64†[2] } It is native to Eurasia but it can be found in many other parts of the world as an cite65†introduced species and a casual cite66†weed of disturbed areas. It is a clumping perennial grass producing sturdy, erect stems that can reach 1.5 meters tall. The cite67†inflorescence is a cite68†panicle of several whorls of branches that divide into secondary branches bearing clusters of spikelets.
