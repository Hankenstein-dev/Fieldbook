L50: 
L51: First published in Handbuch 1: 91 (1829)
L52: 
L53: This species is accepted
L54: 
L55: The native range of this species is Macaronesia to SW. Italy and India, NE. & E. Tropical Africa. It is a perennial and grows primarily in the desert or dry shrubland biome.

L76: cite28† [AERP]  L77: ###  According to GrassBase - The Online World Grass Flora
L78: Morphology General Habit
L79:      Perennial; caespitose; clumped densely. Rootstock evident. Culms geniculately ascending; 10-90 cm long; wiry. Culm-internodes distally glabrous, or pilose. Culm-nodes pubescent. Leaf-sheaths glabrous on surface, or pilose. Ligule a fringe of hairs; 0.4-1 mm long. Leaf-blades flat, or involute; 2-15 cm long; 1-5 mm wide. Leaf-blade surface glabrous, or pubescent; hairy adaxially.
L80: Morphology Reproductive morphology Inflorescences
L81:      Inflorescence a panicle. Panicle open; oblong; dense; 3-15 cm long. Spikelets solitary. Fertile spikelets pedicelled.
L82: Morphology Reproductive morphology Inflorescences Spikelets
L83:      Spikelets comprising 1 basal sterile florets; 1 fertile florets; without rhachilla extension. Spikelets oblong; laterally compressed; 2.5-3.5 mm long; with hairs extending (0.9-)1.3-7 mm beyond apex; falling entire; readily shedding fertile florets. Rhachilla internodes elongated between glumes.
L84: Fertile
L85:      Spikelets comprising 1 basal sterile florets; 1 fertile florets; without rhachilla extension. Spikelets oblong; laterally compressed; 2.5-3.5 mm long; with hairs extending (0.9-)1.3-7 mm beyond apex; falling entire; readily shedding fertile florets. Rhachilla internodes elongated between glumes.
L86: Morphology Reproductive morphology Inflorescences Bracts Glume
L87:      Glumes dissimilar; reaching apex of florets; thinner than fertile lemma. Lower glume oblate; 0.1-0.75(-1) mm long; 0.05-0.3 length of spikelet; hyaline; without keels; 0-1 -veined. Lower glume lateral veins absent. Lower glume surface pilose. Lower glume apex truncate. Upper glume ovate; 1 length of spikelet; membranous; without keels; (3-)5 -veined. Upper glume surface pilose; hairy generally, or below; with tubercle-based hairs. Upper glume hairs white. Upper glume apex acute; mucronate.
L88: Morphology Reproductive morphology Flowers Florets
L89:      Basal sterile florets male; with palea. Lemma of lower sterile floret similar to upper glume; ovate; 1 length of spikelet; membranous; 5(-7) -veined; pilose; hairy all along, or below; with tubercle-based hairs; bearing white hairs; acute; mucronate. Fertile lemma elliptic; dorsally compressed; 2-3 mm long; cartilaginous; pallid; shiny; without keel; 3-5 -veined. Lemma margins flat. Lemma apex acute. Palea cartilaginous; 2 -veined; without keels.
L90: Morphology Reproductive morphology Flowers
L91:      Anthers 3. Stigmas terminally exserted.
L92: Distribution
L93:      Europe: southeastern. Africa: north, Macaronesia, northeast tropical, and east tropical. Asia-temperate: western Asia and Arabia. Asia-tropical: India.
L94: Reference
L95:      Paniceae. FTEA.
L96: cite29† [GB]  L97: ###  According to Flora of Tropical East Africa
L98: 
L99: Gramineae, W. D. Clayton, S. M. Phillips & S. A. Renvoize. Flora of Tropical East Africa. 1974
L100: Morphology General Habit
L101:      Perennial, forming tussocks from a woody rootstock; culms 10–60 cm. high, ascending, wiry with narrow leaves or herbaceous and leafy.
L102: Morphology Leaves
L103:      Leaf-blades flat or involute, 2–15 cm. long, 1–5 mm. wide.
L104: Morphology Reproductive morphology Inflorescences
L105:      Panicle usually narrowly oblong, 3–15 cm. long, fairly dense.
L106: Morphology Reproductive morphology Inflorescences Spikelets
L107:      Spikelets 2.5–3.5 mm. long; lower glume a tiny truncate scale (rarely up to 0.5 mm.); upper glume ovate, tuberculate-pilose with white hairs extending 0.5–4 mm. beyond the tip but often giabrous on the upper 1/4, acute and usually mucronate; lower lemma resembling the upper glume.
L108: Habitat
L109:      Dry stony hills, often in crevices between boulders; 200–2800 m.
L110: Distribution
L111:      K1 K2 K3 K4 K6 K7 T1 T2 T3 U1 extends northwards to the Mediterraneanthence westwards to Canary and Cape Verde Is., and eastwards to India
L112: cite30† [FTEA]  L113: ###  According to Flora of Somalia
L114: 
L115: M. Thulin. Flora of Somalia, Vol. 1–4 [updated 2008] https://plants.jstor.org/collection/FLOS
L116: Distribution
L117:      East tropical Africa northwards to the Mediterranean region, thence westwards to Macaronesia and eastwards to India.
L118: Morphology General Habit
L119:      Perennial, forming tussocks from a woody rootstock, up to 60 cm high, ascending, the stems wiry with narrow leaves or herbaceous and leafy; leaves flat or inrolled, glabrous or thinly pubescent
L120: Morphology Reproductive morphology Inflorescences
L121:      Panicle usually narrowly oblong, 3–15 cm long, fairly dense, fully exserted
L122: Morphology Reproductive morphology Inflorescences Spikelets
L123:      Spikelets 2.5–3.5 mm long; lower glume a minute truncate scale (rarely up to 0.5 mm); upper glume ovate, tuberculate-pilose with white hairs extending 0.5–4 mm beyond the tip, but often glabrous in the upper 1/4, acute and usually mucronate.
