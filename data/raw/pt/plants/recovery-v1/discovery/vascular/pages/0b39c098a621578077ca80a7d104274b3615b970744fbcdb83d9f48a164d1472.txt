L11@P0: Abstract
L12@P0: Briškaitė R., Patova E., Juzėnas S., 2016: Desmid flora in the lakes of the Khrebtovyi Nature Reserve in the Polar Ural (Russia) [Dvyniečių flora Chrebtovyj draustinio ežeruose Šiaurės Urale (Rusija)]. – Bot. Lith., 22(2):
L13@P0: 113–122.
L14@P0: Fourteen thermokarst lakes and one mountain lake were explored in the study period. A total of 116 species of
L15@P0: desmids belonging to 22 genera were identified. The genera Cosmarium (29), Staurastrum (18) and Closterium
L16@P0: (13) were most abundant. The highest number of species (88) was recorded in the habitats with Sphagnum and
L17@P0: brown mosses. Twenty-seven species and one variety of desmids were observed for the first time in the Polar
L18@P0: Ural Mountains.
L19@P0: Keywords: algae, benthos, Desmidiales, Polar Ural Mountains, Russia.
L20@P0: INTRODUCTION
L21@P0: Desmids are one of the most diverse groups of
L22@P0: algae in the mountain-tundra water bodies. They play
L23@P0: an important role in the formation of algocenoses
L24@P0: in water bodies and especially are typical in phytobenthos and periphyton (Getzen, 1985). Desmids
L25@P0: belong to one of the groups that can inhabit water
L26@P0: bodies with low concentration of organic materials.
L27@P0: To date, the Arctic regions experience increasing anthropogenic impact on natural landscapes, accelerating processes of global climate change, and degradation of the natural complexes. Mainly because of
L28@P0: remoteness and limited accessibility of the territory,
L29@P0: the diversity and distribution of desmids are not sufficiently explored in the Polar Ural water bodies, including mountain lakes. The aim of this paper was to
L30@P0: review the diversity and distribution of species in the
L31@P0: explored water bodies in the territory of the Khrebtovyi Nature Reserve.
L32@P0: STUDY AREA
L33@P0: The Urals are the mountain range that stretches
L34@P0: in the north-south direction in Russia, making border
L35@P0: between Asian and European continents. The total
L36@P0: area of the mountains is about 25 000 km. The Polar
L37@P0: Ural covers the northern end of the overall structure
L38@P0: of the Ural Mountains – starts from the origins of
L39@P0: the Khulga River in the southern part and stretches
L40@P0: almost for 550 km, up to the Konstantynov Kamen’
L41@P0: Mountains in the north. In 1989, in the part of this
L42@P0: territory, the Khrebtovyi Nature Reserve was established, the aim of which is to preserve typical and
L43@P0: rare landscape of the Polar Ural (Fig. 1).
L44@P0: In the area of the Reserve, different types of
L45@P0: vegetation are also under protection, including rare
L46@P0: larch forests, crooked birch forests, willow forests,
L47@P0: palsas, lichen mountain tundra, rocky tundra, brushwood moss lichen tundra and barrens. The list of protected objects consists of thermokarst and mountain

L55@P1: The climate of the Polar Ural is distinctly continental. The Polar Ural belongs to the Eastern region of the Atlantic climate area. The average annual
L56@P1: temperature is below zero, and in different regions it
L57@P1: varies from –6o

L62@P1: July varies from 8o

L66@P1: 1000–1500 mm. Permanent snow cover comes on

L68@P1: March. The depth of snow cover reaches 80–90 cm

L195@P3: RESULTS AND DISCUSSION
L196@P3: Physicochemical characteristics of the lakes
L197@P3: The analysis of water parameters of the investigated thermokarst and mountain lakes showed that
L198@P3: they belong to slightly acidic or neutral type, pH
L199@P3: varied from (4.5)5.7 to 7.0, conductivity – from 2
L200@P3: to 73 μS/cm, and temperature – from (6.3)16.0°C to
L201@P3: 20.3°C.
L202@P3: Desmid community composition
L203@P3: From the samples collected in the Khrebtovyi Nature Reserve, we identified 116 species of desmids
L204@P3: belonging to five families: Desmidiales, Closteriaceae, Mesotanenaceae, Peniaceae and Gonatozygaceae (Table 2).
L205@P3: In the Khrebtovyi Nature Reserve, we found
L206@P3: only 22% of desmid species compared to the northern territories of Russia (Luknitskaya, 2006). In the
L207@P3: investigated territory of the Polar Ural, the families
L208@P3: Desmidiaceae and Closteriaceae are predominant
L209@P3: groups, this is consistent with the conclusions of
L210@P3: other authors (Sterlyagova, 2008; Stepankovič et
L211@P3: al., 2008; Šovran et al., 2013) that these families
L212@P3: are most diverse in the northern mountainous areas.
L213@P3: These families comprised 91.4% of all algae species
L214@P3: found. However, the lowest number of desmid species were from such families as Gomatozygaceae
L215@P3: and Peniaceae (2.6% of all desmid species found).
L216@P3: The highest diversity of species was observed in
L217@P3: Cosmarium, Staurastrum, Euastrum and Closterium.
L218@P3: These four genera united 62.9% of all identified species. Such taxonomic composition is typical to the
L219@P3: northern European and Russian regions (Luknitskaya, 2006; Stepankovič et al., 2008) Even though
L220@P3: the genera Cylindrocystis, Pleurotaenium, Teilingia
L221@P3: had the lowest number of species; they are still typical genera of the northern mountainous regions (Coesel, 1996; Getzen et al., 1994; Jarushina, 2004).
L222@P3: Twenty seven species and one variety of the identified desmids were found for the first time in this northern region. Taxons of that kind in the common table
L223@P3: of desmids are marked with an asterix (Table 3). The
L224@P3: species such as Cosmarium isthmium and Gonatozygon brebissonii are included in the Red List of St. Petersburg area (Cvelev, 2000) and species Cosmarium
L225@P3: contractum, C. nasutum, Micrasterias radiosa are recognized as rare on the territory of Russia.
