L66: cite27†[AERP] L67: ### According to Flora of Iraq
L68: 
L69: Ghazanfar, S. A. & Edmondson, J. R (Eds). (2016) Flora of Iraq, Volume 5 Part 1: Elatinaceae to Sphenocleaceae.
L70: Morphology General Habit
L71:     Annual, erect, branched or not, 8–30(–40) cm tall, glaucescent, subglabrous or sparsely pubescent below, more densely pubescent above
L72: Morphology Leaves
L73:     Basal leaves spatulate to oblong-spatulate, soon withering; cauline leaves in 4–7 pairs, oblong-spatulate or ovate-oblong or lanceolate, sessile and shortly contracted at base, 10–50 × 4–15 mm, obtuse, somewhat undulate
L74: Morphology Reproductive morphology Inflorescences
L75:     Inflorescence a many-flowered dichasium, seldom 1–flowered; branches sometimes ± congested
L76: Morphology Reproductive morphology Flowers Pedicel
L77:     Pedicels of variable length
L78: Morphology Reproductive morphology Inflorescences Bracts
L79:     Bracts lanceolate, to 10 mm, shortly pubescent, subacute
L80: Morphology Reproductive morphology Flowers Calyx
L81:     Calyx ± turbinate, sub-clavate in fruit, 9–12 mm, umbilicate, weakly striate, whitish or purplish-tinged, papery, very finely puberulent; teeth short, ± semi-circular, not more than 1.5 mm
L82: Morphology Reproductive morphology Flowers Corolla
L83:     Petals pink, limb to 3 mm, emarginate
L84: Morphology Reproductive morphology Flowers Receptacle
L85:     Anthophore 3–5 mm, pubescent
L86: Morphology Reproductive morphology Fruits
L87:     Capsule ovoid, 6–8 × 3–3.5 mm, not or slightly exserted from calyx
L88: Morphology Reproductive morphology Seeds
L89:     Seeds reniform.
L90: Ecology
L91:     Fallow and irrigated fields; alt. 30–150 m;
L92: Phenology
L93:     Flowering: Feb.–May.
L94: Distribution
L95:     Iraq, Mediterranean area except Balkans; N Africa (Algeria, Egypt, Libya, Morocca, Tunisia); Palestine, Jordan, Lebanon, Syria, NW and N Iran.
