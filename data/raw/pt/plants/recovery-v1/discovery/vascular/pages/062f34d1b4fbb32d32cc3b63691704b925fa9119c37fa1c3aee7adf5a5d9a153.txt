L187: Herbaceous Perennial
L188: # Arctotis × hybrida hort. 'Wine'
L189: 
L190: African daisy 'Wine'
L191: 
L192: A tender perennial with lobed grey-green leaves. The dusky pink daisy-like flowers are held above the foliage on upright stems from midsummer through autumn.
