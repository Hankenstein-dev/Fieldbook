L123: Oeosporangium is a genus of cite40†ferns in the subfamily cite43†Cheilanthoideae of the family cite42†Pteridaceae .[ 2 ] The genus has not always been recognized. In the cite44†Pteridophyte Phylogeny Group classification of 2016 (PPG I) it was included in Allosorus[ 2 ] (a genus since subsumed into cite45†Aleuritopteris ). It was accepted by the Checklist of the Ferns and Lycophytes of the World as of January 2020cite24†[update] .[ 1 ] As cite46†circumscribed there, the genus is native to the cite47†Old World .[ 1 ]
