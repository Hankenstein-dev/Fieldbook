L68: Annual, stems stiff, erect, up to 30 cm, branching near base to form dense clump. Young stems green and hairy, older stems glossy, yellowish. Leaves narrowly lance-shaped, 0.5* 2-4 cm, edges smooth. Leaves below tapering towards base, upper leaves half-clasping. Aromatic. Flowerheads on short stalks, solitary or in pairs. Flowerheads like buttons, yellow, 0.8 cm across. Fruit with hair tufts. Flowering from February to April.
