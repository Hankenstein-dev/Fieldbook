L59: ## Descriptions
L60: ### According to Flora of Iraq
L61: 
L62: Ghazanfar, S. A., Edmondson, J. R. & Hind, D. J. N. (Eds). (2019). Flora of Iraq, Volume 6: Compositae.Kew Publishing
L63: Morphology General Habit
L64:     Annual, viscid-pubescent, 15–40 cm., simple or branched from a little  above  base upwards
L65: Morphology Stem
L66:     Stem and leaves somewhat rough with rather coarse glandular hairs
L67: Morphology Leaves
L68:     Basal leaves petiolate, usually pinnatisect or pinnatifid with lobes dentate or pinnatifid, rarely acutely lobed; median stem leaves sessile, mostly pinnatifid, auriculate, amplexicaul, 3.5–7 × 0.8– 4 cm; upper leaves similar but reducing, pinnatifid to linear and dentate
L69: Morphology Reproductive morphology Inflorescences
L70:     Inflorescence a lax corymb with several showy capitula
L71: Morphology Reproductive morphology Inflorescences Capitulum
L72:     Capitula 1.75–3.5 cm in diameter; involucre broadly obconical to shortly cylindrical; phyllaries narrowly lanceolate, subacute, glandular-hairy, 7 mm, apices often purplish; calycular bracts few, 4–6, linear, 1.5–2.5 mm, also often purplish at tip
L73: Ray
L74:     Rays pinkish-purple to purple, rarely white, (5–)7–12(–15) mm, disk florets yellow
L75: Morphology Reproductive morphology Fruits
L76:     Achenes hairy, 3–4 mm; pappus dimorphic, 6 mm, white.
L77: Ecology
L78:     Said to be sometimes cultivated on the irrigated alluvial plain; alt. ± 35 m;
L79: Phenology
L80:     flowering Mar.
L81: Distribution
L82:     A South African species which has been reported widely cultivated in gardens in Iraq; no other specimens have been seen.
