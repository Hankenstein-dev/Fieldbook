L15@P0: Introduction. Epidendrum L. is one of the largest genera of Orchidaceae, comprasing 1400–2400 species
L16@P0: (Christenhusz et al., 2017; Hágsater & Soto-Arenas,
L17@P0: 2005). It is included in subfamily Epidendroideae Lindl,
L18@P0: tribe Epidendreae Kunth, and subtribe Laeliinae Benth.,
L19@P0: differing from the remaining genera in these groups by
L20@P0: the presence of a clawed labellum usually adnate to the
L21@P0: column, presence of a cuniculus (internal nectary in
L22@P0: the ovary) and by the dorsal apical anther (Hágsater &
L23@P0: Soto-Arenas, 2005). In Brazil, the genus is represented
L24@P0: by 133 species, 65 of which are endemic. The species
L25@P0: are distributed in all regions, but they are more common in humid forests in the north and east of the country
L26@P0: (Pessoa, 2020). Species show wide vegetative and floral morphological variation (Hagsáter & Soto-Arenas,
L27@P0: 2005), leading to the description of several names currently treated as synonyms. In Brazil, for example, 413
L28@P0: synonyms are listed besides the accepted names (BFG,
L29@P0: 2015, 2018, 2022). Recent studies have investigated the
L30@P0: validity of similar species (Pessoa et al., 2021), even
L31@P0: showing a hybrid origin for some of them (Pessoa et
L32@P0: al., 2022a), but there are still many species to be further investigated. For example, few Brazilian species
L33@P0: have been sampled in phylogenetic studies of the genus
L34@P0: (Granados-Mendoza et al. 2020; Hagsáter & Soto-Arenas, 2005; Klein et al., 2019; Pessoa et al., 2012, 2021,
L35@P0: 2022b; Pinheiro et al. 2009).
L36@P0: Taxonomic studies focusing on Epidendrum have
L37@P0: been carried out in some regions of Brazil (Engels &
L38@P0: Rocha, 2017; Gomes et al., 2021; Pessoa et al., 2024;
L39@P0: Abstract. The state of Mato Grosso, in Central-West Brazil, is located within the Amazon Forest, Cerrado
L40@P0: and Pantanal phytogeographical domains, harboring diverse habitats for species of Epidendrum. The goal of
L41@P0: the present study was to develop a taxonomic treatment, including diagnoses, identification key, photographs
L42@P0: and distribution maps for the species of Epidendrum in the state. We confirmed the presence of 20 species.
L43@P0: Additionally, after analyzing the type specimen of E. callobotrys, a species described from Mato Grosso and
L44@P0: known from only one specimen, we concluded it does not differ from E. coronatum; thus, we propose its
L45@P0: synonymization. Most species are distributed in the Amazon (17 spp.) and Cerrado (13 spp.) domains. On the
L46@P0: other hand, the Pantanal only had five species, recorded for the first time in the present study, which is among
L47@P0: the first studies on Orchidaceae in this domain.
L48@P0: Resumo. O estado de Mato Grosso, no Centro-Oeste do Brasil, está localizado entre os domínios fitogeográficos da Amazônia, Cerrado e Pantanal, e possui uma grande diversidade de habitats para espécies de
L49@P0: Epidendrum. O objetivo deste estudo foi desenvolver um tratamento taxonômico incluindo diagnoses, chave
L50@P0: de identificação, fotografias e mapas de distribuição para as espécies de Epidendrum que ocorrem no estado.
L51@P0: Confirmamos a presença de 20 espécies. Adicionalmente, depois de analisar o espécime tipo de E. callobotrys,
L52@P0: uma espécie descrita para Mato Grosso e conhecida apenas por uma amostra, concluímos que esta não difere
L53@P0: de E. coronatum, portanto propomos sua sinonimização. A maioria das espécies são distribuídas nos domínios
L54@P0: da Amazônia (17 spp.) e Cerrado (13 spp.). Por outro lado, no Pantanal ocorrem somente cinco espécies, todas
L55@P0: registradas pela primeira vez para o domínio neste estudo, que é o primeiro para a família para o Pantanal.

L76@P1: Material and methods. The state of Mato Grosso
L77@P1: comprehends a total area of 903,207.047 km² (almost
L78@P1: as big as Venezuela), located in the Central-West region of Brazil (IBGE, 2022). The state contains distinct ecosystems within its large territory. The northern
L79@P1: part of the state is in the Amazon phytogeographical
L80@P1: domain, with an extension of 480,215 km² (53.6% of
L81@P1: the state’s area), the median part is covered by Cerrado, with 354,823 km2
L82@P1:  (39.6%), and the Pantanal is
L83@P1: found to the south, with 60,885 km² (6.8%) (SEMA,
L84@P1: 2010) (Fig. 1). The tropical monsoon and tropical
L85@P1: savanna climates dominate, with high annual mean
L86@P1: temperature, with rainy summers and dry winters. It
L87@P1: contains abundant hydric resources, with several water
L88@P1: bodies, springs, and aquifers (IBGE, 2022).
