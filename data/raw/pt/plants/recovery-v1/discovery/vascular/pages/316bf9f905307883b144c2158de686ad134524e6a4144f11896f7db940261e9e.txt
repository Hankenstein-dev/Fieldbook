L46: 
L47: First published in Cat. Jard. Bot. Krzemieniec, Suppl. 4: 20, 61, 67 (1815)
L48: 
L49: This hybrid is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this hybrid is Canary Islands, NW. Africa, Europe. It is a shrub and grows primarily in the temperate biome. The hybrid formula is R. canina × R. rubiginosa.
