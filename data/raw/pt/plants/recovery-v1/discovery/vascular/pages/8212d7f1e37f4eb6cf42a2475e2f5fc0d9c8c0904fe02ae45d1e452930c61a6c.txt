L145: 
L146: Scorzoneroides or hawkbits is a genus of plants of the tribe cite62†Cichorieae within the family cite60†Asteraceae .^{cite67†[2] }^{cite68†[3] }
L147: Scorzoneroides used to be included in the genus cite69†Leontodon . Recent molecular studies revealed that cite69†Leontodon in the older, broader sense is an unnatural adhesion of two separate groupings. Therefore, the former subgenus Oporinia of the genus cite69†Leontodon had to be resurrected as a separate genus.^{cite70†[4] } ^{cite71†[5] }
L148: ## Secondary metabolites
L149: 
L150: [cite72†edit ]
L151: 
L152: In some members of the genus Scorzoneroides, cite73†germacranolides and costus cite74†lactone -type cite75†guaianolides were detected. The genus is a rich source of hieracin-type and lactucin-type guaianolides.^{cite76†[6] }
L153: cite77†Phenolics found in flowering heads of Scorzoneroides taxa include cite78†luteolin type cite79†flavonoids and cite80†caffeoyl cite81†quinic acid derivatives such as cite82†chlorogenic acid and 3,5-cite83†dicaffeoylquinic acid . Moreover, Scorzoneroides are source species of cite84†caffeoyl tartaric acid derivatives caffeoyl tartaric acid and cite85†cichoric acid .^{cite86†[7] }
