L57: Native to (or naturalized in) Oregon:
L58: 
L59: No
L60:   *  Broadleaf, evergreen ground cover or shrub, 1-2 ft tall (0.3-0.6  m) spreading to 6 ft (1.8  m).  Leaves simple, alternate, glossy green.   Prolific producer of coral red spherical fruit.  It is a fully fertile hybrid that reproduces sexually
L61: 
L62:   * Sun to part shade, average watering, adaptable to soil type but best on well-drained
L63: 
L64:   * Hardy to USDA Zone 5
L65: 
L66:   * Possibly more than one selection under this name, Sunset Western Garden Book lists it as growing only to 6 inches (15 cm) high.
L67:   * Taxonomy: 'Coral Beauty’ is now considered a selection of the hybrid, Cotoneaster x suecicus, which was derived from a cross of Cotoneaster dammeri x Cotoneaster conspicuous.   However, it is often offered as Cotonaeaster dammeri 'Coral Beauty' in the nursery trade. It may also be offered as 'Pink Beauty' and 'Royal Beauty'.
L68: 
L69:   * suecicus: Swedish.  The hybrid, Cotoneaster x suecicus, is known as the Swedish Cotoneaster, I don't know why (yet).
