L15: Description
L16: 
L17: 
L18: Wheat Celosia is a dense herbaceous annual with an upright spreading habit of growth in the Amaranth family.  Its multiple showy blooms reach up to 4 feet tall providing a long season of blooms They are great as a cut or dried flower also. They withstand the fall weather to provide structure in a winter garden.
L19: The foliage is dense to the ground and its narrow leaves remain green all season. It prefers rich soils in full sun and tolerates a variety of pHs and drought once established. It is intolerant of standing water.
L20: 
L21: Wheat Celosia works well planted in mass, along borders or in containers as the center "thriller."
