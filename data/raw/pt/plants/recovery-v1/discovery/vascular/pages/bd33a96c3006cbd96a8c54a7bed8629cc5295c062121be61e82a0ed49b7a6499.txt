L46: 
L47: First published in Hort. Bot. Panorm. 1: 70 (1878)
L48: 
L49: This hybrid is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this hybrid is Cape Prov. It is a succulent perennial and grows primarily in the subtropical biome. The hybrid formula is A. maculata × A. striata.
