L59: ## Descriptions
L60: ### According to Flora of Iraq
L61: 
L62: Ghazanfar, S., Edmondson, J., & Haloob, A. (Eds.). (2026). Flora of Iraq Volume 7: Solanaceae to Biebersteiniaceae. Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew.
L63: Morphology General Habit
L64:     Suffruticose, often mat-forming perennial
L65: Morphology Stem
L66:     Stems prostrate to erect, usually much branched from base, 5–50 cm tall, glabrous to hirsute, sometimes pilose above
L67: Morphology Leaves
L68:     Leaves very variable in size and shape, 4–15 × 2–6 mm, linear-lanceolate to ovate-oblong, rarely suborbicular to orbicular, petiolate to 2.5 mm long, attenuate, cuneate or rounded at base, acute to acuminate at apex, entire or slightly serrulate, glabrous to ± pilose or velutinous with sessile glands
L69: Morphology Reproductive morphology Inflorescences
L70:     Inflorescence a dense terminal head, verticillasters 8–17-flowered
L71: Morphology Reproductive morphology Inflorescences Bracts
L72:     Bracts usually broader than leaves, 3–13 × 1.8–8.5 mm, ovate to ovate-lanceolate or ovate-elliptic or spatulate, sessile, entire, densely pilose or velutinose at margins, acute to acuminate at apex
L73: Morphology Reproductive morphology Inflorescences Bracteoles
L74:     Bracteoles linear-lanceolate or subulate, 1–2 mm
L75: Morphology Reproductive morphology Flowers Calyx
L76:     Calyx (3–)4–6(–8) mm, weakly bilabiate, green or partly purple, 13-veined, glabrous to densely hirsute-villous, with sessile glands, hairy in throat; teeth triangular-subulate, 1–1.5 mm
L77: Morphology Reproductive morphology Flowers Corolla
L78:     Corolla 7–12 mm, purplish pink (rarely white), tube included in calyx (in females) or shortly exserted (in hermaphrodites) corolla bilabiate, upper lip emarginate; lower lip 3-lobed, median lobe truncate, lateral lobes rounded
L79: Morphology Reproductive morphology Flowers Androecium Stamens
L80:     Fertile stamens 2, as long as upper lip or slightly longer; filaments 3–5 mm, glabrous, appendage absent
L81: Morphology Reproductive morphology Flowers Gynoecium Style
L82:     Style unequally bifid, 5–11 mm, included in corolla or longer
L83: Morphology Reproductive morphology Fruits
L84:     Nutlets 1.2–1.8 × 0.5–0.8 mm, obovoid-oblong, slightly papillate, brown.
L85: Distribution
L86:     Ziziphora clinopodioides has a wide distribution from Turkey (except for western Turkey) to Afghanistan and shows a wide variation of morphological characters; 4 subspecies are recognized for Iraq: General Turkey (except for western Turkey) to W. Himalaya.
