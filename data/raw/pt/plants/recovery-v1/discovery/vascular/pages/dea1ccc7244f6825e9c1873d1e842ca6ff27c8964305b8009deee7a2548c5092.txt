L70: cite28†[AERP] L71: ### According to Flora Zambesiaca under the synonym Littonia modesta
L72: 
L73: J. R. Timberlake, E. S. Martins (2009). Flora Zambesiaca, Vol 12 (part 2). Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew
L74: Type
L75:     Type South Africa, Durban (Port Natal), x.1883, anderson s.n. (K holotype).
L76: Morphology General Habit
L77:     Perennial herb to 2 m
L78: Vegetative Multiplication Corms
L79:     Corm 5–6  c.2 cm, oblong
L80: Morphology Stem
L81:     Stem scandent, 60–200 cm high, the basal 18 cm lacking leaf
L82: Morphology Leaves
L83:     Leaves very numerous, set close together in whorls of 3–4, growing smaller and narrower towards stem apex, variable in width even on same plant, 10–17 0.5–3.5 cm, lanceolate or linear-lanceolate, tip drawn out into tendril c.2 cm long (included in leaf length)
L84: Morphology Reproductive morphology Flowers
L85:     Flowers axillary, bright yellow or orange
L86: Morphology Reproductive morphology Flowers Pedicel
L87:     Pedicel 2–5.5 cm, bent at apex so flowers are nodding
L88: Morphology Reproductive morphology Flowers Calyx
L89:     Sepals and petals lanceolate, acute, united for 2–4 mm at base, 17–40 4–10 mm
L90: Morphology Reproductive morphology Flowers Androecium Stamens Filaments
L91:     Filaments c.7 mm long; anther c.8 mm long
L92: Morphology Reproductive morphology Flowers Gynoecium Ovary
L93:     Ovary 8–10 × 3 mm (possibly partly developed); style 4 mm long with 3 recurved branch 2–3 mm long.
L94: Distribution
L95:     Zimbabwe, Mozambique. Also in South Africa and Swaziland.
L96: Ecology
L97:     Woodland; 800–1500 m.
L98: Conservation
L99:     Conservation notes Widespread and not threatened globally, but within the Flora area likely to be Vulnerable.
