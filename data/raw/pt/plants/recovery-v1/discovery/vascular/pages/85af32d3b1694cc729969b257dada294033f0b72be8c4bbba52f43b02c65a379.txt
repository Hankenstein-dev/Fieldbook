L7: Alternative names
L8: 
L9: Slender Pearlwort
L10: Sagina apetala subsp. filicaulis
L11: 
L12: Description
L13: 
L14: There are several similar members of the Pearlwort family, but Sagina filicaulis usually has distinctive red margins to the sepals, excessively protruding fruit, and some of the sepals at right angles.
L15: Similar Species
L16: 
L17: other pearlworts.  It is very similar to Sagina apetala; these two species have been much confused in the past, and are sometimes grouped into Sagina apetala sensu lato.
L18: 
L19: Identification difficulty
L20: 
L21: ID checklist (your specimen should have all of these features)
L22: 
L23: Annual, with all shoots producing flowers; leaves finely pointed; sepals in at least some flowers spreading ('patent') in fruit, and the outer sepals sub-obtuse to obtuse or hooded.
L24: Sagina apetala has sepals erect to erecto-patent in fruit, with the outer subacute to acute or acuminate.

L26: Recording advice
L27: 
L28: Records must be verified by a County Recorder from a specimen or series of photos
L29: 
L30: Habitat
L31: 
L32: Dry ground, sandy places, old walls and crevices.
L33: 
L34: When to see it
L35: 
L36: Flowering April to August.
L37: 
L38: Life History
L39: 
L40: Annual.
L41: 
L42: UK Status
L43: Status in Britain is difficult to determine as records seem to be rather confused.  It was previously regarded as a subspecies of Sagina apetala.  Stace (2019) 4th edition states ' . . probably commoner in man-made habitats than S apetala . . .'  .
L44: 
L45: Leicestershire & Rutland Status
L46: 
L47: It was not recorded in the 1979 Flora survey of Leicestershire.
L48: 
L49: In the current checklist (Jeeves, 2011) it is listed as Native; current distribution and frequency uncertain
