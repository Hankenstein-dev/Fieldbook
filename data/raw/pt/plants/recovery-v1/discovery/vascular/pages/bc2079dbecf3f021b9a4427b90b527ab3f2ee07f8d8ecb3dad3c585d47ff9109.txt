L78: cite19†View picture†cdn.auckland.ac.nz L79: Nephrolepis flexuosa is a terrestrial fern with short, erect stems and lateral stems (runners) that form new plants at their tips.  The almost parallel-sided fronds are yellow green, stiff and erect when young but drooping with age. Often confused with the naturalised and aggressive Nephrolepis cordifolia which differs in having tubers and wider, stouter and more rigid fronds.
L80: 
L81: Found in thermal areas of the North Island.
L82: Vegetative characteristics  | Fertile frond and sporangia  |
L83: --- | --- | ---
L84: Plant form: erect stem with fronds up to 65 cm in length  | Frond appearance: leaflets are slightly shorter and margins rarely toothed  |
L85: Frond stalk, midrib: pale brown  | Sporangium location: lower frond surface  |
L86: Frond shape: linear  | Sporangium position: near leaflet margin  |
L87: Frond blade: pinnate (divided 1x into leaflets)  | Sporangia distribution: in groups (sori)  |
L88: Frond surface: leathery, with hydathode glands  | Sorus shape: circular  |
L89: Leaflets: oblong, smooth to slightly toothed     | Sorus covering: half-moon shaped, attached on one side, opening towards leaflet tip  |
