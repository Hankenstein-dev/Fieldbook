L13: As a vital and vibrant part of the Smithsonian experience, Smithsonian Gardens engages people with plants and gardens, informs on the roles both play in our cultural and natural world, and inspires appreciation and stewardship.

L46: cite4†Image L47: SG strives to cultivate a diverse array of orchids from all over the world. L49: Smithsonian Gardens Tree Collection
L50: SG is dedicated to maintaining the 2,000 trees that are located throughout the gardens. Showcasing native, exotic, historic, and recently cultivated trees, these plants play a vital role in living collection projects such as photo documentation and phenology tracking.

L52: Smithsonian Gardens Display Collection
L53: The Display Collection is our newest collection underway. We are documenting the roughly 20,000 perennial and woody plants throughout SG’s 13 gardens and landscapes, each of which has its own theme that supports the museum it surrounds.
