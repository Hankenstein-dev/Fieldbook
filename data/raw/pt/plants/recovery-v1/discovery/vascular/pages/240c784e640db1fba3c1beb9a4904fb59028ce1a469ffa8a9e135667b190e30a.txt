L8@P0: Rhizome short creeping, clothed with chaffy lanceolate scales, embeded in a nest of fronds and
L9@P0: roots. Roots proliferous. Fronds dimorphic with sterile base fronds laminating against the host
L10@P0: and sterile or fertilze foliage fronds emergent. Base fronds rounded-reniform, 15.5–36 cm long,
L11@P0: at least 15–26 cm wide; upper margin entire to slightly wavy, firmly appressed to substrate,
L12@P0: soon becoming brown. Foliage fronds ascending, 23–90 cm or more long, to 60 cm wide; base
L13@P0: cuneate, spreading out to a broad apex; distal one-third forked 1–5 times; ultimate segments
L14@P0: oblong, 1–6 cm wide, dark green, glabrous or almost so. Sporangia covering most of the
L15@P0: undersurface of ultimate segments of foliage fronds, frequently reaching apex and sometimes
L16@P0: extending around sinus of the ultimate fork. Spores 40–52.5 µm long, 20–35 µm wide.

L20@P0: Epiphytic or lithophytic in lowland vine forest. Most commonly seen as a large mass forming

L22@P0: Natural history
L23@P0: The nest fronds of this species are thick and corky and contribute to a thick sponge-like nest.
L24@P0: The upper margin of the nest-fronds are not lobed and seal the upper surface of the nest.
L25@P0: Cultivation
L26@P0: Readily cultivated on a tree, rock, slab or in a pot of coarse material. Grows well in a garden or
L27@P0: fernhouse in the tropics and subtropics. A usefull host for growing pendulous epiphytes.
