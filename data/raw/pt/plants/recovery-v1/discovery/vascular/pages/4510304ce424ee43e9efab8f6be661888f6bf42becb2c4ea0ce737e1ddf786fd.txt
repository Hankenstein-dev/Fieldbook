L61@P1: Other Considerations
L62@P1: ORNHIC - List 3. Most North American records for this species have been renamed Racomitrium ryszardii, and
L63@P1: R. aquaticum has been restricted to the Old World (Benarek-Ochyra 2000). It has also been confused with R.
L64@P1: pacificum, so its distribution in the Pacific Northwest is unclear. Furthermore, Bednarek-Ochyra has not yet
L65@P1: annotated material from California and Oregon, and populations south of the Columbia River may belong to a
L66@P1: different species and cannot be assumed to be the same as R. ryszardii. The identity of collections in smaller
L67@P1: herbaria should be verified and annotated, to clarify the distribution. Whatever it is called, Lawton (1971)
L68@P1: considered it rare in the Pacific Northwest.
