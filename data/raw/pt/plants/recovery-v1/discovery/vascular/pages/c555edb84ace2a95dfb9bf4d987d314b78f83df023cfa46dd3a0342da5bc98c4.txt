L119: Trimezia coerulea
L120: 
L121: Description:
L122: 
L123: Accent plant with arching, sword-like leaves; produces clusters of gorgeous, intricately-patterned, violet-blue flowers; blooms repeatedly in partial shade during the hottest part of the summers.
