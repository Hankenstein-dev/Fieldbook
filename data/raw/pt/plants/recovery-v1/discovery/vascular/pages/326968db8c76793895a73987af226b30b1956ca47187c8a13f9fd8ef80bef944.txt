L157: The cite93†Canary Islands are an archipelago in the cite94†North Atlantic Ocean , off the northwest coast of Africa.
L158: The Canary Islands are home to dozens of cite95†endemic species and subspecies of cite96†vascular plants , including the endemic genera cite97†Allagopappus , cite98†Bethencourtia , cite99†Dicheranthus , cite100†Gesnouinia , cite101†Gonospermum , cite102†Ixanthus , cite103†Parolinia , cite104†Pleiomeris , cite105†Rivasgodaya , cite106†Rutheopsis , cite107†Todaroa , and cite108†Vieraea .^{cite109†[1] } Although the Canary Islands are politically part of cite110†Spain , the cite111†World Geographical Scheme for Recording Plant Distributions treats the Canary Islands as distinct botanical country.
L159: The Canary Islands are part of cite112†Macaronesia , a biogeographical region which also includes the cite113†Azores , cite114†Cape Verde Islands , cite115†Madeira , and the cite116†Selvagens .^{cite117†[2] }
L160: Plants are listed alphabetically by plant family. Extinct and presumed extinct species are indicated with †.
