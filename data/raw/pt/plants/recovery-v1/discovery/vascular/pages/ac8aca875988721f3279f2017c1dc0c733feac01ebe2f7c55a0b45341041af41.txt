L149: ## How to Grow
L150: ### Cultivation
L151: 
L152: Under glass grow in peat-free. loam-based potting compost with added extra cite8†organic matter . Provide moderate to high humidity and protect from draughts. Water freely during the growing season and apply a balanced liquid feed every two weeks; water moderately in winter. Repot every 2-3 years in spring
