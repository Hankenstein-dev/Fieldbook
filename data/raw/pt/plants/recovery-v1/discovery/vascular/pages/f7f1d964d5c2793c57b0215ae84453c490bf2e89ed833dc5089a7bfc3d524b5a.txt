L12: NEW SOUTH WALES FLORA ONLINE cite12†Image L13: Genus Telopea Family cite13†Proteaceae L14: cite14†Image Description: Shrubs or rarely small trees, usually multistemmed from a lignotuber. Adult leaves simple, margins entire or toothed; intermediate leaves simple with margins sinuate- to pinnate-lobed. Conflorescences terminal, capitate, subtended by red to pink involucral bracts, not aggregated, pseudo-racemose; flowers borne in pairs. Flowers zygomorphic, usually red, bird-pollinated. Perianth strongly incurved in bud; tepals cohering in a split tube after anthesis [separating in T. truncata].
L15: Hypogynous glands fused into a crescentic to horseshoe-shaped nectary. Ovary stipitate; pollen presenter strongly oblique. Follicle woody; seeds winged.
