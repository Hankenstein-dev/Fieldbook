L46:

L48: 
L49: This hybrid is accepted
L50: 
L51: The native range of this hybrid is Temp. Northern Hemisphere. It is a hydrosubshrub and grows primarily in the temperate biome. The hybrid formula is P. gramineus × P. natans.
