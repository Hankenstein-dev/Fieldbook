L174: ## Folk-medicinal and ritual uses
L175: 
L176: [cite91†edit ]
L177: Several cite92†Himalayan species belonging to the genus are both taken internally and burnt as dhoop or incense as cite93†sedatives to soothe mental turmoil of various kinds in cite94†Tantric rituals. Given that cite95†aphrodisiac properties are also reported they may also be used in practices related to cite96†sex magic / sacred sexuality. They are aromatic and mildly psychoactive without being unduly toxic - some species are recorded as having been used both as human food and cattle fodder.^{cite97†[3] }
