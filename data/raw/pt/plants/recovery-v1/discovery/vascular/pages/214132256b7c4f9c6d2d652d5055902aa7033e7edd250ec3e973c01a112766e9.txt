L87: cite55†Image†static.wixstatic.com L88: ###### Acanthus syriacus Boiss.
L89: 
L90: (Nouvelle Flore du Liban et de la Syrie, vol. 3, p. 272, Pl. CXXXVIII; 1973)
L91: 
L92:   * Life‑form & habit : Perennial, softly pubescent herb forming a loose clump 40 – 80 cm high; stem and flowering spike rise only slightly above the foliage.
L93: 
L94:   * Leaves : Basal leaves long, oblong‑lanceolate, briefly tapering into a petiole; pinnatipartite into lanceolate segments that are themselves lobed and spiny, the lobes merging at the base. Usually two shorter, sessile cauline leaves.
L95:   * Inflorescence & bracts : Spike ovoid‑cylindrical, compact, often flushed purple when fresh. Bracts conspicuously large (4 – 6 cm or more), equalling or exceeding the flower, leathery, with raised reticulate nerves; margins ciliate; each side armed with 4 – 5 stout spines.
L96: 
L97:   * Calyx : Glabrous; upper lobe large, herbaceous, oblong‑spatulate, faintly toothed at the tip; lower lobe narrower, linear, widened and bifid at the apex, the tips spinulose.
L98:   * Corolla : White, completely glabrous; single lip trilobed, erect, the lobes rounded.
L99: 
L100:   * Flowering period : March – May.
L101: 
L102:   * Habitat & elevation : Fields and rocky ground in coastal and other Mediterranean‑climate zones, at low to mid elevations.
L103: 
L104:   * Distribution : Eastern Mediterranean—Lebanon, Syria, Turkey, and Palestine.
