L268: cite204†Aizoaceae Martynov, nom. cons. (fig-marigold family) is a large cite205†family of cite206†dicotyledonous cite207†flowering plants in the cite208†order cite209†Caryophyllales containing 135 currently accepted cite210†genera and about 1800 cite211†species .^{cite212†[1] } They are commonly known as ice plants, carpet weeds or vygies.
L269: 23,420 species of vascular plant have been recorded in South Africa, making it the sixth most species-rich country in the world and the most species-rich country on the African continent. Of these, 153 species are considered to be threatened.^{cite213†[2] } Nine cite214†biomes have been described in South Africa: cite215†Fynbos , Succulent cite216†Karoo , cite217†desert , cite218†Nama Karoo , cite219†grassland , cite220†savanna , cite221†Albany thickets , the cite222†Indian Ocean coastal belt , and cite223†forests .^{cite224†[3] }
L270: The 2018 cite225†South African National Biodiversity Institute 's cite226†National Biodiversity Assessment plant checklist lists 35,130 taxa in the phyla cite227†Anthocerotophyta (hornworts (6)), cite228†Anthophyta (flowering plants (33534)), cite229†Bryophyta (mosses (685)), cite230†Cycadophyta (cycads (42)), cite231†Lycopodiophyta (Lycophytes(45)), cite232†Marchantiophyta (liverworts (376)), cite233†Pinophyta (conifers (33)), and cite234†Pteridophyta (cryptogams (408)).^{cite235†[4] }
L271: 169 cite236†genera are represented in the literature. Listed cite237†taxa include cite211†species , cite238†subspecies , cite239†varieties , and cite240†forms as recorded, some of which have subsequently been allocated to other taxa as cite241†synonyms , in which cases the accepted taxon is appended to the listing.^{cite235†[4] } Multiple entries under alternative names reflect cite242†taxonomic revision over time.
