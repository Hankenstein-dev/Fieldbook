L27: ## Facts
L28: 
L29: Stout dotted smartweed is a native smartweed, found in New England mainly on the coastal plain along shorelines, and in swamps and marshes. It is distinctive for its tepals, which are dotted with small, brown glands and its relatively wide leaves (20-45 mm, 1-2 inches) and copious, dense, pink flowers.
L30: ## Habitat
L31: 
L32: Marshes, shores of rivers or lakes, swamps, wetland margins (edges of wetlands)
