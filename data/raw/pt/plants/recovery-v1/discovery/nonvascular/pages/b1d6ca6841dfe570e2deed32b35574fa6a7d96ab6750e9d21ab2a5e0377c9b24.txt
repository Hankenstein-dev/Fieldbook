L23:   * cite16†Books L24:   * cite17†Videos L25: # Ellisolandia elongata (J.Ellis & Solander) K.R.Hind & G.W.Saunders, 2013
L26: Other names: Also known as Corallina mediterranea and Corallina elongata Ellis et Solander
L27: Common name: Elongate Coral Weed
L28: Description: Chalky whitish-pink to reddish-lilac, calcified, limp, articulated, elegant fronds,  regular fish-bone-like arrangement, to 50 mm high, axis compressed, repeatedly pinnate from discoid base, more abundantly and regularly branched than C. officinalis. Articulations small (0.5-2 mm broad).
L29: Habitat: On rock, wave-exposed coasts, lower intertidal. Often on mussels on exposed coasts
L30: Distribution: Southern and western coasts of Britain and Ireland, occasional; probably under-recorded. .South to Mauritania. Mediterranean. Black Sea. Records from the southern parts of the range may be other species.
