L291@P10: PROPOSALS FOR ADDING NEW SPECIES
L292@P10: Macrophytes Species
L293@P10: At the last ordinary meeting (Almeria, January 2008), the Contracting Parties to the
L294@P10: Barcelona Convention adopted a format for the revision of Annex II and III of the Protocol on
L295@P10: Specially Protected Areas and Biological Diversity in the Mediterranean (SPA/BD) and asked
L296@P10: RAC/SPA to propose amendments to these annexes. RAC/SPA asked two experts to set up
L297@P10: a preliminary list of over a hundred vegetal species worthy of study so that they could be
L298@P10: included in Annex II of the Protocol.
L299@P10: In January RAC/SPA with IUCN – Mediterranean organized a joint meeting in Tunis of an ad
L300@P10: hoc group of experts (Table III) in order to
L301@P10: i) Initiate an evaluation of marine macrophyte species so as to create a red list
L302@P10: for the Mediterranean
