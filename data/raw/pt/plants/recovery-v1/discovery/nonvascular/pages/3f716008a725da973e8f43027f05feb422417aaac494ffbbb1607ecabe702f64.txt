L49: Morphological Characteristics
L50: The coenobium composed of 2, 4 or 8 cells arranged in rows. The cells are fusiform; those inside the coenobium have rounded extremities, while the external ones have thread-like extremities and short spines sprouting from the poles.

L68: Distribution
L69: In plankton and littoral of mesotrophic to eutrophic waters, common, possible cosmopolitan.
