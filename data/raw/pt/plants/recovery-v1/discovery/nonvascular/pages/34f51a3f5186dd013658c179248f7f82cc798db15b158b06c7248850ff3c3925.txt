L242: Biology & Natural History Information:
L243: 
L244: Habitat:
L245: 
L246: Grows on rocks, often embedded in sand, and other hard substrates including smaller stones and shells in lower intertidal and subtidal (to 18m/60feet deep).
L247: 
L248: Description:
L249: Slender, cylindrical, somewhat fleshy thallus supports many rather brittle fronds, laterals tapering both at their bases and tips. The thallus attaches to the substrate with a discoid holdfast. The whole plant is pale pink to deep red or reddish brown in color and the cylindrical, fleshy, pointed-tipped branches may be opposite or grow from all angles of the main axis (radial). Succulent Seaweed can grow from 10 to 45cm tall.
L250: Some authors describe the plant as looking like a “cluster of translucent noodles” and it is noted that this species often grows in clumps. This seaweed will “bleach” to straw yellow or even white in the summer.
L251: Pacific Coast Distribution:
L252: 
L253: Ketchikan, Alaska (southern tip of Alaska), to Baja California. Also found in the Galapagos Islands, Peru, Chile as well as the south coast of England where it was likely introduced.
