L713: #### Description
L714: Collected with permit as part of CALS lichen bioblitz. On pine branch. This is a continuous pale gray crust with numerous 0.5-2.0 mm lirellae, some of which show several parallel ridges. Cross-sections show lirellae measuring about 300 microns wide and 150 microns tall and with blackish borders. The septate spores are about 9-celled and measure 50-55 x 12 microns.
L715: The thallus shows a P plus orange reaction and a K plus yellow reaction with formation of red sheaves of norstictic acid crystals under the compound scope.
