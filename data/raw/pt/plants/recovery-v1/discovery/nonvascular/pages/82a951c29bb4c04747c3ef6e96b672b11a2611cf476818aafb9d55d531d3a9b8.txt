L127: Polysiphoides fucoides (Hudson) Greville (Vertebrata fucoides)^{cite52†[1] } is a common marine cite53†alga ^{cite54†[2] } in the Division cite55†Rhodophyta .
L128: ## Description
L129: 
L130: [cite56†edit ]
L131: Polysiphonia fucoides is a relatively small seaweed. It grows as branched, cylindrical tufts reaching 30 cm or more in length.^{cite52†[1] } The branches are themselves densely branched, the main branch usually distinct. It is attached by matted branches which produce cite57†rhizoids . The branches are composed of a central axes of cells surrounded by 11 to 21 pericentral cells all of the same length.
L132: cite58†Cortication is generally absent but may be formed on the major branches near the cite59†holdfast , with the cells growing down in grooves between the pericentral cells.^{cite60†[3] }^{cite52†[1] } The branching may resemble cite61†Vertebrata thuyoides , but the branches of B.thuyoides are cite62†corticated almost to the tips.^{cite60†[3] }^{cite52†[1] }
L133: ## Reproduction
L134: 
L135: [cite63†edit ]
L136: 
L137: The plants are cite64†dioecious . Spermatangial branchlets occur near the apices of the branches. cite65†Cystocarps are borne on short stalks. and tetrasporangia occur in series towards the apices of the branches.^{cite60†[3] }
