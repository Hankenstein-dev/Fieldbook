L145: 
L146: Lithophyllum is a genus of thalloid cite55†red algae belonging to the family cite58†Corallinaceae .
L147: 
L148: Lithophyllum stictaeforme. L. orbiculatum on a rock fragment; rock length ~3 cm. Lithophyllum okamurae Lithophyllum sp.
L149: ## Fossil record
L150: 
L151: [cite63†edit ]
L152: 
L153: This genus is known in the fossil record from the cite51†Silurian to the cite64†Quaternary (from about 418.7 to 0.0 million years ago). Fossils of species within this genus have been found in Europe, United States, South America, Egypt, Iran, Iraq, India, Japan and Australia.^{cite65†[2] }
L154: ## Description
L155: 
L156: [cite66†edit ]
L157: 
L158: The monomerous, crustose thalli are composed of a single system of filaments which grow close to the underlying surface. Lithophyllum reproduces by means of cite67†conceptacles . The cite68†epithallus is periodically shed to avoid organisms growing on top of the alga.^{cite69†[3] }^{cite70†[4] }
