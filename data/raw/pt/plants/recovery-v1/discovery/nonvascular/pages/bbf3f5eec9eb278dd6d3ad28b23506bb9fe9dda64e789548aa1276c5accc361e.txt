L4: 
L5: * * *

L7: Description: Thallus uniseriate in erect dense tufts to 15 cms long. Rhizoidal filaments form a thick cortex.
L8: 
L9: Habitat: Epiphytic, epilithic and epizoic in the lower littoral.
L10: 
L11: Distribution: Widely distributed in the British Isles. Further afield: Canary Islands.
