L2: cite1†Image L3: # Polyneura latissima (Harvey) Kylin (BC)
L4: This typically bright red species is highly variable in size, 1–30+ cm, and shape, being roughly obovate (Image A), to lobed (Image B) to highly dissected in exposed habitats (Image C). Regardless to the gross morphology, this species is easily (for most individuals) identified by its characteristic pattern of anastomosing veins, which are particularly evident in tetrasporangial individuals as the sori are produced in abundance between the veins (Image D).
L5: In some individuals the blades can become noticeably bullate between the venation (Image E). Tetrasporangial sori are round to oval, at times weakly elliptical (Image F) and the tetrasporangia have a tetrahedral division pattern (Image G). Mature females have cystocarps scattered across the upper 2/3 of the blade surface (Image H).
L6: This species is widely distributed in BC, from low intertidal to deep subtidal (16 m) growing on rock and other hard substrata, including coralline algae, as well as occasionally on noncalcified algae. The anastomosing venation is the key to identifying this species.
