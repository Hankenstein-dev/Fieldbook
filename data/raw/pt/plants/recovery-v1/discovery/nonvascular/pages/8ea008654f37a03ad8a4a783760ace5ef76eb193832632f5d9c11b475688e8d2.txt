L12: 
L13: Revised name: Hypnum cupressiforme var. resupinatum
L14: 
L15: English name: Supine plait-moss
L16: 
L17: A pleurocarpous moss with silky looking, spreading shoots and leaves that point up. Capsules are held more or less erect and have a long, beaked lid.
L18: 
L19: Capsules mature from late autumn to winter.
L20:
