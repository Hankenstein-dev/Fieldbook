L9@P0: point. They are moderately concave and sometimes slightly curved to the side. When
L10@P0: dry, they are not or only slightly shrunken, and not at all twisted. The leaves have
L11@P0: a single nerve and very finely toothed margins. Capsules are common and often
L12@P0: plentiful. These are curved, egg-shaped or oblong, with a beaked lid, and borne on
L13@P0: a rather short (6–10 mm long), erect, smooth seta.

L18@P0: tapering tip. The very rare R. rotundifolium (p. 763) has broader leaves which become
L19@P0: shrunken and twisted when dry. Brachythecium velutinum (p. 745) differs in its
