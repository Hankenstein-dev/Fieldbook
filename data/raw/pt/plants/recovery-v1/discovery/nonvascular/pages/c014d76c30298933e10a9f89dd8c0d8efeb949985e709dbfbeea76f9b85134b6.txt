L89: General environment
L90: This is a marine species.
L91: Description
L92: Medium to large species with cylindrical, rather brittle fronds, laterals tapering both basally & apically. Colour varies from straw yellow in summer when bleached to pale pink to deep red or reddish brown. Morphologically similar to Gracilaria bursa-pastoris but differing anatomically (filamentous medulla) & with zonate terasporangia & less obvious cystocarps.
L93: Habitat
L94: Lower intertidal pools to upper subtidal, mainly on small stones & shells; also marina pontoons. Probably introduced to the south coast of England.
