L2: cite1†Image L3: # Caulacanthus ustulatus  (Mertens ex Turner) Kützing (BC)
L4: This species forms brownish to yellowish red carpets typically on rock (Image A). Axes are straight to more typically curved with irregular branching, but regions of unilateral development can dominate the thalli (Image B). An obvious apical cell is found at the branch tips (Image C) and the surface cells are polygonal to irregular in shape and not arranged in a pattern (Image D).
L5: The uniaxial construction is obvious in long (Image E) and cross section (Image F) with the axial cells bearing 2 cortical laterals, these borne at different levels and at about 90^{o} to one another. The specimen on which the vegetative anatomy is based here appears to be producing carpogonia (possibly hairs, I need to look into these structures more), but no other reproductive structures were observed (Image F).
L6: We have collected this species widely in BC from southern Vancouver Island to Haida Gwaii (northerly range extension relative to Gabrielson & Lindstrom (2018)). More typical of sheltered to semi-exposed locations, on rock in the low to mid intertidal (upper intertidal pools).
