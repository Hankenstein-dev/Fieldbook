L9@P0: (Ducl.) Hauck was cultured from the North Aegean Sea and the Channel Islands. The two

L18@P0: and their fertilization, and continuation or suppression of fertile axes during carposporophyte

L21@P0: and Platythamnion is studied, and resurrection of the name Pterothamnion crispum (Ducl.)

L213@P3: DISCUSSION
L214@P3: The results of the crossing experiments
L215@P3: confirm those reached by Sundene (1975)

L219@P3: from the Channel Islands and the other
L220@P3: from the North Aegean Sea, suggests that a
L221@P3: single species is involved with a warmtemperate distribution along the coasts of
L222@P3: Europe, as referred to in the literature.
L223@P3: The lateral position and development of
L224@P3: procarps and carposporophytes in this
L225@P3: species is similar to that in P. plumula, as
