L128: Species
L129: ---
L130: 
L131:   * cite53†Kirchneriella aperta L132:   * cite54†Kirchneriella lunaris L133: Kirchneriella is a cite55†genus of cite56†green algae in the family cite50†Selenastraceae .^{cite57†[2] } It is found in cite58†freshwater habitats, as cite59†phytoplankton or cite60†metaphyton .^{cite61†[3] }
L134: 
L135: The genus name of Kirchneriella is in honour of cite62†Emil Otto Oskar von Kirchner (1851–1925), who was a German cite63†botanist and cite64†agronomist .^{cite65†[4] }
L136: 
L137: The genus was cite66†circumscribed by cite67†Wilhelm Schmidle in Ber. Naturf. Ges. Freiburg vol.7 on page 82 in 189.
L138: ## Description
L139: 
L140: [cite68†edit ]
L141: 
L142: Kirchneriella usually consists of colonies of cells within a thin layer of mucilage. Usually four to 16 are present within a colony, but sometimes they are solitary. Cells are crescent-shaped, containing a single cite69†chloroplast with one cite70†pyrenoid . They are irregularly distributed within the mucilage.^{cite61†[3] }
L143: Species are distinguished from each other based on cell size and shape.^{cite71†[5] } The similar genus cite72†Pseudokirchneriella was split off from Kirchneriella; it differs from this genus in that its cells lack a pyrenoid.^{cite61†[3] } Some authors do not recognize the two genera as being distinct.^{cite73†[6] }
L144: ## Reproduction
L145: 
L146: [cite74†edit ]
L147: 
L148: Kirchneriella reproduces by cite75†autospores , which are arranged serially within the mother cell. They are released by rupture of the mother cell wall.^{cite73†[6] }
