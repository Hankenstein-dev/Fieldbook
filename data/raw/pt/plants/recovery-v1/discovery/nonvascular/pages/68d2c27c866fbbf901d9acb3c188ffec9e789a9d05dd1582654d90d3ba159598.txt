L27: cite18†Fact Sheet (Download PDF) L28: Description: Cartilaginous, cylindrical, dull purple-red fronds, to 500 mm long, one or several arising from small, fleshy, perennial discoid holdfast. Branching very irregular, sparse or profuse, branches to 2 mm diam., apices pointed. Internal tissue of large thin-walled more or less colourless cells with narrow outer cortical zone of small coloured cells.
L29: Habitat: On rocks and stones, intertidal and subtidal, especially on sandy shores, generally distributed, common.
L30: Usage: A species of importance for agar production in other parts of the world.
L31: Note: Occasionally infected with the colourless, hemispherical parasite Holmesella pachyderma.
