L100: #### Bird’s-foot Wing-moss (Nogopterium gracile)
L101: 
L102: Resources
L103: 
L104:   * cite52†BC Species and Ecosystems Explorer Entry†a100.gov.bc.ca L105:   * cite53†Biodiversity Galiano iNaturalist Observations†www.inaturalist.org L106: 
L107: Status
L108: 
L109:   * BC: Blue – S2S4 (2021)
L110:   * COSEWIC: Not Ranked
L111:   * SARA: Not Ranked
L112:   * Global: Vulnerable (1999)
L113:   * Galiano Island Status: Confirmed
L114: 
L115: Description
L116: 
L117: Greenish brown moss with branches that, when dry, usually arch in the same direction, resembling the foot of a bird.
L118: 
L119: Habitat
L120: Forested mountains, rock, boulders, trunks and base of hardwood trees, and soil.
L121: 
L122: Range
L123: 
L124: Found in British Columbia, California, Oregon, Washington, South America, Europe, Asia, and Africa.
L125: 
L126: Threats
L127: 
L128: Air pollution.
L129: 
L130: Galiano Status
L131: 
L132: Several populations have been documented on the south end of Galiano Island.
