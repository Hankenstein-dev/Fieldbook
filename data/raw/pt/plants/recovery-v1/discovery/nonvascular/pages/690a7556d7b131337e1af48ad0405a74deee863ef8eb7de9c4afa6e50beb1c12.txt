L53: ### Habitat and Range ^{cite23†4 }
L54: 
L55: Commonly found on rocks burred in sand intertidally and subtidally (to 40 m), ranging from southern British Columbia to the Revillagigedo Islands, Mexico.
L56: 
L57: Source: Marine Algae of California (MAC)
L58: ### Morphology and Identification ^{cite23†4 }
L59: 
L60: Thallus grows 4 - 25 cm tall, color varies from pink to red. Flattened axes with large branches distichous, small branches pectinate and curved outward.
L61: 
L62: Previously Plocamium cartilagineum.
L63: 
L64: Source: Marine Algae of California (MAC)
