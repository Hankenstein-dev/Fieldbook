L3: ## Dudresnaya verticillata (Withering) Le Jolis
L4:

L6: 
L7: Dudresnaya verticillata
L8: 
L9: Description: An erect frond arising from a discoid holdfast to lengths of 20 or 30 cms and a diameter of about 2 mm. Branches irregular, the whole thallus soft, gelatinous and easily squashed. Composed of whorls of fine short filaments repeatedly branched.
L10: 
L11: Habitat: Uncommon. Generally epilithic from the upper sublittoral to over 13 m depth.
L12: Distribution: Widely distributed, but mainly on the western shores of the British Isles. Europe: Mediterranean, the Baltic, Norway and Sweden. Further afield: Canary Islands.
