L3: ## Laurencia obtusa (Hudson) Lamouroux
L4: 
L5: * * *
L6: 
L7: Description: Thallus erect to 20 cms with distinct main axis, terete with latterals spiral and opposite. Consists of medullary cells and an outer cortex.
L8: 
L9: Habitat: Uncommon. Epiphytic from the mid littoral to 6 m in the sublittortal.
L10: Distribution: Widely distributed on westerly coasts of the British Isles including the Shetlands and Channel Isles. Europe: Mediterranean, Azores, Atlantic coasts of Spain and France. Further afield: Canary Islands, Jamaica, West Indies and Mauritius.
L11: 
L12: Similar Species: Chondria coerulescens may appear similar but can be distinguished in transverse section by the 5 conspiculous periaxial cells to be seen in in Chondria and not in L.obtusa.
