L3: ## Stenogramma interruptum (C.Agardh) Montagne, 1846
L4:

L6: 
L7: Stenogramma interruptum
L8: 
L9: Description: Thalli arising from a small attachment disc to form small fronds which devide dichotomously into flattened branches 10 cms (or more) long. Often with an interrupted midrib formed by cystocarps.
L10: 
L11: Habitat: Epilithic and on shells, sublittoral to 13 m deep.
L12: Distribution: Generally south-western in the British Isles, south Wales, south-west England, the most northerly records being from Northern Ireland. Europe: Azores, Portugal and Spain. Further afield: Canary Islands, widely in the Pacific.
L13: 
L14: Similar Species: Specimens without a mid-rib resemble Rhodymenia pseudopalmata.
L15: 
L16: Key Identification Features:
L17: 
L18:   * The mid-rib is a distinguishing feature when the plants have developing cystocarps.
