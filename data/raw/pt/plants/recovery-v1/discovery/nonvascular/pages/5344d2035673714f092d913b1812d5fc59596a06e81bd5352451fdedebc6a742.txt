L67@P2: A morphological and molecular study of Fauchea repens, the type species of Fauchea, (Faucheaceae, Rhodymeniales) and its
L68@P2: comparison with Gloiocladia furcata, the type species of Gloiocladia, establishes that the two are closely related and belong
L69@P2: in the same genus. Accordingly, we propose a new combination, Gloiocladia repens (C. Agardh) Sa´nchez et Rodrı´guez-Prieto,
L70@P2: comb. nov., for the plant presently known as Fauchea repens (C. Agardh) Montagne et Bory in Montagne and designate a
L71@P2: lectotype. We provide an emended description of the genus Gloiocladia, and transfer all the species presently placed in Fauchea
L72@P2: to Gloiocladia.
L73@P2: Key words: 18S rDNA, Gloiocladia, Faucheaceae, Gloiocladia furcata, Gloiocladia repens, reproduction, Rhodymeniales,
L74@P2: Rhodophyta, taxonomy
L75@P2: Introduction
L76@P2: The red algal family Faucheaceae Strachan,
L77@P2: Saunders & Kraft is currently placed in the
L78@P2: Rhodymeniales (Rhodophyta) (Saunders et al.,
L79@P2: 1999). The genus Fauchea Montagne et Bory
L80@P2: (in Montagne, 1846: 64) includes 13 species,
L81@P2: widely distributed in tropical, subtropical and
L82@P2: temperate seas (Bornet, 1890; Weber-van Bosse,
L83@P2: 1928; Baardseth, 1941; Segawa, 1941; Yamada,
L84@P2: 1941; Børgesen, 1944; Taylor, 1945, 1960; Sparling,
L85@P2: 1957; Norris & Aken, 1985; Xia & Zhang, 1999),
L86@P2: of which F. repens (C. Agardh) Montagne et Bory
L87@P2: (in Montagne, 1846) is the type species. Generic
L88@P2: delineation of the genus Fauchea has been based
L89@P2: primarily on vegetative characters, presence of
L90@P2: a tela arachnoidea (network of erect persistent
L91@P2: filaments surrounding the procarp and the gonimoblast and developing from a basal nutritive
L92@P2: tissue) in the cystocarp, cystocarp position and
L93@P2: morphology, and by the arrangement and division
L94@P2: pattern of the tetrasporangia (Montagne, 1846;
L95@P2: Kylin, 1931; Irvine & Guiry, 1980; Norris, 1991).
L96@P2: The absence of detailed information on the
L97@P2: reproductive structures in the type species has
L98@P2: prevented the realization of a clear concept
L99@P2: of Fauchea and its relationship with Gloiocladia
L100@P2: J. Agardh (Irvine & Guiry, 1980; Norris, 1991).
L101@P2: Based on sequence analyses, Saunders et al. (1999)
L102@P2: included both genera in the family Faucheaceae,
L103@P2: together with Gloioderma fructiculosa (Harvey)
L104@P2: J. Agardh reported as Gloiocladia fruticulosa
L105@P2: (Harvey) R.E. Norris.
L106@P2: This paper presents a detailed description of F.
L107@P2: repens (as Gloiocladia repens (C. Agardh) Sa´nchez
L108@P2: et Rodrı´guez-Prieto comb. nov.) based on fresh
L109@P2: material collected during a study of the
L110@P2: Faucheaceae of the Iberian Peninsula (Sa´nchez,
L111@P2: 2005), along with a reinvestigation of original
L112@P2: material housed in the Agardh Herbarium at
L113@P2: Lund (LD) and material from other herbarium
L114@P2: collections. We report new morphological data
L115@P2: relating to vegetative and reproductive characters
L116@P2: of G. repens, and have selected a lectotype for this
L117@P2: species and reinvestigated the generic boundaries
L118@P2: between Fauchea and Gloiocladia. We also generated 18S rRNA gene sequences from specimens of
L119@P2: G. repens and G. furcata (C. Agardh) J. Agardh,
L120@P2: and compared them with sequences from other
L121@P2: Faucheaceae available in GenBank. Taxonomic
L122@P2: changes include: a re-assessment of the genus
L123@P2: Gloiocladia and transfer of Fauchea species to

L129@P3: Materials and methods
L130@P3: Morphological analyses
L131@P3: Herbarium acronyms follow Holmgren et al. (1990).
L132@P3: Specimens of G. repens were collected by SCUBA from
L133@P3: the Mediterranean coasts of Spain. Preserved specimens
L134@P3: from the original collection of C. Agardh housed at LD,
L135@P3: and others from HGI, MA, MGC, SANT, VAL and the
L136@P3: personal herbarium of J.J. Rodrı´guez y Femenı´as,
L137@P3: located in the Ateneu of Mao´, Minorca (Spain), were
L138@P3: also examined. Sections were made with a razor blade or
L139@P3: a freezing microtome, stained with acidified aqueous 1%
L140@P3: aniline-blue and mounted in 50% Karo(r) corn syrup
L141@P3: (Bestfoods, Englewood Cliffs, NJ, USA). Habit photographs were taken with a Pentax Program camera
L142@P3: (Pentax, Golden, CO, USA). Photomicrographs were
L143@P3: taken with a Spot Insight digital camera (Diagnostic
L144@P3: Instruments, Sterling Heights, MI, USA) attached to an
L145@P3: Axioskop 2 plus microscope (Zeiss, Berlin, Germany).
L146@P3: Voucher specimens and slides were deposited in the
L147@P3: Herbarium of the University of Girona, Spain (HGI).
L148@P3: Molecular analyses
L149@P3: Total genomic DNA was extracted from specimens of
L150@P3: G. repens from Columbretes Islands, Spain and of
L151@P3: G. furcata from Formigues Islands, Spain (desiccated in
L152@P3: silica gel or in 95% ethanol) following Hughey et al.
L153@P3: (2001), with a final cleaning step using the Gene Clean II
L154@P3: kit (Bio 101, Vista, CA, USA). The nuclear-encoded 18S
L155@P3: ribosomal RNA gene was amplified and sequenced
L156@P3: as described in Freshwater et al. (2005) using the
L157@P3: oligonucleotide primers published by Saunders &
L158@P3: Kraft (1994). Sequencing reactions were run on an
L159@P3: ABI 3100 Genetic Analyzer (DNA Analysis Core
L160@P3: Facility, Centre for Marine Science, UNCW) and the
L161@P3: reaction results edited and sequence contigs created
L162@P3: using Sequencher (Gene Codes Corp., Ann Arbor, MI,
L163@P3: USA). The resulting sequences have been deposited in
L164@P3: GenBank under the accession numbers DQ790749 and
L165@P3: DQ790750.
L166@P3: An 18S rDNA alignment including the two newly
L167@P3: generated sequences, seven Faucheaceae sequences
L168@P3: available in GenBank and a Lomentaria baileyana
L169@P3: (Harvey) Farlow sequence as the outgroup, was
L170@P3: assembled using MacClade (v. 4, Maddison &
L171@P3: Maddison, 2000). Lomentaria baileyana was chosen as
L172@P3: the outgroup for a number of reasons: (i) The
L173@P3: Lomentariaceae and Faucheaceae are sibling groups in
L174@P3: all published SSU analyses (Saunders et al., 1999;
L175@P3: Freshwater et al., 2005); (ii) the topological arrangement
L176@P3: of species within the Faucheaceae is basically the same
L177@P3: in all published SSU trees and our unpublished analyses
L178@P3: including 49 non-Faucheaceae Rhodymeniales whether
L179@P3: or not L. baileyana is the only outgroup; (iii) various
L180@P3: permutations of non-Faucheaceae species as outgroups
L181@P3: did not change the basic Faucheaceae topology; and
L182@P3: (iv) uncertain homology among sites was small when
L183@P3: L. baileyana was the only outgroup included in the
L184@P3: alignment. GenBank accession numbers for previously
L185@P3: published sequences are shown under the relevant
L186@P3: species names in Fig. 38. Alignment of these sequences
L187@P3: was straightforward and performed by eye. Sites from
L188@P3: the 50 and 30 ends of the alignment were removed due to
L189@P3: missing data, so that the final alignment included 1,676
L190@P3: sites. Two internal regions of the alignment totalling
L191@P3: 10 sites were excluded from analyses because the
L192@P3: homology of these sites was uncertain. Data set
L193@P3: characteristics and rate models were determined using
L194@P3: MacClade, Modeltest (Posada & Crandall, 1998) and
L195@P3: PAUP (Swofford, 2002). The aligned sequence data set
L196@P3: and model parameters are available from the second
L197@P3: author (DWF). Phylogenetic analyses using maximum
L198@P3: likelihood (ML), maximum parsimony, and distance
L199@P3: algorithms were performed using PAUP. ML searches
L200@P3: were performed using the Branch-and-Bound algorithm
L201@P3: and evolutionary model derived with Modeltest. ML
L202@P3: bootstrap analyses consisted of 463 replications of
L203@P3: Branch-and-Bound searches with the Modeltest model.
L204@P3: Distance analyses consisted of neighbor-joining (NJ)
L205@P3: tree building, using distances corrected with the same
L206@P3: model used in the ML analyses. NJ bootstrap analyses
L207@P3: were based on 5,000 replications of NJ tree building with
L208@P3: the same distance correction. Parsimony searches were
L209@P3: performed using the Branch-and-Bound algorithm and
L210@P3: parsimony bootstrap analyses consisted of 2,000 replications of Branch-and-Bound searches.

L274@P5: Habit and vegetative structure
L275@P5: Thalli decumbent, to 25 cm high and 18 cm
L276@P5: wide (Figs 1–6), primarily attached to the
L277@P5: substratum by a discoid holdfast (Fig. 5).
L278@P5: Plants sessile or occasionally stipitate (Fig. 6).
L279@P5: The stipe, when present, cartilaginous, cylindrical,
L280@P5: up to 13.0 mm long and 3.0 mm in diameter
L281@P5: (Fig. 6). Fronds much branched, complanate
L282@P5: and somewhat dichotomous, with dorsiventrally
L283@P5: compressed branches, 2–6 mm wide at the base and
L284@P5: 1–6 mm wide near the apex. Branch tips rounded
L285@P5: and furcate with smooth margins (Figs 1–6).
L286@P5: Thallus 560–1,500 mm thick at the base of
L287@P5: the plant and 500–1,000 mm at the apex. Texture
L288@P5: cartilaginous and slippery to the touch, and
L289@P5: the appearance shiny. Colour bright rose or
L290@P5: reddish, but some old individuals nearly white.
