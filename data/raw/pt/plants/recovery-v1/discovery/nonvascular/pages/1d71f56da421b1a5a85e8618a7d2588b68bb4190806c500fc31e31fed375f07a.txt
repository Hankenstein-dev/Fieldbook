L5: cite0†  L6: Erythroglossum laciniatum
L7: 
L8: Description: Thallus a short stipe, erect, consisting of one or more blades to 16 cms long and 15 or 20 cms wide, fan-shaped and divided into lobes with pointed apices. Veins macroscopic, also fine veins. Proliferations on the margin may curve down and reattach. Very variable.
L9: 
L10: Habitat: Epilithic in rock pools at extreme low littoral and sublittoral to 30 m.
L11: Distribution: Generally around the British Isles including the Shetland Islands and Channel Isles. Europe: Mediterranean, Portugal and France.
L12: 
L13: Similar Species: Haralidiophyllum bonnemaisonii does not have microscopic or macroscopic veins. Cryptopleura ramosa has veins only one cell wide just behind the apices and smooth blade margins.
