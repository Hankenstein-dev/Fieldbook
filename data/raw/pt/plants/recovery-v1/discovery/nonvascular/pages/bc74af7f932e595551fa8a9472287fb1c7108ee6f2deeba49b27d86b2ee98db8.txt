L28: 
L29: Description: Gelatinous, bushy, crimson fronds, to 250 mm long. Central axis polysiphonous, much branched, clothed with close-set whorls of short, repeatedly dichotomously branched monosiphonous ramui, 3-4 mm wide.
L30: Habitat: On rocks, low intertidal (very rarely) and subtidal, often on maërl. Widely distributed; a rare, sporadic summer annual.
