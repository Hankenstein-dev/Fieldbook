L136: Ankistrodesmus falcatus
L137: Ankistrodesmus is a cite59†genus of cite60†green algae in the family cite55†Selenastraceae .^{cite58†[2] } It is one of the most common types of cite61†phytoplankton in freshwater habitats around the world.^{cite62†[3] } The name Ankistrodesmus comes from the Greek roots ankistron, meaning "cross", and desmos, meaning "bond".^{cite63†[4] }
L138: ## Description
L139: 
L140: [cite64†edit ]
L141: 
L142: Ankistrodesmus forms colonies of cells, usually found as loose bundles or tufts of cells. Sometimes the cells may twist around each other in the colony. Cells are many times longer than wide, and are variously needle-shaped, sickle-shaped, or sigmoid in outline. They contain a single cite65†chloroplast ; the chloroplast usually contains a single cite66†pyrenoid , but may contain none or several pyrenoids.^{cite62†[3] }
L143: ### Life cycle
L144: 
L145: [cite67†edit ]
L146: 
L147: Reproduction occurs cite68†asexually ; sexual reproduction is unknown in Ankistrodesmus. It exclusively forms cite69†autospores , and does not appear to have a flagellated stage in its life cycle.^{cite57†[1] }
L148: 
L149: Some strains of Ankistrodesmus have been observed to undergo cite70†programmed cell death . The evolutionary role of this process is unknown.^{cite71†[5] }
L150: ### Identification
L151: 
L152: [cite72†edit ]
L153: Ankistrodesmus is chiefly distinguished from the similar genus cite73†Monoraphidium in its habit: Ankistrodesmus forms colonies, while Monoraphidium is found as single cells.^{cite74†[6] } Meanwhile, cite75†Selenastrum differs in having more strongly curved cells.^{cite57†[1] } The distinctions between the genera are artificial, since they do not correspond with monophyletic groupings;^{cite76†[7] } occasionally, Ankistrodesmus is found as single cells.^{cite74†[6] } Other similar genera include cite77†Keratococcus and cite78†Elakatothrix .^{cite62†[3] }
L154: Identification of species chiefly depends on details of the size and shape of cells.^{cite57†[1] } However, a cite79†molecular phylogenetic study has shown evidence of considerable cite80†cryptic diversity .^{cite81†[8] }
L155: ## Uses
L156: 
L157: [cite82†edit ]
L158: 
L159: Some strains of Ankistrodesmus are promising candidates for cite83†biodiesel production, or for producing cite84†fatty acids on a commercial scale.^{cite85†[9] }
