L29@P3: This guide contains information on the common marine plants
L30@P3: and animals that inhabit the shallow rocky reefs of the Santa
L31@P3: Barbara Channel. Its purpose is to aid student, staff and faculty
L32@P3: researchers of the Santa Barbara Coastal Ecosystem LongTerm Ecological Research program (SBC-LTER) in field
L33@P3: identification. It also provides those less familiar with the Santa
L34@P3: Barbara Channel with a glimpse of the diverse marine life that
L35@P3: can be found at SBC-LTER study sites. SBC-LTER is one of 26
L36@P3: sites established and funded by the National Science
L37@P3: Foundation to investigate long-term ecological phenomena.
L38@P3: More information on SBC-LTER’s mission and research can be
L39@P3: found at: http:/sbc.lternet.edu.
