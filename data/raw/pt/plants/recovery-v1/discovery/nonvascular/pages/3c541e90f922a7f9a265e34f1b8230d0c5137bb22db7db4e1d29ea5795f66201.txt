L291@P16: Marine Protected Areas are increasingly becoming an important
L292@P16: management tool to conserve the marine biodiversity and to protect
L293@P16: natural resources, globally.
L294@P16: Monitoring is an essential component of resource management by providing
L295@P16: science-based information to guide key management decisions like prioritizing
L296@P16: conservation strategies, proper allocation of resources, and ultimately, whether
L297@P16: or not these marine protected areas are meeting their intended objectives.
L298@P16: At Mediterranean level, the monitoring of habitats and species does not seem
L299@P16: to be a common practice in Marine Protected Areas and its surrounding areas.
L300@P16: Therefore, The Contracting Parties to the Barcelona Convention are currently
L301@P16: working to implement an Ecosystem Approach in the Region with the ultimate
L302@P16: objective of achieving the Good Ecological Status (GES) of the Mediterranean
L303@P16: Sea. They act for establishing a new monitoring program in line with the
L304@P16: Ecosystem Approach. This will enable for the first time a quantitative monitoring
L305@P16: of the status of the Mediterranean Sea on a regional basis, covering biodiversity
L306@P16: and non-indigenous species. Mediterranean countries are however in need of
L307@P16: diverse technical tools, including guides, to assist that process.
L308@P16: The present guide is intended to provide technical guidance to all actors at
L309@P16: different levels in developing site-based monitoring plans to complement
L310@P16: existing management plans or in developing new ones. It aims to set up a
L311@P16: methodological tool for environmental and ecological monitoring of Marine
L312@P16: Protected Areas and for littoral seabeds of surrounding areas, given their role
L313@P16: as buffer zones between Marine Protected Areas and more distant anthropized
L314@P16: zones.
L315@P16: Furthermore, it will enable promoting the creation of a network of underwater
L316@P16: sentinel stations to act as an environmental monitoring and warning system
L317@P16: helping to detect changes in the littoral ecosystem and the seascape it forms
L318@P16: with its fauna and flora.
L319@P16: Finally, this methodological tool aims to standardize monitoring across rocky
L320@P16: seabeds in Marine Protected Areas by providing guidance on monitoring
L321@P16: objectives, sampling design, indicators, and methodology. It was developed
L322@P16: for Marine Protected Areas managers, including conservation officers and
L323@P16: researchers who collect monitoring data but also for an ample collection of
