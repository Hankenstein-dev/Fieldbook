L26: Description: Cartilaginous but flaccid, dark brown-red fronds becoming yellow-brown apically, to 200 mm long. Stipe gradually beoming compressed, to 100 mm long, from sparingly branched rhizoidal holdfast. Blade lanceolate, to 10 mm wide, irregularly pinnate, clothed with tendril-like coiled or hooked processes, to 100 mm or more long in summer.
L27: Habitat: On rocks and epyphytic, especially on cite18†Corallina officinalis in large open pools, midtidal to shallow subtidal (5 m), common on southern and western shores, rare in N Ireland and W Scotland.
L28: Similar species: cite19†Calliblepharis ciliata is broader (to 50 mm), its marginal proliferations are shorter and less entangled, and it is mainly subtidal.
L29: Notes: Genus name from Greek, kallos (beauty) & blepharis (eyelash).
