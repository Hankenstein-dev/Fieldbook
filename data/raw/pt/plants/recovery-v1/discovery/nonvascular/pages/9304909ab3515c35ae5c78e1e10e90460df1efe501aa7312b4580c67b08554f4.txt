L124: Scytosiphon lomentaria
L125: (cite52†Lyngbye ) cite53†J. Agardh L126: Scytosiphon lomentaria is a cite54†littoral brown cite55†seaweed with an irregularly lobed many filamentous form. It is a member of the cite47†Phaeophyceae in the order cite48†Ectocarpales and grows attached to shells and stones in rock-pools and in near-shore waters. The attachment to the substrate is by a small disc shaped cite56†holdfast .
L127: ## Description
L128: 
L129: [cite57†edit ]
L130: 
L131: Scytosiphon lomentaria has cylindrical, shiny, olive brown, unbranched fronds up to 400 mm long. They have short stalks and a large number may arise from a single holdfast. They widen to 3-10mm and narrow again near the tip. They are hollow and often have irregular constrictions.^{cite58†[1] }
L132: ## Distribution and habitat
L133: 
L134: [cite59†edit ]
L135: 
L136: This species is cosmopolitan in distribution being found in temperate waters around the world. The cite60†type location is cite61†Denmark .^{cite62†[2] } It occurs in the cite63†littoral zone and favours wave-exposed shores and cite64†rock pools .^{cite65†[3] } Small plants are often found growing on cite66†limpets and pebbles.^{cite58†[1] }
