L3: ## Pterothamnion crispum (Ducluzeau) Nägeli
L4: 
L5: * * *
L6: 
L7: Description: Thallus small , monosiphonous, erect in tufts to 1.5 cm long, branching pseudodichotomously in whorls of 4 on the axis.
L8: 
L9: Habitat: Epiphytic and epizoic from low littoral to 10m sublittoral.
L10: 
L11: Distribution: British Isles on southwestern coasts: Ireland, Wales, England, Isle of Man and the Channel Islands. Europe: Mediterranean and Spain to the Netherlands. Further afield: Canary Islands.
L12: 
L13: Similar Species: Pterothamnion plumula.
