L165: Diagnosis:
L166:   * coenobia spherical
L167:   * 4–32 cells
L168:   * coenobia up to 36 µm diameter
L169:   * cells ovoid or almost spherical
L170:   * cell wall smooth or slightly wrinkled

L172:   * cells connected to lateral neighboring cells
L173:   * connecting surfaces to neighboring cells flattened
L174:   * one parietal chloroplast with one pyrenoid
L175:   * planktonic lifestyle
L176: 
L177: cite157†Image: Coelastrum-astroideum Coelastrum astroideum
L178: I found many coenobia of Coelastrum astroideum in the plankton of the cite135†pond of the waste disposal company Constance . The coenobia had a diameter of 20–30 µm and thus corresponded to the data given by Komarek & Trebon (1983). I found most coenobia in autumn.
L179: 
L180: The cells in the coenobia were ovoid in shape as drawn by Komarek (s. drawing above). The tapered ends of the cells always points outward. The cell walls in my population were all slightly wrinkled (s. fig. 1 b).
L181: Coelastrum astroideum differs from the similar species Coelastrum pseudomicroporum by the type of connection between the cells. In Coelastrum astroideum, there are only connections to the lateral neighboring cells, whereas in Coelastrum pseudomicroporum there are also connections at the basal surfaces of the cells to the inner cells.
