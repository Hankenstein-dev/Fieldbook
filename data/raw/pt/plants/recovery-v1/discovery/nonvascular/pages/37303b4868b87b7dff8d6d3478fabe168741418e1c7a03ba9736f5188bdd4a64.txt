L37: From Flora of North America:
L38: Plants loosely prostrate in thin patches, pale yellow-green, in wet places frequently suberect and sometimes deep green. Thallus strongly convex dorsally, 15–60 × 1.2–2.2 mm, margins conspicuously deflexed to revolute; dichotomous and ventral branches common. Costa 60–120 µm wide, 2 large cells (50+ µm wide) wide dorsally, 2(–4) cells wide ventrally; ventral surface bearing hairs 170–230 µm long, incurved or hamate.
L39: Wings (8–)12–20 cells wide, naked dorsally, with some setae occurring ventrally; hairs on lamina margins mostly paired, incurved or hamate, to 170–230 µm. Laminal cells often subisodiametric, 40–65 × 30–45 µm medially, smaller laterally. Gemmae produced occasionally along thallus margins some distance from apex, 135–225 × 400–650 µm at time of abscission. Sexual condition dioicous; mostly sterile. Androecial branches naked, each with 4–8 antheridia in 2 rows.
L40: Gynoecial branches rotund-ovate, with no costa, with long, mostly solitary, straight hairs on margins and sometimes few on dorsal surface. Sporophytes not seen.
