L27: cite18†Fact Sheet (Download PDF) L28: Description: Soft, cylindrical, crimson to brownish red fronds, to 200 mm (normally much shorter). Lighter when wet (above), darker when dry (below). Much branched, axes constricted into bead-like portions, ramuli at nodes, often whorled. Base of creeping rhizoidal filaments.
L29: Habitat: On rocks and stones, in pools, lower intertidal and subtidal, generally distributed, sometimes almost zone-forming, frequent.
L30: Distribution: Widely distributed in Britain and Ireland.
L31: Similar species: None in Britain and Ireland, although Lomentaria hakodatensis, introduced into Brittany from Japan, is similar. cite19†Lomentaria clavellosa and cite20†Lomentaria orcadensis are both distinctly flattened and mostly branched in one plane.
