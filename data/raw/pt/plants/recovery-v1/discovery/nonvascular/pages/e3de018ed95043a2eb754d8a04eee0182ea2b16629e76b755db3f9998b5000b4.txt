L23:   * cite16†Books L24:   * cite17†Videos L25: # Gelidium pulchellum (Turner) J.V. Lamouroux
L26: Description: Cartilaginous, regularly or irregularly bipinnate, dark red-brown fronds, 50-100 mm high, arising from a creeping base. Main axes narrow, cylindrical, somewhat flattened above. Ultimate branches short, pointed at first, later ± spathulate, particularly when reproductive. Appearance variable with habitat and time of year.
L27: Habitat: Abundant in and around tidal pools in mid- and lower intertidal in high-light situations. Generally epiphytic on cite18†Corallina officinalis and growing on limpets. Widely distributed, common.
L28: Similar species: cite19†Gelidium spinosum is more flattened and more regularly pinnately branched and occurs in lower-shore habitats and in the subtidal. cite20†Pterocladiella capillacea is much less common, more regular and pyramidal in outline.
L29: Key characteristics: Pinnate branching, mid- and lower-intertidal low-light habitats, and slighly flattened main axis.
